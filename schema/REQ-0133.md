## 1. Normative Statement

The verification engine SHALL compute and verify Merkle root digest $``H_{\text{root}} \in \{0, 1\}^{256}``$ over 7-tier traceability graph `TraceGraph_Alpha`, emitting manifest `HashAttestation_0` (`MerkleTree_1`) to guarantee bitwise determinism.

Attestation MUST enforce:
1. **Serialization**: $``V_{\text{trace}}``$ partitions into tiers $``T_1 \dots T_7``$. Nodes $u$ serialize to canonical payload $\text{Data}(u)$ ($``\tau_V``$, $``\Delta_V``$, FQN, digest). Unpartitioned nodes emit `E0400`.
2. **Child Sorting**: Children sort into canonical order $``\text{Children}_{\text{canon}}(u)``$ by tier, relation, FQN, and UUIDv5. Disordered iterations emit `E0400`.
3. **Bottom-Up Hashing**: Post-order hashing uses hash $H$ (BLAKE3/SHA-256). Leaves evaluate $``H(w) = H(\text{TAG}_{\text{leaf}} \mathbin{\Vert} \text{Data}(w))``$; internal nodes evaluate $``H(u) = H(\text{TAG}_{\text{node}} \mathbin{\Vert} \text{Data}(u) \mathbin{\Vert} k \mathbin{\Vert} H(v_1) \dots H(v_k))``$, memoizing diamond DAGs.
4. **Root Synthesis**: Root $``H_{\text{root}}``$ hashes graph metrics and sorted roots $``r_1 \prec \dots \prec r_m``$. Sub-hashes $``H(T_i)``$ isolate deltas. Mismatches emit `E0400`.
5. **Inclusion Proofs**: Proofs $\Pi(u)$ verify membership in $``O(\text{depth} \cdot d_{\max})``$ time. Forged proofs emit `E0409`.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Merkle DAG attestation, canonical ordering, and diagnostic triggers satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
G_{\text{trace}}=\langle V_{\text{trace}},E_{\text{trace}},\mathcal{T}_{\text{tiers}},\tau_V,\tau_E,\Delta_V,\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\rangle,\quad V_{\text{trace}}=\biguplus_{i=1}^7 T_i \\
\text{Data}(u)=\text{Enc}(\tau_V(u),\Delta_V(u),\text{FQN}(u),\text{Digest}_{\text{content}}(u),\text{Span}(u)) \\
\text{Children}_{\text{canon}}(u)=\langle v_1,\dots,v_k\rangle\implies\forall j<k,\; v_j\prec_{\text{canon}} v_{j+1} \\
H(u)=\begin{cases} H(\text{TAG}_{\text{leaf}}\mathbin{\Vert}\text{Data}(u)), & k=0 \\ H(\text{TAG}_{\text{node}}\mathbin{\Vert}\text{Data}(u)\mathbin{\Vert} k\mathbin{\Vert} H(v_1)\dots H(v_k)), & k\ge 1 \end{cases} \\
H_{\text{root}}=H(\text{TAG}_{\text{graph}}\mathbin{\Vert}|V_{\text{trace}}|\mathbin{\Vert}|E_{\text{trace}}|\mathbin{\Vert} m\mathbin{\Vert} H(r_1)\dots H(r_m)),\quad H(T_i)=H(\text{TAG}_{\text{tier}}\mathbin{\Vert} i\mathbin{\Vert}|T_i|\mathbin{\Vert} H(u_{i,1})\dots H(u_{i,n_i})) \\
\text{VerifyProof}(\Pi(u),H(u),H_{\text{root}})\equiv(\text{RecomputeRoot}(\Pi(u),H(u))=H_{\text{root}}) \\
(H_{\text{computed}}\ne H_{\text{manifest}}\lor\text{Children}(u)\ne\text{Children}_{\text{canon}}(u))\implies\text{Diagnostic}(u,\text{E0400})\land u\gets\bot_{\text{hash}} \\
(\text{VerifyProof}(\Pi(u),\dots)=\text{false}\lor v\notin V_{\text{trace}})\implies\text{Diagnostic}(u,\text{E0409}) \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199}),\quad \Delta_u=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(u))
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(|V| \cdot H)$ Merkle root computation ($``|V| = |V_{\text{trace}}|``$); sorting takes $``O(|E| \log d_{\max})``$; proofs take $``O(\text{depth} \cdot d_{\max})``$. Work-stealing: $``O((|V| \log |V| + |E| \log d_{\max}) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$. Arena: $O(|V| + |E|)$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** N/A (Deterministic P). Memoization avoids diamond DAG path explosion without search (avoiding NP-1 through NP-8).

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical Merkle DAG Root
- **Given:** Graph `TraceGraph_Alpha` (`Req_Mission_0` to `Witness_0`).
- **When:** Pass `MerkleTree_1` evaluates the graph.
- **Then:** Emits root $``H_{\text{root}}``$ and manifest `HashAttestation_0`.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Mutation Avalanche Detection
- **Given:** `Package_0` mutating a byte in `Req_Mission_0` or edge target.
- **When:** Verification checks recomputed Merkle root.
- **Then:** Hash divergence alters $``H_{\text{root}}``$, rejecting compilation.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` (MerkleAttestationMismatch).

### AC-03: Inclusion Proof Validation
- **Given:** Graph `TraceGraph_Alpha` and witness `Witness_0`.
- **When:** Checking proof $``\Pi(\text{Witness\_0})``$ against forged sibling hashes.
- **Then:** Valid proof verifies in $O(1)$; forged proof emits `E0409`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0409` (InvalidMerkleInclusionProof).

### AC-04: Non-Canonical Ordering Recovery
- **Given:** Declaration violating $``\prec_{\text{canon}}``$ before `;`, followed by a valid node.
- **When:** Sorting triggers delimiter recovery.
- **Then:** Emits `E0400`, binds $``\bot_{\text{hash}}``$, recovers on `;`, halting at limit with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400`, `E0199`.
