## 1. Normative Statement

The dynamics solver SHALL implement an axiomatic solver for Allen's 13 qualitative interval relations over occurrences, actions, and states.

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Well-Formedness**: Interval `Interval_X` $``= [x_s, x_e]``$ on $\mathbb{R}$ SHALL satisfy $``x_s \lt x_e``$. Inverted ($``x_s \gt x_e``$) or degenerate ($``x_s = x_e``$) bounds emit `E0400`.
2. **JEPD Relations**: Classifies pairs into $``\mathcal{B}_{13}``$ in $O(1)$; conflicts with assertions emit `E0400`.
3. **Bitmask Algebra**: Encodes relations as $``\mathbf{m}_R \in [0, 8191]``$; evaluates boolean algebra and converse ($R^\smile$). Empty intersection emits `E0401`.
4. **Symmetry**: Mandates identity $R(X, X) \equiv \{ \mathbf{eq} \}$ (breaches emit `E0400`) and converse symmetry $R(Y, X) \equiv (R(X, Y))^\smile$ (contradictions emit `E0401`).
5. **Difference Lowering**: Lowers relations into difference inequalities $u - v \le c$ ($``u, v \in \{ x_s, x_e, y_s, y_e \}``$) with guard $``\epsilon_{\text{dur}} \gt 0``$.

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(1)$ per relation check, $O(N^2)$ for pairwise matrix over $N$ intervals, $O(N + M)$ for $M$ constraints over $N$ intervals.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Solves ORD-Horn fragment (NP-1 anchor N/A). Work-stealing scales as $``O((N + M) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Atomic Relation Classification
- **Given:** `Interval_Alpha` $= [10.0, 20.0]$, `Interval_Beta` $= [20.0, 30.0]$.
- **When:** Evaluating endpoint ordering ($``x_e = y_s``$).
- **Then:** Classifies atomic `meets` ($\mathbf{m}$), lowering difference inequalities.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Bitmask Algebraic Inversion and Conjunction
- **Given:** Relations $``R_1 = \{ \mathbf{b}, \mathbf{m} \}``$, $``R_2 = \{ \mathbf{m}, \mathbf{o} \}``$ on `(Interval_X, Interval_Y)`.
- **When:** Evaluating bitmask operations.
- **Then:** Computes $``R_1 \cap R_2 = \{ \mathbf{m} \}``$ (`0x0004`), $``(R_1)^\smile = \{ \mathbf{bi}, \mathbf{mi} \}``$ (`0x000A`).
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Inverted Interval and Relation Contradiction
- **Given:** `Interval_X` with $``x_s = 25.0 \gt x_e = 15.0``$, and assertion `equals` on $[0, 5]$ and $[10, 15]$.
- **When:** Validating interval constraints.
- **Then:** Inverted endpoints and relation conflict ($[0,5] \mathbf{b} [10,15] \ne \mathbf{eq}$) emit `E0400`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400`.

### AC-04: Disjoint Relation Conflict and Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Conflicting assertions `Interval_A meets Interval_B` and `Interval_B before Interval_A`.
- **When:** Checking intersection and error recovery.
- **Then:** Detects $R \cap (R')^\smile = \emptyset$, emits `E0401`, binds $``\bot_{\text{rel}}``$, recovers on `;`, halting at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0401`, `E0199`.
