## 1. Normative Statement

## 2. Formal Invariant

For virtual registers $V$ and dependency DAG $``\mathcal{D}_{\text{bb}}=(I,E_{\text{dep}})``$, invariants satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\text{degree}(v)\lt K&\implies\text{Push}(v,\mathcal{S}_{\text{alloc}})\land\mathcal{G}_{\text{interf}}\gets\mathcal{G}_{\text{interf}}\setminus\{v\} \\
\text{degree}(v)\ge K&\implies v_{\text{spill}}=\arg\min_{v}\frac{\text{spill\_cost}(v)}{\text{degree}(v)}\land\text{Push}(v_{\text{spill}},\mathcal{S}_{\text{alloc}}) \\
c(v)&=\min\left(\{1,\dots,K\}\setminus\{c(u)\mid (u,v)\in E\}\right) \\
\text{Priority}(u)&=\text{latency}(u)+\max_{v\in\text{succ}(u)}\left(\text{Priority}(v)+\lambda(u,v)\right) \\
\text{HasCycle}(\mathcal{D}_{\text{bb}})&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0300})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land\mathcal{D}_{\text{bb}}\gets\bot_{\text{dag}} \\
(u,v)\in E\land c(u)=c(v)&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0506})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land\text{Alloc}\gets\bot_{\text{alloc}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}| \ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}
\end{aligned}
```

| Symbol / Term | Domain / Formal Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| $``V, E, \mathcal{G}_{\text{interf}}, K, c(v)``$ | Virtual registers, interference graph, budget, and colors |
| $``I, E_{\text{dep}}, \mathcal{D}_{\text{bb}}, \lambda, \text{Priority}(u)``$ | Instructions, dependency DAG, latency, and priorities |
| $``\mathcal{S}_{\text{alloc}}, \bot_{\text{alloc}}, \bot_{\text{dag}}``$ | Allocation stack and failure sentinels |
| $``\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}, C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ | Diagnostic sink and error capacity bound |

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** NP-Hard (NP-7 Register Allocation and DAG Scheduling).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** Kempe allocation executes in $O(|V|^2)$ and critical-path list scheduling executes in $``O(|I| + |E_{\text{dep}}|)``$. Liveness analysis takes $O(|\mathcal{B}| \cdot |V|)$. Work-stealing scales as $``O(|V|^2 / N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$. Arena footprint is $``O(|V| + |E| + |I| + |E_{\text{dep}}|)``$.
- **Tractable Subclass and Frontier:** NP-7 (Chaitin Graph K-Coloring and Latency-Constrained DAG Instruction Scheduling). Kempe simplification with optimistic spilling and single-pass list traversal guarantee polynomial tractability.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical Allocation and Scheduling
- **Given:** Block `Block_0` in `Package_0` with budget $K = 16$.
- **When:** Lowering via `RegisterAllocator_Alpha` and `Scheduler_1`.
- **Then:** Assigns colors ($c(u) \ne c(v)$ for $(u, v) \in E$) with zero stalls.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Register Pressure and Optimistic Spilling
- **Given:** Graph `InterferenceGraph_0` with degree exceeding budget $K$.
- **When:** Lowering via Kempe simplification with spilling.
- **Then:** Selects candidate $``v_{\text{spill}}``$ minimizing cost ratio and assigns stack slots.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Dependency Cycle Rejection
- **Given:** Block `Block_0` with cyclic instruction dependencies ($u \to v \to u$).
- **When:** Lowering DAG `DAG_Alpha`.
- **Then:** Emits `E0300`, binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{dag}}``$, and fails closed.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300`.

### AC-04: Allocation Conflict and Recovery
- **Given:** Conflict in `Block_0`, delimiter `;`, and `Block_1`.
- **When:** Lowering across block boundaries.
- **Then:** Emits `E0506`, recovers at `;`, parses `Block_1`, halting at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0506`, `E0199`.
