## 1. Normative Statement

The network solver SHALL synthesize generalized Kirchhoff Potential Law (KPL) equations across conservative loops.

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Cycle Basis**: Spanning forest $\mathcal{T}$ on $``\mathcal{G}_{\text{flow}}``$ ($|\mathcal{K}|$ components) leaves chords $``e_k \in \mathcal{E}_{\text{chord}}``$ ($``N_C = |\mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}}| - |\mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}| + |\mathcal{K}|``$), inducing fundamental cycles $``\mathcal{L}_k``$.
2. **Cycle Matrix**: Assembles $``\mathbf{B} = [\mathbf{B}_T \mid \mathbf{I}_{N_C}]``$ ($``B_{k, e} \in \{+1, -1, 0\}``$), enforcing $\mathbf{B} \mathbf{\Delta e}(t) = \mathbf{0}$. Cycles satisfy $\oint \Delta e = 0$.
3. **Datum Potential**: Datum $``J_{\text{ref}, m}``$ sets $``e(J_{\text{ref}, m}) = 0``$; $``\mathbf{\Delta e} = \mathbf{A}_{\text{red}}^T \mathbf{e}_{\text{node}}``$ ensures $\mathbf{B} \mathbf{\Delta e} = \mathbf{0}$ since $\mathbf{B} \mathbf{A}^T = \mathbf{0}$.
4. **Homogeneity**: Loop branches require $``\mathbf{d}_{\text{across}}(e_a) \equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{across}}(e_b)``$ in $\mathbb{Q}^7$; mismatches emit `E0300`.
5. **Static Balance**: Imbalance $``\sum B_{k, e} \Delta e_e \ne 0``$ emits `E0301`.

\mathbf{B}=[\mathbf{B}_T \mid \mathbf{I}_{N_C}]\in\{-1,0,+1\}^{N_C\times |\mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}}|},\quad \mathbf{B}\mathbf{\Delta e}(t)=\mathbf{0},\quad \mathbf{B}\mathbf{A}^T=\mathbf{0} \\
\forall\mathcal{L}_k\in\mathcal{C},\;\sum_{e\in\text{Edges}(\mathcal{L}_k)}B_{k,e}\Delta e_e(t)=0,\quad \mathbf{\Delta e}=\mathbf{A}_{\text{red}}^T\mathbf{e}_{\text{node}}\implies\mathbf{B}\mathbf{\Delta e}=\mathbf{0} \\

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(V + E)$ forest, $``O(N_C \cdot V)``$ cycle basis and $\mathbf{B}$ assembly, $O(E)$ balance.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Basis extraction avoids exponential cycle search (NP-4). Work-stealing scales as $``O((V + N_C \cdot V) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Cycle Basis Extraction & KPL Synthesis
- **Given:** Network `Network_Alpha` with 5 junctions (`Junction_0`--`Junction_4`) and 7 branches (`FlowBranch_0`--`FlowBranch_6`).
- **When:** Extracting spanning forest and cycle constraints.
- **Then:** Partitions 3 chords ($``N_C = 3``$), builds $\mathbf{B}$, emitting $``\sum B_{k, e} \Delta e_e = 0``$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Potential Field & Arbitrary Loop Closure
- **Given:** Network `Network_Alpha` ($``e(\text{Junction\_0}) = 0``$), $``\mathbf{\Delta e} = \mathbf{A}_{\text{red}}^T \mathbf{e}_{\text{node}}``$, and cycle $``\mathcal{L}_{\text{comb}} = \mathcal{L}_1 \oplus \mathcal{L}_2``$.
- **When:** Evaluating potential closure on $``\mathcal{L}_{\text{comb}}``$.
- **Then:** Yields $\mathbf{B} \mathbf{\Delta e} = \mathbf{0}$ and $\oint \Delta e = 0$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Incommensurable Dimensional Mismatch Rejection
- **Given:** Cycle `Loop_Alpha` with `FlowBranch_1` ($[M]^1 [L]^2 [T]^{-3} [I]^{-1}$) and `FlowBranch_2` ($[M]^1 [L]^{-1} [T]^{-2}$).
- **When:** Dimensional checking executes on loop branches.
- **Then:** Rejection emits `E0300`, binding sentinel $``\bot_{\text{dim}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300` (DimensionalMismatch).

### AC-04: Static Imbalance & Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Loop `Loop_Beta` with static imbalance $\sum \Delta e = 1.0 \ne 0$ before `;` and valid `Loop_Gamma`.
- **When:** Balance checking and delimiter recovery execute.
- **Then:** Emits `E0301`, resynchronizes on `;`, parses `Loop_Gamma`, emitting `E0199` at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0301`, `E0199`.
