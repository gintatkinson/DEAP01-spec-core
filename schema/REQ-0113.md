## 1. Normative Statement

The metrology engine SHALL perform static boundary value analysis (BVA), verifying tolerance envelopes satisfy safety bounds.

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Tolerance Hyperbox**: Parameters $``x_i``$ declare nominal $``x_{i,0}``$ and tolerances $``\delta_i^-,\delta_i^+ \ge 0``$, defining interval $``\mathcal{I}(x_i) = [x_{i,0}-\delta_i^-, x_{i,0}+\delta_i^+]``$ and box $``\mathcal{B} = \prod \mathcal{I}(x_i)``$. Inverted bounds emit `E0301`.
2. **Response Extrema**: For $y = f(\mathbf{x})$, computes range $``\mathcal{R}_y = [y_{\min}, y_{\max}]``$. Affine forms evaluate in $O(k)$; general expressions compute enclosure $``[y_{\min}^{\text{IA}}, y_{\max}^{\text{IA}}]``$ via interval arithmetic.
3. **Safety Containment**: Verifies $``[y_{\min}, y_{\max}] \subseteq [Y_{\text{lower\_limit}}, Y_{\text{upper\_limit}}]``$. Breaches emit `E0301`; passes record margins $``\mu_{\text{lower}}, \mu_{\text{upper}} \ge 0``$.
4. **Homogeneity**: Requires $``\mathbf{d}(x_i) \equiv \mathbf{d}(\delta_i)``$ and $``\mathbf{d}(y) \equiv \mathbf{d}(f(\mathbf{x})) \equiv \mathbf{d}(Y_{\text{limit}})``$ in $\mathbb{Q}^7$; mismatches emit `E0300`. Units scale via $``c_u \in \mathbb{Q}^+``$.
5. **Symbolic Synthesis**: Lowers verified bounds into IR assertion nodes and SMT-LIB2 clauses.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Parametric tolerance hyperbox, response extrema, safety containment, and diagnostic trigger predicates:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{I}(x_i)=[x_{i,0}-\delta_i^-,x_{i,0}+\delta_i^+],\quad \mathcal{B}=\prod_{i=1}^k\mathcal{I}(x_i)\subset\mathbb{R}^k,\quad \mathcal{R}_y=[y_{\min},y_{\max}]\subseteq [y_{\min}^{\text{IA}},y_{\max}^{\text{IA}}] \\
[y_{\min},y_{\max}]\subseteq [Y_{\text{lower\_limit}},Y_{\text{upper\_limit}}]\iff (y_{\min}\ge Y_{\text{lower\_limit}}\land y_{\max}\le Y_{\text{upper\_limit}}) \\
\mu_{\text{lower}}=y_{\min}-Y_{\text{lower\_limit}}\ge 0,\quad \mu_{\text{upper}}=Y_{\text{upper\_limit}}-y_{\max}\ge 0 \\
\mathbf{d}(x_i)\equiv\mathbf{d}(\delta_i^-)\equiv\mathbf{d}(\delta_i^+),\quad \mathbf{d}(y)\equiv\mathbf{d}(f(\mathbf{x}))\equiv\mathbf{d}(Y_{\text{lower\_limit}})\equiv\mathbf{d}(Y_{\text{upper\_limit}}) \\
(x_{i,\min}\gt x_{i,\max}\lor\delta_i\lt 0)\implies\text{Diagnostic}(T,\text{E0301})\land T\gets\bot_{\text{tol}} \\
(\mathbf{d}(x_i)\not\equiv\mathbf{d}(\delta_i)\lor\mathbf{d}(f)\not\equiv\mathbf{d}(Y_{\text{limit}}))\implies\text{Diagnostic}(T,\text{E0300})\land\mathbf{d}(T)\gets\bot_{\text{dim}} \\
(y_{\min}\lt Y_{\text{lower\_limit}}\lor y_{\max}\gt Y_{\text{upper\_limit}})\implies\text{Diagnostic}(T,\text{E0301}),\quad \Delta_T=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(T))
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(k)$ for linear bounding; $O(K)$ for interval arithmetic over $K$-node ASTs; aggregate is $``O(N_{\text{tol}} \cdot (K + k))``$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Avoids exponential search over NP-3 frontier. Work-stealing scales as $``O((N_{\text{tol}} \cdot (K + k)) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Linear Affine Tolerance Containment
- **Given:** Linear $``y = 2 x_1 + 3 x_2``$ with $``x_1 \in [1.0, 3.0]``$, $``x_2 \in [2.0, 4.0]``$, target $[5.0, 20.0]$.
- **When:** Lowering evaluates extrema.
- **Then:** Computes $``[y_{\min}, y_{\max}] = [8.0, 18.0] \subseteq [5.0, 20.0]``$ with $``\mu_{\text{lower}} = 3.0``$, $``\mu_{\text{upper}} = 2.0``$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Unit Conversion and Boundary Clearance
- **Given:** Nominal $1.0\text{ m}$ with tolerance $50.0\text{ mm}$ and bound $[0.95, 1.05]\text{ m}$.
- **When:** Normalizing units and boundaries.
- **Then:** Scales via $``c_u = 1/1000 \in \mathbb{Q}^+``$ to $[0.95, 1.05]\text{ m}$ with zero margin violation.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Safety Envelope Breach Rejection
- **Given:** Response $[2.0, 12.0]$ against envelope $[0.0, 10.0]$.
- **When:** Containment checking executes.
- **Then:** Upper breach ($12.0 \gt 10.0$) emits `E0301`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0301` (ToleranceEnvelopeViolation).

### AC-04: Dimension Mismatch and Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Mismatched $[L]^1$ vs $[T]^1$ before `;`, followed by valid `Tolerance_Beta`.
- **When:** Type checking and recovery execute.
- **Then:** Emits `E0300`, binds $``\bot_{\text{dim}}``$, recovers on `;`, parses `Tolerance_Beta`, halting at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300`, `E0199`.
