## 1. Normative Statement

The network solver SHALL verify power product balance across closed conservative boundaries $\Omega$.

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Port Power**: Ports $``P_k \in \mathcal{P}_{\text{ext}}``$ bind $``(e_k, f_k)``$ with $``P_k(t) = e_k(t) f_k(t)``$ and $``c_k \in \mathbb{Q}^+``$. Mismatches against $``\mathbf{d}_{\text{Power}}``$ emit `E0300`. External power satisfies $``P_{\text{ext}}(t) = \sum \sigma_k c_k P_k(t) = P_{\text{in}}(t) - P_{\text{out}}(t)``$.
2. **Storage Rate**: States $``S_s \in \mathcal{S}_{\text{storage}}``$ require $``\mathbf{d}(E_{\text{internal}}) \equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{Energy}}``$ and $``\mathbf{d}(\frac{dE}{dt}) \equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{Power}}``$; mismatches emit `E0300`.
3. **Dissipation Non-Negativity**: Dissipation satisfies $``P_{\text{dissipated}}(t) \ge 0``$; negative dissipation ($``P_{\text{dissipated}} \lt 0``$) without active sources emits `E0301`.
4. **Power Balance**: Assemblies enforce $``P_{\text{in}}(t) = P_{\text{out}}(t) + \frac{d E_{\text{internal}}}{dt}(t) + P_{\text{dissipated}}(t)``$. Static imbalance emits `E0301`.
5. **Passivity Invariant**: Passive models require $``\int_0^T P_{\text{ext}}(t) \, dt \ge -E_{\text{initial}}``$ for all $T \ge 0$; violations emit `E0301`.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Conjugate port power, internal storage, dissipation non-negativity, power balance, passivity, and diagnostic predicates:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{d}_{\text{Power}}=(1,2,-3,0,0,0,0)\in\mathbb{Q}^7,\quad \mathbf{d}_{\text{Energy}}=(1,2,-2,0,0,0,0)\in\mathbb{Q}^7 \\
P_k(t)=e_k(t)f_k(t),\quad P_{\text{ext}}(t)=\sum_{k=1}^m \sigma_k c_k P_k(t)=P_{\text{in}}(t)-P_{\text{out}}(t) \\
P_{\text{in}}(t)=P_{\text{out}}(t)+\frac{d E_{\text{internal}}}{dt}(t)+P_{\text{dissipated}}(t),\quad P_{\text{dissipated}}(t)\ge 0 \\
\text{is\_passive}(\Omega)\implies\left(\forall T\ge 0,\;\int_0^T P_{\text{ext}}(t)\,dt\ge -E_{\text{initial}}\right)\land\left(\forall t\ge 0,\;E_{\text{internal}}(t)\ge 0\right) \\
\mathbf{d}(e_k)+\mathbf{d}(f_k)\not\equiv\mathbf{d}_{\text{Power}}\lor\mathbf{d}(E_s)\not\equiv\mathbf{d}_{\text{Energy}}\implies\text{Diagnostic}(n,\text{E0300})\land n\gets\bot_{\text{dim}} \\
(P_{\text{dissipated}}\lt 0)\lor\left|P_{\text{in}}-P_{\text{out}}-\frac{dE}{dt}-P_{\text{dissipated}}\right|\gt 0\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\Omega,\text{E0301}) \\
\forall n\in\mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}},\quad \Delta_n=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(n))
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(m + S + R)$ per assembly boundary ($m$ ports, $S$ storage, $R$ dissipation); $O(N)$ lowering overall.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Linear formulation avoids NP-hard energy optimization (NP-4 fragment). Work-stealing scales as $``O(N / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Multi-Port Power Balance
- **Given:** Assembly `Assembly_Alpha` with inputs `Port_1` ($60\text{ W}$), `Port_2` ($40\text{ W}$) and output `Port_3` ($-100\text{ W}$).
- **When:** Lowering power balance equations.
- **Then:** Evaluates $``P_{\text{in}} = P_{\text{out}} = 100\text{ W}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Dynamic Storage and Scaling
- **Given:** Assembly `Assembly_Beta` with input `Port_1` ($2\text{ kW}$, $``c_1=1000``$), output `Port_2` ($1500\text{ W}$), dissipation $200\text{ W}$, storage rate $300\text{ W}$.
- **When:** Lowering dynamic balance constraints.
- **Then:** Evaluates $(1000 \cdot 2) - 1500 = 300 + 200$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Negative Dissipation Rejection
- **Given:** Assembly `Assembly_Gamma` with passive dissipation $``P_{\text{dissipated}} = -50\text{ W}``$.
- **When:** Static dissipation validation executes.
- **Then:** Rejects assembly, binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{power}}``$, emitting `E0301`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0301` (KirchhoffConservationViolation).

### AC-04: Dimensional Mismatch and Recovery
- **Given:** Assembly `Assembly_Delta` with mismatched port dimensions before delimiter `;` and valid `Assembly_Epsilon`.
- **When:** Dimension checking and delimiter recovery execute.
- **Then:** Emits `E0300`, binds $``\bot_{\text{dim}}``$, recovers on `;`, parsing `Assembly_Epsilon`, emitting `E0199` at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300`, `E0199`.
