## 1. Normative Statement

The state machine solver SHALL validate failsafe fallback transitions, priority preemptive evacuation, and absorbing safe states across discrete statecharts.

Validation MUST enforce:
1. **Fallback Completeness**: States $``s \in \mathcal{S}_{\text{op}}``$ and faults $``e_{\text{fault}} \in \mathcal{E}_{\text{fault}}``$ require transition $``T_{\text{failsafe}}``$ to $``s_{\text{safe}} \in \mathcal{S}_{\text{safe}}``$, defined on $s$ or inherited ($``s_{\text{src}} \in \text{scope}(s)``$); missing coverage emits `E0400`.
2. **Preemptive Priority**: Fallback transitions require priority $``\pi(T_{\text{failsafe}})``$ exceeding nominal transitions in scope ($``\\pi(T_{\text{failsafe}}) \gt \pi(T_{\text{nominal}})``$); inversions emit `E0410`.
3. **Priority Disjointness**: Fallback transitions from state $s$ or ancestors for identical faults require distinct priorities ($``\pi(T_1) \ne \pi(T_2)``$); conflicts emit `E0410`.
4. **Tautological Guard**: Guards must evaluate unconditionally to true ($g \equiv \text{true}$); non-tautological guards emit `E0410`.
5. **Safe State Absorption & Reset**: State $``s_{\text{safe}}``$ must be reachable via $``G_{\text{failsafe}}``$ (unreachable states emit `E0400`). State $``s_{\text{safe}}``$ is absorbing: spontaneous ($\epsilon$) or nominal exits emit `E0400`; exit requires certified event $``e_{\text{reset}} \in \mathcal{E}_{\text{reset}}``$. Transitions bind atomic safing actions $``A_{\text{safing}}``$.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Failsafe coverage completeness, preemptive priority ordering, tautological guard satisfaction, safe state absorption, and diagnostic triggers:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{S}=\mathcal{S}_{\text{op}}\cup\mathcal{S}_{\text{safe}}\cup\mathcal{S}_{\text{init}},\quad \mathcal{E}=\mathcal{E}_{\text{nominal}}\cup\mathcal{E}_{\text{fault}}\cup\mathcal{E}_{\text{reset}} \\
\mathcal{T}_{\text{failsafe}}=\{T\in\mathcal{T}\mid e_{\text{trig}}(T)\in\mathcal{E}_{\text{fault}}\land s_{\text{tgt}}(T)\in\mathcal{S}_{\text{safe}}\} \\
\forall s\in\mathcal{S}_{\text{op}},\;\forall e\in\mathcal{E}_{\text{fault}},\;\exists T\in\mathcal{T}_{\text{failsafe}}:s_{\text{src}}(T)\in\text{scope}(s)\land e_{\text{trig}}(T)=e \\
\forall T_{\text{fail}}\in\mathcal{T}_{\text{failsafe}},\;\forall T_{\text{nom}}\in\mathcal{T}_{\text{nominal}}\text{ in scope},\;\pi(T_{\text{fail}})\gt\pi(T_{\text{nom}}) \\
\forall T\in\mathcal{T}_{\text{failsafe}},\;g(T)\equiv\text{true},\quad \forall s\in\mathcal{S}_{\text{op}},\;s_{\text{safe}}\in\text{Reach}(s,G_{\text{failsafe}}) \\
\forall T\in\mathcal{T},\;s_{\text{src}}(T)\in\mathcal{S}_{\text{safe}}\implies(e_{\text{trig}}(T)\in\mathcal{E}_{\text{reset}}\land e_{\text{trig}}(T)\ne\epsilon) \\
(\text{MissingFallback}(s,e)\lor s_{\text{safe}}\notin\text{Reach}(s,G_{\text{failsafe}})\lor\text{SpontaneousSafeExit}(T))\implies\text{Diagnostic}(u,\text{E0400})\land u\gets\bot_{\text{failsafe}} \\
(\pi(T_{\text{nom}})\ge\pi(T_{\text{fail}})\lor\text{EqualPrioConflict}(T_1,T_2)\lor g(T_{\text{fail}})\not\equiv\text{true})\implies\text{Diagnostic}(T,\text{E0410})\land T\gets\bot_{\text{trans}} \\
\forall u\in\mathcal{S}\cup\mathcal{T}\cup\mathcal{E},\;\Delta_u=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(u))
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $``O(|S| \cdot |E_{\text{fault}}|)``$ fallback coverage; $O(V + E)$ reverse BFS safe reachability; aggregate is $``O(|S| \cdot |E_{\text{fault}}| + |\mathcal{T}|)``$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Avoids NP-5 / PSPACE state explosion (REQ-0124) via static graph checks. Work-stealing scales as $``O((|S| \cdot |E_{\text{fault}}| + |\mathcal{T}|) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Direct and Hierarchical Fallback Evacuation
- **Given:** `State_Nominal_Alpha` in `State_Super_0`, `State_Safe_Omega`, and fallback `Transition_Failsafe_1` from `State_Super_0` ($\pi = 100, g \equiv \text{true}$).
- **When:** Validating fallback coverage and transition priorities.
- **Then:** Certifies inherited coverage, preempting nominal transitions ($\pi = 10 \lt 100$).
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Safe State Reachability and Absorbing Boundary
- **Given:** Operational states `State_Operational_0`, `State_Operational_1` targeting `State_Safe_Omega`, with exit on `Event_Reset`.
- **When:** Validating reachability and absorbing boundaries.
- **Then:** Reverse BFS confirms reachability in $O(V + E)$ time; verifies no spontaneous ($\epsilon$) or nominal exits.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Missing Fallback and Isolation Rejection
- **Given:** `State_Operational_0` lacking fallback for `Event_Fault_Critical`, or `State_Safe_Omega` with spontaneous exit.
- **When:** Auditing fallback completeness and absorbing boundaries.
- **Then:** Rejects model, emits `E0400`, and binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{failsafe}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` (MissingFailsafeTransition or SpontaneousSafeStateExit).

### AC-04: Priority Inversion and Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Failsafe transition with guard `param_x > 0.0` or priority $``\pi = 5 \le \pi_{\text{nominal}} = 10``$ before `;`, followed by a valid state.
- **When:** Evaluating semantic rules and delimiter recovery.
- **Then:** Emits `E0410`, binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{trans}}``$, recovers on `;`, halting at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0410`, `E0199`.
