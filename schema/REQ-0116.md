## 1. Normative Statement

The dynamics solver SHALL perform path consistency constraint propagation using Allen's $13 \times 13$ composition table, verifying qualitative temporal constraints.

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Matrix Model**: Encodes `TemporalNetwork_Alpha` as matrix $``\mathbf{M} \in (2^{\mathcal{B}_{13}})^{N \times N}``$. Diagonals initialize to $\{ \mathbf{eq} \} = 0\text{x}1000$; unconstrained off-diagonals to $\mathbf{U} = 0\text{x}1\text{FFF}$. Assertion $``C(X_i, X_j) = R``$ sets $\mathbf{M}[i, j] \leftarrow R$ and $\mathbf{M}[j, i] \leftarrow R^\smile$.
2. **Composition Table**: Precomputes 169 compositions $``\mathbf{T}_{\text{comp}}[r_1][r_2]``$ as 13-bit masks. Disjunctive composition $``R_1 \circ R_2``$ evaluates in $O(1)$ via bitwise OR over active bits.
3. **Queue-Driven PC-2**: Queue $Q$ tracks revised pairs. For popped $(i, j)$ and vertex $k$, updates $\mathbf{M}[k, j] \leftarrow \mathbf{M}[k, j] \cap (\mathbf{M}[k, i] \circ \mathbf{M}[i, j])$ and converse $\mathbf{M}[j, k] \leftarrow (\mathbf{M}[k, j])^\smile$.
4. **Monotonic Convergence**: Enforces $\mathbf{M}^{(t+1)}[i, j] \subseteq \mathbf{M}^{(t)}[i, j]$, terminating within $13 \cdot N(N - 1)$ insertions since cells prune at most 13 times.
5. **Conflict Rejection**: Emptied cells ($\mathbf{M}[i, j] = \emptyset$) emit `E0400`; reflexivity breaches ($\mathbf{eq} \notin \mathbf{M}[i, i]$) emit `E0401`.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Qualitative relation bitmask encoding, composition evaluation, path consistency fixed point, and diagnostic triggers:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{m}_R=\sum_{k=0}^{12}b_k\cdot 2^k\in [0,8191],\quad R_1\circ R_2=\bigcup_{r_1\in R_1}\bigcup_{r_2\in R_2}\mathbf{T}_{\text{comp}}[r_1][r_2] \\
\mathbf{M}^{(t+1)}[i,j]=\mathbf{M}^{(t)}[i,j]\cap (\mathbf{M}^{(t)}[i,k]\circ\mathbf{M}^{(t)}[k,j]),\quad \mathbf{M}[j,i]\equiv (\mathbf{M}[i,j])^\smile \\
\forall i,j,k\in \{1,\dots,N\},\quad \mathbf{M}^*[i,j]\subseteq \mathbf{M}^*[i,k]\circ\mathbf{M}^*[k,j] \\
(\exists i,j:\mathbf{M}[i,j]=\emptyset)\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\langle X_i,X_j\rangle,\text{E0400})\land\mathbf{M}\gets\bot_{\text{tcn}} \\
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(1)$ composition lookup; $O(N^2)$ matrix allocation; $O(N^3)$ path consistency over $N$ intervals.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Polynomial fragment of NP-1 (Temporal Satisfiability). Work-stealing scales as $``O((K \cdot N^3) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Direct Transitive Deduction
- **Given:** Asserted $``X_1 \mathbf{b} X_2``$ (`before`) and $``X_2 \mathbf{b} X_3``$ in `TemporalNetwork_Alpha`.
- **When:** Path consistency executes.
- **Then:** Refines $``\mathbf{M}[X_1, X_3]``$ from $\mathbf{U} = 0\text{x}1\text{FFF}$ to $\{ \mathbf{b} \} = 0\text{x}0001$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Converse Symmetry and Fixed-Point Idempotency
- **Given:** Consistent network of $N = 10$ intervals converged to $\mathbf{M}^*$.
- **When:** Verifying symmetry and re-executing propagation on $\mathbf{M}^*$.
- **Then:** All $\mathbf{M}^*[j, i] \equiv (\mathbf{M}^*[i, j])^\smile$, queue $|Q| = 0$, with zero mutations.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Latent Conflict Rejection
- **Given:** Circular triad $``X_1 \mathbf{b} X_2``$, $``X_2 \mathbf{b} X_3``$, and $``X_3 \mathbf{b} X_1``$.
- **When:** Propagation intersects deduced $``X_1 \mathbf{b} X_3``$ with $``(X_3 \mathbf{b} X_1)^\smile = \mathbf{bi}``$.
- **Then:** Intersection yields $\emptyset$, rejecting with `E0400`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` (PathConsistencyViolation).

### AC-04: Reflexive Contradiction and Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Invalid $``X_1 \mathbf{b} X_1``$ before `;`, then valid `TemporalNetwork_Beta`.
- **When:** Lowering and constraint ingestion execute.
- **Then:** Emits `E0401`, binds $``\bot_{\text{tcn}}``$, recovers at `;`, parsing `TemporalNetwork_Beta`, halting at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0401`, `E0199`.
