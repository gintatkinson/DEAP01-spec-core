## 1. Normative Statement

The compiler SHALL enforce compile-time dangling port gate `DanglingGate_Alpha` over boundary ports during Level 1C ICD generation.

---

## 2. Formal Invariant

Let $``\mathcal{P}_{\text{boundary}}``$ denote boundary ports, $\mathcal{C}$ channels, $\mathcal{F}$ flows, and $\mathcal{B}$ delegation bindings. The gate satisfies:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{P}_{\text{mandatory}} = \{p \in \mathcal{P}_{\text{boundary}} \mid m_{\min}(p) \ge 1\}, & \quad \mathcal{P}_{\text{optional}} = \mathcal{P}_{\text{boundary}} \setminus \mathcal{P}_{\text{mandatory}} \\
\deg(p) = \sum_{e \in \mathcal{C} \cup \mathcal{F} \cup \mathcal{B}} [p \in \mathcal{E}(e)], & \quad \forall p \in \mathcal{P}_{\text{mandatory}}, \, \deg(p) \ge 1 \lor \text{IsTerminated}(p) \\
\forall p \in \mathcal{P}_{\text{boundary}}, \, (\text{IsTerminated}(p) \implies \deg(p) = 0) & \land (\neg\text{IsTerminated}(p) \implies \deg(p) \le m_{\max}(p)) \\
(\deg(p) = 0 \land \neg\text{IsTerminated}(p)) \implies \text{Diagnostic}(p, \text{E0220}) & \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \land p \gets \bot_{\text{port}} \\
((\deg(p) \ge 1 \land \text{IsTerminated}(p)) \lor \deg(p) \gt m_{\max}(p)) & \implies \text{Diagnostic}(p, \text{E0220}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
\text{Target}(e) \notin \mathcal{P}_{\text{boundary}} \implies \text{Diagnostic}(e, \text{E0200}) & \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}| \ge C_{\text{max\_errors}} \implies \text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}, & \quad \Delta_x = \text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(x))
\end{aligned}
```

| Symbol / Term | Domain / Formal Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| $``\mathcal{P}_{\text{boundary}}, \mathcal{P}_{\text{mandatory}}``$ | Boundary ports and mandatory subset ($``m_{\min} \ge 1``$) |
| $\deg(p), \mathcal{E}(e)$ | Port degree and endpoint projection |
| $\text{IsTerminated}(p), \mu(p)$ | Termination marker and multiplicity interval |
| $``\bot_{\text{port}}, \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}, C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ | Sentinel, diagnostic sink, and capacity limit |
| $``\Delta_x, \text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}``$ | UUIDv5 anchor and namespace OID |

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial Time).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $``O(|P_{\text{mandatory}}|)``$ dangling scan after $O(|V| + |E|)$ degree aggregation ($``V = |\mathcal{P}_{\text{boundary}}|``$, $E = |\mathcal{C}| + |\mathcal{F}| + |\mathcal{B}|$). Pool bound: $``O((|V| + |E|)/N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$. Space: $O(|V| + |E|)$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** N/A (Deterministic P). Linear evaluation, bypassing NP-2 (REQ-0147) and NP-8 (REQ-0197).

---

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Connected Mandatory Port
- **Given:** Port `Port_Mandatory` ($``m_{\min} = 1``$) connected via `Channel_0`.
- **When:** Lowering model through gate `DanglingGate_Alpha`.
- **Then:** Evaluates $\deg(p) = 1$ and passes.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Explicitly Terminated Port
- **Given:** Port `Port_Mandatory` with $\deg(p) = 0$ marked `IsTerminated(p) == true`.
- **When:** Evaluating connectivity and termination.
- **Then:** Confirms $\deg(p) = 0$ and passes.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Dangling Port Rejection
- **Given:** Port `Port_Mandatory` with $\deg(p) = 0 \land \neg\text{IsTerminated}(p)$.
- **When:** Evaluating dangling port gate.
- **Then:** Fails closed, emits `E0220`, and binds $``\bot_{\text{port}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0220` (DanglingBoundaryPortViolation).

### AC-04: Conflicting Termination & Recovery
- **Given:** Ports with $\deg(p) \ge 1 \land \text{IsTerminated}(p)$ and missing `Endpoint_0`, separated by `;`.
- **When:** Delimiter recovery on syntax and semantic faults.
- **Then:** Emits `E0220` and `E0200`, resynchronizes, and halts at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0220`, `E0200`, `E0199`.
