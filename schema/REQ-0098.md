## 1. Normative Statement

The metrology engine SHALL model physical dimensions as vector space $(\mathbb{Q}^7,+,\cdot)$ over $\mathbb{Q}$. Canonical basis $``\mathcal{B}_{\text{SI}} = \{\mathbf{e}_M, \dots, \mathbf{e}_J\}``$ maps bijectively to the 7 SI base quantities ($M, L, T, I, \Theta, N, J$).

Metamodel lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Irreducible Coordinates**: Every dimension $\mathbf{d} \in \mathbb{Q}^7$ is a tuple $(\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta,\epsilon,\zeta,\eta)$ with $``d_i = \frac{p_i}{q_i}``$ ($``\gcd(|p_i|,q_i)=1, q_i \in \mathbb{Z}^+``$). Zero vector $\mathbf{0}$ models dimensionless scalars.
2. **Homomorphisms**: $``\mathbf{d}(Q_1 \cdot Q_2) = \mathbf{d}(Q_1) + \mathbf{d}(Q_2)``$, $``\mathbf{d}(Q_1 / Q_2) = \mathbf{d}(Q_1) - \mathbf{d}(Q_2)``$, $\mathbf{d}(Q^k) = k \cdot \mathbf{d}(Q)$ ($k \in \mathbb{Q}$).
3. **Exact Arithmetic**: Operations use checked 64-bit rationals. Divisors $q = 0$ or overflow emit `E0300`.
4. **Homogeneity**: Operations ($A \pm B$, $A == B$, $A \bowtie B$) require $\mathbf{d}(A) = \mathbf{d}(B)$; mismatches emit `E0300`.

\mathbf{d}(Q_1 \cdot Q_2) &= \mathbf{d}(Q_1) + \mathbf{d}(Q_2),\; \mathbf{d}(Q_1 / Q_2) = \mathbf{d}(Q_1) - \mathbf{d}(Q_2),\; \mathbf{d}(Q^k) = k \cdot \mathbf{d}(Q) \\

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical Basis Initialization
- **Given:** Compiler loading `Package_0`.
- **When:** Bootstrapping basis $``\mathcal{B}_{\text{SI}}``$ and dimensionless constants.
- **Then:** 7 base dimensions map to $``\mathbf{e}_1 \dots \mathbf{e}_7 \in \mathbb{Q}^7``$ and scalars map to $\mathbf{0}$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Exact Vector Arithmetic and Exponentiation
- **Given:** Quantities `Quantity_1` ($(1,1,-2,0,0,0,0)$) and `Quantity_2` ($(0,1,0,0,0,0,0)$) with $k = 1/2$.
- **When:** Lowering `Quantity_1 * Quantity_2`, `Quantity_1 / Quantity_2`, and `Quantity_2^(1/2)`.
- **Then:** Dimensions evaluate to $``\mathbf{d}_1 + \mathbf{d}_2``$, $``\mathbf{d}_1 - \mathbf{d}_2``$, and $``k \cdot \mathbf{d}_2``$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Static Incommensurability Rejection
- **Given:** Expression `param_x : Real [DimVector_Alpha] + param_y : Real [DimVector_Beta]` with $``\mathbf{d}_{\alpha} \ne \mathbf{d}_{\beta}``$.
- **When:** Dimensional type checking executes.
- **Then:** Lowering halts evaluation, assigns sentinel $``\bot_{\text{dim}}``$, and emits `E0300`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300` (DimensionalMismatch).

### AC-04: Invalid Denominator Rejection and Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Malformed `DimVector_Alpha` ($``q_i = 0``$) preceding valid `DimVector_Beta`.
- **When:** Delimiter recovery executes.
- **Then:** Ingestion emits `E0300`, recovers on delimiter, and halts at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300`, `E0199`.
