## 1. Normative Statement

The compiler SHALL execute dimensional algebra over $\mathbb{Q}$ without floating-point arithmetic, synthesizing flow continuity ($\sum f = 0$) and loop conservation ($\sum \Delta e = 0$).

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Rational Exponents**: Coordinates in $\mathbb{Q}^7$ bind irreducible fractions $a/b$ ($b \gt 0$); operations evaluate $``\mathbf{d}_1 \pm \mathbf{d}_2``$ and $k \cdot \mathbf{d}$; $b = 0$ emits `E0300`.
2. **Flow Continuity**: Multigraph $``\mathcal{G}_{\text{flow}}``$ lowers to incidence matrix $\mathbf{A}$, synthesizing $``\mathbf{A} \mathbf{f} = \mathbf{0}``$ at conservative junctions.
3. **Cycle Conservation**: Matrix $\mathbf{B}$ lowers from cycle basis $\mathcal{C}$, synthesizing $``\mathbf{B} \mathbf{\Delta e} = \mathbf{0}``$ with $\mathbf{A} \mathbf{B}^T = \mathbf{0}$ and $\mathbf{f}^T \mathbf{\Delta e} = 0$.
4. **Homogeneity**: Flows and potentials MUST match in $\mathbb{Q}^7$ (mismatches emit `E0300`); balance faults emit `E0301`.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Rational arithmetic and topological conservation invariants:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\forall \mathbf{d} \in \mathbb{Q}^7,\; &\mathbf{d} = (q_1, \dots, q_7),\; q_i \in \mathbb{Q},\; \mathbf{d}(Q_1 Q_2) = \mathbf{d}_1 + \mathbf{d}_2,\; \mathbf{d}(Q^k) = k \cdot \mathbf{d} \\
\mathbf{A} \mathbf{f} &= \mathbf{0} \iff \forall J_i \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}},\; \sum_{e \in \text{Incident}(J_i)} A_{i, e} f_e = 0 \\
\mathbf{B} \mathbf{\Delta e} &= \mathbf{0} \iff \forall \mathcal{L}_k \in \mathcal{C},\; \sum_{e \in \text{Edges}(\mathcal{L}_k)} B_{k, e} \Delta e_e = 0 \\
\mathbf{A} \mathbf{B}^T &= \mathbf{0},\; \mathbf{f}^T \mathbf{\Delta e} = 0,\; \Delta_n = \text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(n))
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(K + E \cdot V) \subseteq O(K + E^2)$, linear $O(K + V + E)$ on sparse graphs ($``d_{\text{max}} \ll V``$).
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Cycle basis extraction avoids exponential search (NP-4). Work-stealing scales as $``O((K + E \cdot V) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Exact Rational Exponent Arithmetic & Scaling
- **Given:** Expressions $``Q_1 : \text{Mass}^{1/2}``$ and $``Q_2 : \text{Mass}^{1/3}``$ in `Package_0`.
- **When:** Lowering computes $``Q_1 \cdot Q_2``$ and $``(Q_1)^{2/3}``$.
- **Then:** Exponents in $\mathbb{Q}^7$ evaluate to $5/6$ and $1/3$ without drift.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Zero-Denominator & Incommensurable Dimensions
- **Given:** Exponent $3/0$ or junction with mismatched flow dimensions.
- **When:** Dimensional checking executes.
- **Then:** Engine halts, assigns sentinel $``\bot_{\text{dim}}``$, emitting `E0300`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300`.

### AC-03: Sparse Incidence Matrix & Continuity Synthesis
- **Given:** Network `Network_Alpha` with 4 junctions and 5 branches in `Package_0`.
- **When:** Constraint synthesis lowers topology.
- **Then:** Matrix $\mathbf{A}$ lowers and $``\mathbf{A} \mathbf{f} = \mathbf{0}``$ synthesizes.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-04: Static Conservation Violation & Recovery
- **Given:** Unbalanced static flow preceding delimiter (`;`) and valid flow.
- **When:** Semantic evaluation executes with error recovery.
- **Then:** Engine emits `E0301`, resynchronizes on delimiter, emitting `E0199` at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0301`, `E0199`.
