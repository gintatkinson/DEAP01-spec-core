## 1. Normative Statement

The ICD engine of Subsystem 8 SHALL synthesize Connection Binding Rosters mapping connections to endpoints, channels, and stream IDs, computing exact binary wire layouts.

---

## 2. Formal Invariant

The binding tuple, layout recurrence, packing boundary containment, and byte-swapping satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\text{Binding}(C)&=\langle C,\text{ChannelID},p_s,p_t,\text{DataRate},\Pi_{\text{protocol}},\Lambda_{\text{layout}}\rangle \\
\text{Offset}(\phi_1)&=0,\quad\text{Offset}(\phi_{i+1})=\text{Offset}(\phi_i)+\text{BitWidth}(\phi_i)+\text{PadBits}(\phi_i,\text{Align}(\phi_{i+1})) \\
\text{PadBits}(\phi_i,A)&=(A-((\text{Offset}(\phi_i)+\text{BitWidth}(\phi_i))\bmod A))\bmod A \\
\text{TotalBits}&=\text{RawBits}+((8-(\text{RawBits}\bmod 8))\bmod 8) \\
\forall\phi_i\in\Phi(C),&\quad\lfloor\text{Offset}(\phi_i)/W\rfloor=\lfloor(\text{Offset}(\phi_i)+\text{BitWidth}(\phi_i)-1)/W\rfloor \\
\mathcal{E}_{\Pi}(\mathbf{b})&=\begin{cases}\mathbf{b},&\text{if }\text{HostEndian}=\Pi_{\text{endian}}\\\text{ByteSwap}(\mathbf{b}),&\text{otherwise}\end{cases} \\
(p_s\notin\mathcal{P}\lor p_t\notin\mathcal{P})&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(C,\text{E0200})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
(\text{CrossesBoundary}\lor\text{AlignFault})&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\phi_i,\text{E0206})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}
\end{aligned}
```

| Symbol / Term | Domain / Formal Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| $C, \text{Binding}(C)$ | Connection handle and 7-tuple binding record |
| $``p_s, p_t, \Pi_{\text{protocol}}``$ | Endpoint ports and protocol framing specification |
| $``\Lambda_{\text{layout}}, \Phi(C)``$ | Wire layout tuple and ordered payload fields |
| $\text{Offset}, \text{BitWidth}$ | Bit start offset and field width in bits |
| $\text{Align}, \text{PadBits}, W$ | Alignment constraint, padding, and word width |
| $``\mathcal{E}_{\Pi}, \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}``$ | Byte mapping and diagnostic error sink |

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(|\mathcal{C}| \cdot \sum |\text{fields}|)$ wire serialization layout calculation. Work-stealing achieves $``O((|\mathcal{C}| \cdot \sum |\text{fields}|) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$. Arena footprint is $O(\sum |\text{fields}|)$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** N/A (Deterministic P). Single-pass modular layout synthesis avoids NP-2 channel packing (REQ-0147) and NP-8 solving (REQ-0197).

---

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical Binding & Wire Layout
- **Given:** Connection `Connection_Alpha` between `Port_1` and `Port_2` on `Channel_0` with `Field_0` and `Field_1`.
- **When:** Synthesizing binding rosters and wire layouts.
- **Then:** Emits `Binding_0` and `WireLayout_1`; diagnostic sink is empty.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Deterministic Byte Alignment & Padding
- **Given:** Fields `Field_0` (width 24, align 8) and `Field_1` (width 32, align 32).
- **When:** Computing field offsets and padding.
- **Then:** Inserts $\text{PadBits} = 8$, placing `Field_1` at offset 32.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Bitfield Storage Word Boundary Violation
- **Given:** Field `Field_0` (width 5) at offset 5 in word $W = 8$ without split permission.
- **When:** Enforcing bitfield word containment.
- **Then:** Rejects layout, binds $``\bot_{\text{layout}}``$, and emits `E0206`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0206` on boundary violation.

### AC-04: Unmapped Endpoint & Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Connection `Connection_Alpha` referencing unmapped `Port_1` with delimiters (`;`, `}`).
- **When:** Validating endpoint resolution and error recovery.
- **Then:** Records `E0200` in $``\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}``$, recovers, and emits `E0199` at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0200` on unmapped endpoint; `E0199` on error saturation.
