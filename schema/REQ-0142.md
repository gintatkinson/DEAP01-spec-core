## 1. Normative Statement

The safety compiler SHALL synthesize smooth transition interpolation for Run-Time Assurance (RTA) switching logic, interpolating `Signal_0` between `Classifier_Alpha` and `Classifier_Beta` via blender `BlendFunction_Alpha` across duration `TransTime_1` ($``T_{\text{switch}}``$).

---

## 2. Formal Invariant

Every transition blender parameterized by normalized transition time $\tau \in [0, 1]$ satisfies:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\tau(t) &= \frac{t - t_{\text{switch}}}{T_{\text{switch}}}, \quad \mathbf{u}_{\text{blend}}(t) = (1 - \lambda(\tau)) \mathbf{u}_{\text{primary}}(t) + \lambda(\tau) \mathbf{u}_{\text{recovery}}(t) \\
\lambda(\tau) &= 3\tau^2 - 2\tau^3, \quad \lambda(0) = 0, \quad \lambda(1) = 1, \quad \left.\frac{d\lambda}{d\tau}\right|_{\tau=0,1} = 0 \\
\lim_{t \to t_{\text{switch}}^+} \dot{\mathbf{u}}_{\text{blend}} &= \dot{\mathbf{u}}_{\text{primary}}, \quad \lim_{t \to (t_{\text{switch}} + T_{\text{switch}})^-} \dot{\mathbf{u}}_{\text{blend}} = \dot{\mathbf{u}}_{\text{recovery}} \\
\sup \|\dot{\mathbf{u}}_{\text{blend}}\| &\le \dot{\mathbf{u}}_{\max}, \quad \forall \tau \in [0, 1], \; \mathbf{u}_{\text{blend}}(\tau) \in [u_{\min}, u_{\max}] \\
(T_{\text{switch}} \le 0) \lor (T_{\text{switch}} \gt T_{\text{max\_switch}}) &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0400}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
(\sup \|\dot{\mathbf{u}}_{\text{blend}}\| \gt \dot{\mathbf{u}}_{\max}) \lor (\mathbf{u}_{\text{blend}} \notin [u_{\min}, u_{\max}]) &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0440}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}| \ge C_{\text{max\_errors}} \implies \text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}, &\quad \Delta_b = \text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(b))
\end{aligned}
```

| Symbol / Term | Domain / Formal Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| $\tau$ | Normalized parameter in $[0, 1]$ |
| $``T_{\text{switch}}``$ | Duration ($``T_{\text{switch}} \gt 0``$) |
| $``\mathbf{u}_{\text{blend}}``$ | Blended vector in $\mathbb{R}^m$ |
| $\lambda(\tau)$ | Cubic polynomial $3\tau^2 - 2\tau^3$ |
| $``\dot{\mathbf{u}}_{\max}``$ | Slew-rate limit in $(\mathbb{Q}^+)^m$ |
| $``\Delta_b``$ | UUIDv5 anchor string |

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$.
- **Asymptotic Bound:** Polynomial evaluation runs in $O(1)$ per step; slew-rate checks require $O(m)$ for $m$ dimensions; AST validation runs in $O(V + E)$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** N/A (Deterministic P). Polynomial interpolation over $[0, 1]$ bypasses NP frontiers (NP-1 through NP-8).

---

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Nominal Blending Synthesis & $C^1$-Continuity
- **Given:** `Package_0` declaring `BlendFunction_Alpha` with $``T_{\text{switch}} = 0.5\,\text{s}``$.
- **When:** Transition blending synthesis executes.
- **Then:** Synthesizes $\lambda(\tau) = 3\tau^2 - 2\tau^3$ matching endpoint derivatives with $C^1$-continuity.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Actuator Envelope & Slew-Rate Conformance
- **Given:** Blender with signals in $``[u_{\min}, u_{\max}]``$ and rate below limit $``\dot{\mathbf{u}}_{\max}``$.
- **When:** Envelope and rate verification executes.
- **Then:** Verifies $``\mathbf{u}_{\text{blend}}(\tau) \in [u_{\min}, u_{\max}]``$ and $``\sup \|\dot{\mathbf{u}}_{\text{blend}}\| \le \dot{\mathbf{u}}_{\max}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Invalid Switching Duration Rejection
- **Given:** Model `Package_0` with invalid duration ($``T_{\text{switch}} \le 0``$ or $``T_{\text{switch}} \gt T_{\text{max\_switch}}``$).
- **When:** Timing validation executes.
- **Then:** Lowering halts with diagnostic `E0400`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` on invalid duration.

### AC-04: Actuator Slew-Rate Violation
- **Given:** Blender with peak rate $``\sup \|\dot{\mathbf{u}}_{\text{blend}}\| \gt \dot{\mathbf{u}}_{\max}``$.
- **When:** Slew-rate verification executes.
- **Then:** Halts with diagnostic `E0440`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0440` on slew-rate violation.
