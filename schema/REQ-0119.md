## 1. Normative Statement

The dynamics engine SHALL lower action sequences, fork nodes, and join nodes into execution DAGs, verifying precedence, barrier synchronization, and data-race freedom.

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Sequential Precedence**: Steps $``\langle s_1, \dots, s_n \rangle``$ with $``(s_i, s_{i+1}) \in E_{\text{ctrl}}``$ require $``t_{\text{end}}(s_i) \le t_{\text{start}}(s_{i+1})``$; immediate chaining requires equality. Inversions emit `E0400`.
2. **Fork Semantics**: $``N_{\text{fork}}``$ requires $\text{in-deg} = 1$ and fan-out $k \ge 2$ (faults emit `E0400`), replicating tokens across $``P_1, \dots, P_k``$ at $``t_{\text{start}}(P_i) = t_{\text{end}}(N_{\text{fork}})``$.
3. **Barrier Synchronization**: $``N_{\text{join}}``$ requires $\text{in-deg} = k \ge 2$ and $\text{out-deg} = 1$ (faults emit `E0400`), synchronizing at $``t_{\text{start}}(N_{\text{join}}) = \max_i t_{\text{end}}(P_i)``$; early dispatch emits `E0404`.
4. **Token Conservation**: $``N_{\text{join}}``$ MUST consume $k$ tokens ($``\Delta\text{Tokens}_{\text{net}} = 0``$). Deficits or unjoined paths emit `E0400`.
5. **Bernstein Independence**: Concurrent branches $``P_i, P_j``$ require pairwise disjoint read/write sets ($``\mathcal{W}_i \cap \mathcal{W}_j = \emptyset``$, $``\mathcal{W}_i \cap \mathcal{R}_j = \emptyset``$); hazards emit `E0404`. $``G_{\text{exec}}``$ must be acyclic; cycles emit `E0400`.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Execution DAG topology, fork-join synchronization, and diagnostic trigger predicates:

```math
\begin{aligned}
G_{\text{exec}}=(V,E_{\text{ctrl}}\cup E_{\text{data}}),\quad V=V_{\text{step}}\cup V_{\text{ctrl}},\quad V_{\text{ctrl}}=V_{\text{fork}}\cup V_{\text{join}} \\
\forall (s_i,s_{i+1})\in E_{\text{ctrl}},\; t_{\text{end}}(s_i)\le t_{\text{start}}(s_{i+1}) \\
\text{in-deg}(N_{\text{fork}})=1\land\text{out-deg}(N_{\text{fork}})=k\ge 2,\; t_{\text{start}}(P_i)=t_{\text{end}}(N_{\text{fork}}) \\
\text{in-deg}(N_{\text{join}})=k\ge 2\land\text{out-deg}(N_{\text{join}})=1,\; t_{\text{start}}(N_{\text{join}})=\max_{i=1}^k t_{\text{end}}(P_i) \\
\text{Tokens}_{\text{consumed}}(N_{\text{join}})=\text{Tokens}_{\text{produced}}(N_{\text{fork}})=k\implies\Delta\text{Tokens}_{\text{net}}=0 \\
\forall i\ne j,\; (\mathcal{W}_i\cap\mathcal{W}_j=\emptyset)\land(\mathcal{W}_i\cap\mathcal{R}_j=\emptyset)\land(\mathcal{R}_i\cap\mathcal{W}_j=\emptyset) \\
\forall v\in V,\; v\notin\text{Reach}^+(v,G_{\text{exec}}),\quad \Delta_n=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(n)) \\
(\text{deg}(N)\text{ invalid}\lor\text{CycleDetected})\implies\text{Diagnostic}(n,\text{E0400}) \\
(t_{\text{start}}(N_{\text{join}})\lt\max_i t_{\text{end}}(P_i)\lor\text{DataRace})\implies\text{Diagnostic}(n,\text{E0404})
\end{aligned}
```

| :--- | :--- |

| $``N_{\text{fork}}, N_{\text{join}}, P_i``$ | Fork node, join barrier, and concurrent paths ($k \ge 2$) |
| $``t_{\text{start}}, t_{\text{end}}``$ | Occurrence interval timestamps in $``\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}``$ |
| $``\mathcal{R}_i, \mathcal{W}_i``$ | Path read and write variable sets for Bernstein checks |

| $``\bot_{\text{act}}, \bot_{\text{fork}}, \Delta_n``$ | Node error sentinels and deterministic UUIDv5 anchor |

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(V + E)$ for DAG lowering, cycle checking, and barrier timing; $``O(k \cdot |\mathcal{V}_{\text{var}}|)``$ for pairwise Bernstein data-race tests.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Avoids exponential Petri net reachability (NP/EXPSPACE frontier). Work-stealing scales as $``O((V + E) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Sequential Precedence Lowering
- **Given:** Steps `Step_Alpha`, `Step_Beta` with $``t_{\text{end}} = t_{\text{start}} = 10.0``$.
- **When:** Lowering constructs DAG $``G_{\text{exec}}``$ and verifies precedence.
- **Then:** Validates $``t_{\text{end}} \le t_{\text{start}}``$ without diagnostic errors.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Fork-Join Concurrency and Barrier Timing
- **Given:** Fork `ForkNode_1` ($k = 3$) with paths `Path_Alpha`, `Path_Beta`, `Path_Gamma` (durations $10.0, 25.0, 15.0$) meeting at `JoinNode_1`.
- **When:** Evaluating barrier synchronization and token conservation.
- **Then:** Computes $``t_{\text{start}}(\text{JoinNode\_1}) = 25.0``$ and $``\Delta\text{Tokens}_{\text{net}} = 0``$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Degree Malformation and Cycle Rejection
- **Given:** `ForkNode_1` with out-degree 1, or cycle $``\text{Step\_Alpha} \to \text{Step\_Beta} \to \text{Step\_Alpha}``$.
- **When:** Validating degrees and acyclicity via Kahn's sort.
- **Then:** Rejects invalid degrees and cycles, emitting `E0400`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` (MalformedControlNode).

### AC-04: Bernstein Race Hazard and Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Branches `Path_Alpha` and `Path_Beta` mutating `param_x : Real` before `;`, followed by `Step_Gamma`.
- **When:** Checking Bernstein conditions and recovery.
- **Then:** Emits `E0404`, recovers on `;`, parses `Step_Gamma`, halting at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0404`, `E0199`.
