## 1. Normative Statement

The state machine solver SHALL validate and lower hierarchical statecharts and orthogonal regions into active configurations.

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Tree Acyclicity**: States form rooted tree $(S, \sqsubseteq)$. States $``s \ne s_{\text{root}}``$ require unique $\text{parent}(s)$; cycles ($s \sqsubset^+ s$) or multi-parents emit `E0402`. Duplicate sibling names emit `E0402`.
2. **Taxonomic Partitioning**: States partition into atomic leaves $``\mathcal{S}_{\text{atomic}}``$, XOR composites $``\mathcal{S}_{\text{xor}}``$ (activating $\le 1$ child), AND composites $``\mathcal{S}_{\text{orth}}``$ ($m \ge 2$ regions), regions $\mathcal{R}$, and pseudo-states $\mathcal{P}$.
3. **Region Disjointness**: Orthogonal states require $m \ge 2$ regions; degenerate states emit `E0405`. Regions $``R_i \ne R_j``$ partition states into disjoint sets ($``\text{States}(R_i) \cap \text{States}(R_j) = \emptyset``$). Direct cross-region transitions emit `E0405`.
4. **Active Configurations**: Requires $``s_{\text{root}} \in C``$, ancestor closure $\text{ancestors}(s) \subseteq C$ (emits `E0402`), XOR singularity $|C \cap \mathcal{C}| = 1$ (emits `E0402`), and region concurrency $``|C \cap \text{Leaves}(R_i)| = 1``$ (emits `E0405`).
5. **LCA Scope & Synchronization**: Transition scope is $``\text{LCA}(s_{\text{src}}, s_{\text{tgt}})``$. Concurrent leaves $``l_1 \ne l_2 \in \text{Leaves}(C)``$ require $``\text{LCA}(l_1, l_2) \in \mathcal{S}_{\text{orth}}``$ (XOR LCA emits `E0402`). Cross-region transitions synchronize via fork/join nodes.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Statechart tree acyclicity, region disjointness, configuration validity, and diagnostic triggers:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{S}_{\text{all}}=\mathcal{S}_{\text{atomic}}\cup\mathcal{S}_{\text{xor}}\cup\mathcal{S}_{\text{orth}}\cup\mathcal{R}\cup\mathcal{P},\quad \forall s\in\mathcal{S}_{\text{all}}\setminus\{s_{\text{root}}\},\;\exists ! p\in\mathcal{S}_{\text{all}}:\text{parent}(s)=p \\
\forall s\in\mathcal{S}_{\text{all}},\;(s,s)\notin\sqsubset^+,\quad \forall S_{\text{orth}}\in\mathcal{S}_{\text{orth}},\;|\mathcal{R}(S_{\text{orth}})|=m\ge 2 \\
\forall R_i,R_j\in\mathcal{R}(S_{\text{orth}}),\;i\ne j\implies\text{States}(R_i)\cap\text{States}(R_j)=\emptyset \\
s_{\text{root}}\in C\land(\forall s\in C,\;\text{ancestors}(s)\subseteq C)\land(\forall S_{\text{xor}}\in C\cap\mathcal{S}_{\text{xor}},\;|C\cap\mathcal{C}_{S_{\text{xor}}}|=1) \\
\forall S_{\text{orth}}\in C\cap\mathcal{S}_{\text{orth}},\;\forall R_i\in\mathcal{R}(S_{\text{orth}}),\;|C\cap\text{Leaves}(R_i)|=1 \\
\forall l_1,l_2\in\text{Leaves}(C),\;l_1\ne l_2\implies\text{LCA}(l_1,l_2)\in\mathcal{S}_{\text{orth}} \\
((s,s)\in\sqsubset^+\lor\text{ancestors}(s)\not\subseteq C\lor |C\cap\mathcal{C}_{S_{\text{xor}}}|\ne 1)\implies\text{Diagnostic}(s,\text{E0402})\land s\gets\bot_{\text{state}} \\
(|\mathcal{R}(S_{\text{orth}})|\lt 2\lor |C\cap\text{Leaves}(R_i)|\ne 1\lor\text{UnsyncCrossRegion}(\tau))\implies\text{Diagnostic}(S_{\text{orth}},\text{E0405})\land S_{\text{orth}}\gets\bot_{\text{region}} \\
\forall s\in\mathcal{S}_{\text{all}},\;\Delta_s=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(s))
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(D)$ state hierarchy traversal ($D \le 32$); $O(M)$ orthogonal region check ($M = |\mathcal{R}|$); aggregate lowering is $``O(N + |\mathcal{E}_{\text{trans}}|)``$ for $``N = |\mathcal{S}_{\text{all}}|``$ states.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Avoids exponential search over NP-5 frontier. Work-stealing scales as $``O((N + |\mathcal{E}_{\text{trans}}|) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Hierarchical Tree & Active Configuration
- **Given:** `CompositeState_1` with children `State_Alpha`, `State_Beta`, activating `State_Alpha`.
- **When:** Lowering validates tree hierarchy and active states.
- **Then:** Verifies ancestor closure and XOR singularity ($|C \cap \mathcal{C}| = 1$).
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Orthogonal Concurrency & Region Disjointness
- **Given:** `ParallelState_Omega` with regions `Region_1`, `Region_2`, activating leaves in both.
- **When:** Validating region multiplicity, disjointness, and concurrency.
- **Then:** Confirms $m = 2 \ge 2$, disjoint sets, and $``|C \cap \text{Leaves}(R_i)| = 1``$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Containment Acyclicity & Ancestor Closure Rejection
- **Given:** Hierarchy cycle `State_Alpha` $\sqsubset$ `State_Beta` $\sqsubset$ `State_Alpha`, or active leaf without parent.
- **When:** Checking containment acyclicity and ancestor closure.
- **Then:** Rejects cycle or missing ancestor, emits `E0402`, and binds $``\bot_{\text{state}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0402` (StateHierarchyContainmentViolation).

### AC-04: Degenerate Region & Cross-Region Recovery
- **Given:** Degenerate parallel state ($m = 1$) before `;`, followed by an unsynchronized cross-region transition.
- **When:** Semantic validation and delimiter recovery execute.
- **Then:** Emits `E0405`, binds $``\bot_{\text{region}}``$, recovers on `;`, halting at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0405`, `E0199`.
