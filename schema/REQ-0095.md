## 1. Normative Statement

The compiler SHALL bootstrap SysML v2 `Quantities` (`Package_Quantities`) into root scope, formalizing quantities as magnitude-unit pairs and classifying ratio and interval scales.

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Taxonomy**: `QuantityValue` and `MeasurementScale` specialize `DataType`; `RatioScale` and `IntervalScale` specialize `MeasurementScale` disjointly ($`⟦ \text{RatioScale} ⟧ \cap ⟦ \text{IntervalScale} ⟧ = \emptyset`$). `Unit` binds dimension $`\mathbf{d}(u)`$ and scale $`S(u)`$. Non-data specializations emit `E0200`.
2. **Structure**: Nodes bind magnitude $`v \in \mathbb{R}`$, unit $`u \in \mathcal{U}`$, dimension $`\mathbf{d} \in \mathbb{Q}^7`$, and scale $`S`$; missing units emit `E0200`.
3. **Ratio Scales**: Absolute-zero origin ($``0 \cdot u = \mathbf{0}_{\text{abs}}``$) under $``\mathcal{G}_{\text{ratio}}``$; permits scaling ($`k \cdot Q`$) and homogeneous addition.
4. **Interval Scales**: Origin under $``\mathcal{G}_{\text{interval}}``$; permits difference ($``Q_1 - Q_2 \in \mathcal{Q}_{\text{ratio}}``$) and shift ($``Q_{\text{pos}} + Q_{\Delta} \in \mathcal{Q}_{\text{interval}}``$). Direct addition or scaling emits `E0200`.
5. **Homogeneity**: Operations require matching dimensions ($``\mathbf{d}(Q_1) \equiv \mathbf{d}(Q_2)``$); mismatches emit `E0300`.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Measurement scale semantics, transformation groups, and dimensional invariants:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\text{RatioScale} &\le \text{MeasurementScale} \le \text{DataType},\; \text{IntervalScale} \le \text{MeasurementScale} \\
⟦ \text{RatioScale} ⟧ &\cap ⟦ \text{IntervalScale} ⟧ = \emptyset,\; \text{Unit} \le \text{DataType} \\
Q &= \langle v, u, S, \mathbf{d} \rangle \in \mathbb{R} \times \mathcal{U} \times \mathcal{MS} \times \mathbb{Q}^7 \\
Q_1, Q_2 \in \mathcal{Q}_{\text{interval}} &\implies Q_1 - Q_2 \in \mathcal{Q}_{\text{ratio}},\; Q_{\text{pos}} + Q_{\Delta} \in \mathcal{Q}_{\text{interval}} \\
(Q_1 \pm Q_2) &\implies \mathbf{d}(Q_1) \equiv \mathbf{d}(Q_2),\; \Delta_n = \text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(n))
\end{aligned}
```

| Symbol / Term | Domain / Formal Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| $\mathcal{QV}, \mathcal{U}, \mathcal{MS}$ | Universes of quantities, units, and scales |
| $``\mathcal{Q}_{\text{ratio}}, \mathcal{Q}_{\text{interval}}``$ | Partitions on ratio and interval scales |
| $\mathbf{d}(u) \in \mathbb{Q}^7$ | 7D rational dimension vector |
| $``\Delta_n``$ | Deterministic UUIDv5 from $``\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}}``$ |

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(1)$ scale check; $``O(N_Q + N_U + N_S)``$ aggregate lowering.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Strictly polynomial; scale and 7D dimension checks execute in $O(1)$ time, bypassing NP-complete search (NP-1--NP-8). Parallel lowering scales as $``O((N_Q + N_U + N_S) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical Quantity Lowering
- **Given:** `Package_Quantities` with ratio scale `Scale_0` and unit `Unit_Alpha`.
- **When:** Lowering `Quantity_Alpha = 10.0 [Unit_Alpha]` and evaluating `2.5 * Quantity_Alpha`.
- **Then:** Node binds magnitude $25.0$, unit `Unit_Alpha`, scale `Scale_0`.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Interval Scale Operations
- **Given:** Interval scale `Scale_1` with units `Unit_Alpha` and `Unit_Beta` ($m = 1.8, c = 32.0$).
- **When:** Lowering difference `Quantity_Alpha - Quantity_Beta` and sum `Quantity_Alpha + Quantity_Delta`.
- **Then:** Difference lowers to $``Q_{\Delta} \in \mathcal{Q}_{\text{ratio}}``$; sum lowers to interval position in `Scale_1`.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Invalid Interval Scale Rejection
- **Given:** Model attempting addition (`Quantity_Alpha + Quantity_Beta`) or scaling (`2.0 * Quantity_Alpha`) in `Scale_1`.
- **When:** Validating scale invariants.
- **Then:** Rejection emits `E0200` and binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{quantity}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0200` (Invalid interval scale operation).

### AC-04: Dimensional Homogeneity Rejection
- **Given:** Model adding heterogeneous quantities ($``\mathbf{d}_1 \ne \mathbf{d}_2``$).
- **When:** Validating dimensional consistency.
- **Then:** Rejection halts lowering and emits `E0300`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300` (Dimensional homogeneity violation).
