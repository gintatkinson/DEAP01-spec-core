## 1. Normative Statement

The safety engine SHALL execute combinatorial tensor expansion and context pruning across control actions, universal STPA guide words, and operational context states.

Expansion and pruning MUST enforce:
1. **Control Actions & Guide Words**: Extracts actions $``A = \{ a_1, \dots, a_m \}``$ (`ControlAction_Alpha`) and binds 4-word alphabet $G$. Empty $A$ or malformed words emit `E0400`.
2. **Context States ($``S_{\text{context}}``$)**: Binds valuations $``s \in \prod_{j=1}^d D_j``$ ($``d \le d_{\max}``$). Inconsistent predicates emit `E0400`; overflow ($``d > d_{\max}``$, $``|U| > K_{\max}``$) emits `E0414`.
3. **Tensor Expansion ($U$)**: Constructs candidate tensor $``U = A \times G \times S_{\text{context}}``$ (`ContextTensor_0`) with $``|U| = 4 \cdot |A| \cdot |S_{\text{context}}|``$.
4. **Context Pruning**: Evaluates $``\Phi_{\text{reach}}``$, $``\Phi_{\text{active}}``$, and $``\Phi_{\text{hazard}}``$ (`Pruner_1`), pruning invalid tuples and retaining hazardous $``\mathcal{U}_{\text{pruned}} \subseteq U``$.
5. **Deterministic Reduction**: Evaluates tuples on work-stealing threads ($``P_{\text{workers}} \le N_{\text{max\_workers}}``$); canonical sorting guarantees bitwise parity; divergence emits `E0400`.

Directives: UUIDv5 anchors (Directive 1); work-stealing ($``N_{\text{max\_workers}}``$) (Directive 2); symbol interning (Directive 3); recovery on `;` with sentinels ($``\bot_{\text{uca}}``$, $``\bot_{\text{tensor}}``$), halting at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199` (Directive 4); Release budgets (Directive 5). Placeholders (`Package_0`, `ControlAction_Alpha`, `ContextTensor_0`, `Pruner_1`, `param_x : Real`) used.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Combinatorial candidate UCA tensor expansion, context pruning filter, and diagnostic triggers satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
U=A\times G\times S_{\text{context}},\quad |U|=4\cdot|A|\cdot|S_{\text{context}}|,\quad |G|=4,\quad d\le d_{\max} \\
s=\langle v_1,\dots,v_d\rangle\in\prod_{j=1}^d D_j,\quad |P_{\text{workers}}|\le N_{\text{max\_workers}} \\
\mathcal{U}_{\text{pruned}}=\{(a,g,s)\in U\mid\Phi_{\text{reach}}(s)\land\Phi_{\text{active}}(a,s)\land\Phi_{\text{hazard}}(a,g,s)\} \\
u_1\prec u_2\iff\langle\text{FQN}(a_1),\text{index}(g_1),\text{FQN}(s_1)\rangle\lt_{\text{lex}}\langle\text{FQN}(a_2),\text{index}(g_2),\text{FQN}(s_2)\rangle \\
(|A|=0\lor\exists g^*\notin G\lor\text{Contradictory}(s))\implies\text{Diagnostic}(n,\text{E0400})\land n\gets\bot_{\text{uca}} \\
(d\gt d_{\max}\lor |U|\gt K_{\max})\implies\text{Diagnostic}(U,\text{E0414})\land U\gets\bot_{\text{tensor}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199}),\quad\Delta_u=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(a)\mathbin{\Vert} g\mathbin{\Vert}\text{FQN}(s))
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial for $``d \le d_{\max}``$).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(|CA| \cdot 4^d)$ for $|CA| = |A|$ and $d$ binary dimensions (parameter $4^k$). Work-stealing: $``O((|CA| \cdot 4^d) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$. Canonical sorting: $O(K \log K)$ for $``K = |\mathcal{U}_{\text{pruned}}|``$. Space: $O(|CA| \cdot 4^d)$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Strictly polynomial for $``d \le d_{\max}``$, avoiding NP-4 (REQ-0135) NP-completeness.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical Tensor Expansion & Pruning
- **Given:** `Package_0` with 5 actions (`ControlAction_Alpha`), 4 guide words, and 10 states (`ContextTensor_0`).
- **When:** Lowering tensor expansion and pruning (`Pruner_1`).
- **Then:** Allocates $|U| = 200$ tuples and retains $``\mathcal{U}_{\text{pruned}}``$ records.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Dimensionality Limit & Tensor Threshold
- **Given:** `Package_0` with dimension $``d > d_{\max}``$ or tensor size $``|U| > K_{\max}``$.
- **When:** Allocating candidate tensor.
- **Then:** Emits `E0414` and binds $``\bot_{\text{tensor}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0414` (ContextDimensionalityLimitBreach).

### AC-03: Empty Action Set & Invalid Guide Word Rejection
- **Given:** `Package_0` with empty actions ($|A| = 0$) or $g \notin G$.
- **When:** Validating action and guide word alphabets.
- **Then:** Rejects input, emits `E0400`, and binds $``\bot_{\text{uca}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` (MalformedGuideWord).

### AC-04: Delimiter Recovery & Error Accumulation
- **Given:** Contradictory context before `;`, followed by valid action.
- **When:** Parser detects conflict and recovers on delimiter.
- **Then:** Emits `E0400`, recovers on `;`, and halts at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400`, `E0199`.

### AC-05: Bitwise Determinism & Release Performance
- **Given:** $10^4$ tuples across $``N_{\text{max\_workers}}``$ threads.
- **When:** Evaluating and sorting in Release mode (`--release`).
- **Then:** Emits bitwise identical records; latency $< 25\text{ ms}$, RSS $< 100\text{ MB}$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.
