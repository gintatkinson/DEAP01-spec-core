## 1. Normative Statement

The compiler SHALL bootstrap KerML `Collections` (`Package_Collections`) into root scope at initialization, providing `Collection<T>`, `Set<T>`, `Sequence<T>`, `OrderedSet<T>`, and `Bag<T>`.

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Taxonomy**: Types classify along flags $`\text{isUnique}`$ and $`\text{isOrdered}`$: `Set` ($1, 0$), `Sequence` ($0, 1$), `OrderedSet` ($1, 1$), and `Bag` ($0, 0$).
2. **Hierarchy**: Collections specialize `Collection<T>`. `OrderedSet<T>` specializes `Set<T>` and `Sequence<T>`; `Set<T>` specializes `Bag<T>`.
3. **Covariance**: Collections enforce covariant subtyping ($``T_1 \le T_2 \implies \mathcal{C}\langle T_1 \rangle \le \mathcal{C}\langle T_2 \rangle``$); violations emit `E0200`.
4. **Typing & Bounds**: Literals require $``\tau(e_i) \le T``$; mismatches emit `E0200`. Out-of-bounds indexing emits `E0200`.
5. **Functions**: Operations require `In` parameters (`InOut`/`Out` emits `E0201`) and Boolean predicates (non-Boolean emits `E0200`).
6. **Acyclicity**: Recursive collection aliases lacking base stratification emit `E0203`.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Metamodel subtyping, covariance, and UUIDv5 invariants:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{T}_{\text{coll}} &= \{\text{Collection}, \text{Set}, \text{Sequence}, \text{OrderedSet}, \text{Bag}\} \\
\forall T \in \mathcal{T},\; &\text{OrderedSet}\langle T \rangle = \text{Set}\langle T \rangle \wedge \text{Sequence}\langle T \rangle \le \text{Bag}\langle T \rangle \le \text{Collection}\langle T \rangle \\
\forall \mathcal{C} \in \mathcal{T}_{\text{coll}},\; &T_1 \le T_2 \implies \mathcal{C}\langle T_1 \rangle \le \mathcal{C}\langle T_2 \rangle \\
\forall p \in \mathcal{P}_{\text{op}},\; &\text{dir}(p) = \text{In},\quad \Delta_n = \text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(n))
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(1)$ lookup, subtyping, and memoization; $O(k)$ for literal ($k$ elements); $O(|c|)$ for operations on $c$. Lowering is $O(N + M)$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Covariant meet-semilattice subtyping evaluates in $O(1)$ via bitsets, bypassing NP-complete search (NP-3). Work-stealing scales as $``O((N + M) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical `Collections` Bootstrapping
- **Given:** Initialized compiler environment.
- **When:** Bootstrapping standard library packages.
- **Then:** `Collection`, `Set`, `Sequence`, `OrderedSet`, and `Bag` resolve in $O(1)$ with exact matrix flags.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Covariant Subtyping & Multiple Inheritance
- **Given:** `Classifier_Alpha <= Classifier_Beta` in `Package_0`.
- **When:** Evaluating subtyping queries.
- **Then:** `OrderedSet<Classifier_Alpha> <= Set<Classifier_Alpha>`, `OrderedSet<Classifier_Alpha> <= Sequence<Classifier_Alpha>`, and `Set<Classifier_Alpha> <= Set<Classifier_Beta>` evaluate `true`.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Subtyping and Element Type Incompatibility
- **Given:** `Set<Classifier_Alpha>` assigned `Set<Classifier_Beta>` with `Classifier_Beta` not specializing `Classifier_Alpha`.
- **When:** Validating subtyping and elements.
- **Then:** Lowering rejects assignment, binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{type}}``$, and emits `E0200`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0200` (Subtyping or element mismatch).

### AC-04: Parameter Directionality and Cycle Rejection
- **Given:** Model with `InOut` parameter or alias cycle `Type_A = Set<Type_B>` and `Type_B = Sequence<Type_A>`.
- **When:** Signature validation and cycle analysis execute.
- **Then:** Rejection emits `E0201` or `E0203`; recovery halts at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0201`, `E0203`, or `E0199`.
