## 1. Normative Statement

The ICD engine of Subsystem 8 SHALL verify Rate Monotonic Scheduling (RMS) schedulability for periodic task sets on interconnect buses.

---

## 2. Formal Invariant

The task parameters, priority assignment, utilization bounds, and RTA recurrence relation satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\forall\tau_i\in\mathcal{T},&\quad C_i,T_i,D_i\in\mathbb{Q}^+\land 0\lt D_i\le T_i\land B_i=\max(\{C_k\mid k\in\text{lp}(i)\}\cup\{0\}) \\
\forall\tau_i,\tau_j\in\mathcal{T},&\quad(T_i\lt T_j\lor(T_i=T_j\land\text{name}_i\prec_{\text{lex}}\text{name}_j))\iff\pi_i\gt\pi_j \\
U=\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{C_i}{T_i},&\quad U_{\text{LL}}(n)=n(2^{1/n}-1),\quad R_i^{(k+1)}=C_i+B_i+\sum_{j\in\text{hp}(i)}\left\lceil\frac{R_i^{(k)}+J_j}{T_j}\right\rceil C_j \\
\text{Sched}(\tau_i)&\iff\left(U\le U_{\text{LL}}(n)\lor\prod_{i=1}^n(U_i+1)\le 2\lor(\exists k\le k_{\max}:R_i^{(k+1)}=R_i^{(k)}\le D_i-J_i)\right) \\
(U\gt 1.0&\lor\exists i:\neg\text{Sched}(\tau_i)\lor C_i\le 0\lor T_i\le 0\lor C_i\gt D_i)\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\tau_i,\text{E0210})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
\text{EndpointFault}&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0200})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}},\quad|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}
\end{aligned}
```

| Symbol / Term | Domain / Formal Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| $``\mathcal{T}, \tau_i``$ | Task set and periodic task $i$ |
| $``C_i, T_i, D_i``$ | Duration, period, and relative deadline |
| $``B_i, J_i, \pi_i``$ | Blocking delay, jitter, and priority |
| $``U, U_{\text{LL}}(n)``$ | Total utilization and Liu & Layland bound |
| $``R_i^{(k)}, k_{\max}``$ | Response-time recurrence and iteration limit |
| $``\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}, C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ | Diagnostic error sink and capacity limit |

---

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(n \log n)$ period sorting with $O(n^2)$ exact RTA ($n = |\mathcal{T}|$, pseudo-polynomial $``O(n \cdot \max_i T_i)``$). Pool: $``O((n \log n + n^2) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$. Space: $O(n)$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** N/A (Deterministic P). Scheduling synchronous flows avoids NP-2 packing (REQ-0147) and NP-8 solving (REQ-0197).

---

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Liu & Layland Verification
- **Given:** `TaskSet_Alpha` with `PeriodicFlow_0` ($C=1, T=4$) and `PeriodicFlow_1` ($C=2, T=10$).
- **When:** Evaluating utilization against $``U_{\text{LL}}(2) \approx 0.8284``$.
- **Then:** Confirms $``U = 0.45 \le U_{\text{LL}}(2)``$ and certifies schedulable.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Exact RTA Convergence
- **Given:** `TaskSet_Alpha` with `PeriodicFlow_0` ($C=2, T=5$) and `PeriodicFlow_1` ($C=3, T=7$) ($U \approx 0.8286$).
- **When:** Executing iterative RTA for `PeriodicFlow_1`.
- **Then:** Recurrence converges to fixed point $``R_1^* = 5 \le 7``$, certifying schedulable.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Schedulability Violation Rejection
- **Given:** `TaskSet_Alpha` with $U \gt 1.0$ or response time $``R_i^* \gt D_i``$.
- **When:** Validating schedulability and deadlines.
- **Then:** Rejects task set, binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{sched}}``$, and emits `E0210`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0210` (SchedulabilityDeadlineViolation).

### AC-04: Endpoint Fault & Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Flow referencing unmapped `Port_1`, separated by `;`.
- **When:** Ingesting model and recovering across delimiters.
- **Then:** Emits `E0200`, resynchronizes, and halts at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0200`, `E0199`.
