## 1. Normative Statement

The compiler SHALL lower SysML v2 satisfy requirement usages (`SatisfyRequirementUsage`, declared via `satisfy requirement` or `satisfy... by...`) as directed realization dependencies linking structural parts or actions to requirements, specializing KerML `Feature`, `Step`, and `Relationship`.

The lowering engine MUST enforce six invariants:
1. **Specialization**: `SatisfyRequirementUsage` specializes `RequirementUsage` and `AssertConstraintUsage`.
2. **Bipartite Domain**: Disjoint sets $``V_E``$ and $``V_R``$ satisfy $``V_E \cap V_R = \emptyset``$. Usages MUST link $``e \in V_E``$ to $``r \in V_R``$; domain violations emit `E0200`.
3. **Irreflexivity**: Self-satisfaction ($e = r$) and inverted satisfaction ($r \xrightarrow{\text{satisfies}} e$) MUST emit `E0200`.
4. **Subject Conformance**: Realization element $e$ MUST type-conform to the requirement subject parameter ($``\tau(e) \le_{\text{sub}} \tau(p_{\text{subject}})``$); violations emit `E0200`.
5. **Contextual Scoping**: Explicit syntax and contextual shorthand inside part blocks SHALL resolve targets and owners. Unresolved identifiers emit `E0100`.
6. **Coverage & Acyclicity**: Bipartite graph $``G_{\text{trace}} = (V_E \cup V_R, E_{\text{sat}})``$ tracks satisfaction. Zero in-degree requirements emit `E0200` under completeness enforcement. Extended cycles in $``G_{\text{sat\_all}}``$ emit `E0203`.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Metamodel specialization, bipartite domain partition, subject subtyping, coverage metric, and acyclicity invariants:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\text{SatisfyUsage} &\le_{\text{sub}} \text{RequirementUsage} \le_{\text{sub}} \text{Feature} \\
\text{SatisfyUsage} &\le_{\text{sub}} \text{AssertConstraintUsage} \le_{\text{sub}} \text{Relationship} \\
V_E \cap V_R &= \emptyset \;\wedge\; E_{\text{sat}} \subseteq V_E \times V_R \\
\forall (e, r) \in E_{\text{sat}}, \quad & e \ne r \;\wedge\; (\tau(p_{\text{subject}}(r)) \ne \top \implies \tau(e) \le_{\text{sub}} \tau(p_{\text{subject}}(r))) \\
\text{Coverage}(V_R) &= \frac{|\{ r \in V_R \mid \exists e \in V_E, (e, r) \in E_{\text{sat}} \}|}{|V_R|} \\
\text{Orphans}(V_R) &= \{ r \in V_R \mid \text{BackwardSat}(r) = \emptyset \} \\
\forall v \in V_{\text{all}}, \quad & (v, v) \notin E_{\text{sat\_all}}^+ \\
\forall (e, r) \in E_{\text{sat}}, \quad & \Delta_{\text{sat}} = \text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(e) \mathbin{\Vert} \text{"\_satisfies\_"} \mathbin{\Vert} \text{FQN}(r))
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

Lowering and traceability verification execute within Complexity Class $\mathbf{P}$.

For an AST model with realization elements $``V_E``$, requirements $``V_R``$, and satisfaction edges $``E_{\text{sat}}``$:
- **Endpoint & Irreflexivity**: Validating bipartite endpoints and $e \ne r$ evaluates in $O(1)$ per edge ($``O(|E_{\text{sat}}|)``$ total).
- **Subject Type Conformance**: Subtyping evaluates in $O(1)$ amortized time via reachability bitsets (NP-3 fragment).
- **Graph Construction & Indexing**: Building $``G_{\text{trace}}``$ and bi-directional index maps executes in deterministic $``O(|V_E| + |V_R| + |E_{\text{sat}}|)``$ time.
- **Coverage & Orphan Detection**: In-degree evaluation and compliance matrix synthesis execute in deterministic $``O(|V_R| + |E_{\text{sat}}|)``$ time.
- **Dependency Acyclicity**: Topological cycle detection over $``G_{\text{sat\_all}}``$ executes in deterministic $``O(|V_{\text{all}}| + |E_{\text{sat\_all}}|)``$ time.

Total sequential complexity is $``O(|V_E| + |V_R| + |E_{\text{sat}}| + |E_{\text{sat\_all}}|)``$. Work-stealing parallel lowering scales as $``O((|V_E| + |V_R| + |E_{\text{sat}}|) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$. Deterministic bounds bypass NP-hard combinatorial search (NP-1, NP-8).

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical Explicit and Contextual Satisfy Lowering
- **Given:** `Req_1 : ReqDef_Alpha` and `Part_Alpha : Classifier_Alpha` in `Package_0`.
- **When:** Lowering `satisfy requirement Req_1 by Part_Alpha;` or contextual `part Part_Alpha { satisfy requirement Req_1; }`.
- **Then:** Edge `(Part_Alpha, Req_1)` enters $``G_{\text{trace}}``$ with populated adjacency maps and empty diagnostic sink.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Subject Classifier Subtyping Conformance Violation
- **Given:** `Req_1` with subject `Classifier_Alpha` and `Part_Beta : Classifier_Beta` ($``\text{Classifier\_Beta} \not\le \text{Classifier\_Alpha}``$).
- **When:** Lowering evaluates subject subtyping.
- **Then:** Lowering rejects the binding, binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{sat}}``$, and emits diagnostic `E0200`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0200` (Subject type mismatch).

### AC-03: Invalid Realization Domain and Reflexive Satisfaction Prohibition
- **Given:** Malformed satisfy declaring self-satisfaction (`satisfy Req_1 by Req_1;`) or non-requirement target (`satisfy Part_Beta by Part_Alpha;`).
- **When:** Bipartite domain disjointness and irreflexivity validation execute.
- **Then:** Lowering halts edge insertion, binds $``\bot_{\text{sat}}``$, and emits diagnostic `E0200`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0200` (Invalid satisfaction endpoint).

### AC-04: Traceability Coverage and Orphan Requirement Detection
- **Given:** Requirements `Req_1` and `Req_2` with only `Req_1` satisfied by `Part_Alpha` under completeness enforcement.
- **When:** Traceability coverage analysis and orphan detection execute.
- **Then:** Coverage evaluates to $``\text{Coverage}(V_R) = 0.5``$, orphan `Req_2` is flagged, and diagnostic `E0200` is emitted.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0200` (Orphan requirement violation).
