## 1. Normative Statement

The compiler SHALL allocate logical signal flows into shared interconnect channels, minimizing matrix cut bandwidth while enforcing multidimensional capacity bounds.

---

## 2. Formal Invariant

Let $\mathcal{A}$ denote signal flows, $\mathcal{C}$ channels, and $``\mathcal{G}_{\text{mux}}``$ the multiplexer routing graph. Structural allocation satisfies:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\forall a_i\in\mathcal{A},\;\mathbf{w}_i\in(\mathbb{Q}^+)^K,\;V(a_i)=\sum_{k=1}^K\frac{w_{ik}}{C_{\text{norm},k}},\;C_{\text{norm},k}=\max_{c_j} C_{jk} \\
\forall c_j\in\mathcal{C},\;\mathbf{C}_j\in(\mathbb{Q}^+)^K,\;\sum_{j\in\mathcal{C}} x_{ij}=1,\;\sum_{i\in\mathcal{A}} w_{ik} x_{ij}\le C_{jk} y_j \\
x_{ij}=1\implies\{\text{Owner}(p_{\text{src}}(a_i)),\text{Owner}(p_{\text{tgt}}(a_i))\}\subseteq\text{Endpoints}(c_j) \\
\text{Cycle}(\mathcal{G}_{\text{mux}})\implies\text{Diagnostic}(C,\text{E0203})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
((\exists k:w_{ik}\gt C_{jk})\lor N_{\text{step}}\gt\kappa_{\text{alloc}})\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\mathcal{A},\text{E0200})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
\forall e,\Delta_e=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(e)),\;|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}\implies\text{Diagnostic}_{\text{term}}(\text{E0199})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}
\end{aligned}
```

| Symbol / Term | Domain / Formal Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| $``\mathcal{A}, \mathbf{w}_i, V(a_i)``$ | Signal flows, demand vector, and scalar volume |
| $``\mathcal{C}, \mathbf{C}_j, C_{\text{norm}, k}``$ | Interconnect channels, capacity, and reference norm |
| $``x_{ij}, y_j``$ | Flow assignment and channel activation variables |
| $``\mathcal{G}_{\text{mux}}, \kappa_{\text{alloc}}``$ | Multiplexer routing graph and solver step cutoff |
| $``\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}, C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ | Diagnostic sink buffer and maximum error capacity |
| $``\Delta_e, \text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}``$ | Deterministic UUIDv5 anchor and namespace OID |

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** NP-hard (ILP) / Class $\mathbf{P}$ (FFD heuristic).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** FFD: $O(|\mathcal{A}| \log |\mathcal{A}| + |\mathcal{A}| \cdot |\mathcal{C}|)$. ILP with cutoff: $``O(\kappa_{\text{alloc}} \cdot (K \cdot |\mathcal{C}| + |\mathcal{A}|))``$. Space: $O(K \cdot |\mathcal{A}| + K \cdot |\mathcal{C}| + |\mathcal{A}| \cdot |\mathcal{C}|)$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** NP-2. FFD bounds approximation: $\text{Cost}(\text{FFD}) \le \frac{11}{9}\text{OPT} + \frac{6}{9}$. ILP branch-and-bound terminates at step cutoff $``\kappa_{\text{alloc}}``$ with FFD fallback.

---

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical FFD Multi-Resource Flow Allocation
- **Given:** `Package_0` declaring 10 flows (`Flow_Alpha`) and 3 channels (`LogicalChannel_0`).
- **When:** Allocation executes under standard FFD mode.
- **Then:** Flows are volume-sorted and packed without capacity violations.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Exact 0-1 ILP & Step Cutoff Fallback
- **Given:** Model where ILP solver exceeds step budget $``\kappa_{\text{alloc}}=100``$.
- **When:** Solver exceeds step cutoff $``\kappa_{\text{alloc}}``$.
- **Then:** Search aborts, falls back to FFD packing, and emits `E0200`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0200` on step cutoff fallback.

### AC-03: Multi-Resource Capacity Infeasibility Rejection
- **Given:** `Package_0` with `Flow_Alpha` exceeding all channel capacities.
- **When:** Channel capacity validation executes.
- **Then:** Allocation halts, binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{alloc}}``$, and emits `E0200`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0200` on capacity violation.

### AC-04: Multiplexing Routing Cycle & Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Circular routing between `Multiplexer_1` and `Multiplexer_2` before `;`.
- **When:** Cycle detection and delimiter recovery execute.
- **Then:** Emits `E0203`, recovers on `;`, and halts at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0203`, `E0199`.
