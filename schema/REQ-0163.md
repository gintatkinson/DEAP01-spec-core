## 1. Normative Statement

## 2. Formal Invariant

The state abstraction quotient map, behavioral reachability, weak bisimulation, scenario step sequence, and diagnostic rules satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\alpha&:\mathcal{S}_{\text{AST}}\twoheadrightarrow S_{\text{macro}},\quad\bigcup_{m\in S_{\text{macro}}}\alpha^{-1}(m)=\mathcal{S}_{\text{AST}} \\
\forall m,m'\in S_{\text{macro}},&\quad m\xrightarrow{\sigma}m'\iff\exists s\in\alpha^{-1}(m),s'\in\alpha^{-1}(m'):s\xRightarrow{\sigma}s' \\
M_{\text{AST}}\sim_{\text{bisim}} M_{\text{epic}}&\iff\forall\langle s,m\rangle\in\mathcal{R}_{\text{bisim}},(\forall\sigma\in\Sigma_{\text{obs}},s\xrightarrow{\sigma}s'\implies\exists m':m\xrightarrow{\sigma}m') \\
\forall t_1,t_2\in\delta_{\text{macro}},&\quad(m_{\text{src}}(t_1)=m_{\text{src}}(t_2)\land e_{t_1}=e_{t_2}\land t_1\ne t_2)\implies(g_{t_1}\land g_{t_2}\equiv\text{false}) \\
\forall i\in\{1,\dots,p-1\},&\quad s_{\text{tgt}}(\text{step}_i)=s_{\text{src}}(\text{step}_{i+1})\land\exists t\in\delta_{\text{macro}}:\text{Matches}(t,\text{step}_i) \\
(\exists t\in\delta_{\text{macro}}:\neg\text{Realized}(t))&\lor\neg(M_{\text{AST}}\sim_{\text{bisim}} M_{\text{epic}})\implies\text{Diagnostic}(t,\text{E0300})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land t\gets\bot_{\text{macro}} \\
(\exists\mathcal{O}:\neg\text{ValidTrace}(\mathcal{O}))&\lor\text{Conflict}(t_1,t_2)\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\mathcal{O},\text{E0305})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land\mathcal{O}\gets\bot_{\text{scenario}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}},\quad\Delta_x=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(x))
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial Time).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $``O(|S| + |T| + |\mathcal{O}_{\text{steps}}|)``$ projection and bisimulation. Work-stealing scales as $``O((|S| + |T|)/N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$. Space is $O(|S| + |T|)$.
- **Tractable Subclass and Frontier:** N/A (Deterministic P). Quotient abstraction collapses orthogonal states, bypassing NP-5 reachability (REQ-0124) and NP-8 solving (REQ-0197).

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical Macro Statechart Projection
- **Given:** State machine in `Package_0` with states `State_0`, `State_1`.
- **When:** Synthesizing macro statechart `MacroStatechart_Alpha`.
- **Then:** Collapses micro-steps, projects transitions, and emits Mermaid `stateDiagram-v2`.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Operational Scenario Step Verification
- **Given:** Path from `State_0` to `State_1` on `Event_0` with guard `Guard_0`.
- **When:** Synthesizing operational scenario `Scenario_0`.
- **Then:** Validates step sequence and verifies disjoint guards.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Reachability and Bisimulation Violation Rejection
- **Given:** Macro transition asserted without a realizing AST path.
- **When:** Verifying reachability and bisimulation.
- **Then:** Fails closed, emits diagnostic `E0300`, and binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{macro}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300` (MacroReachabilityBisimulationViolation).

### AC-04: Inconsistent Scenario Trace and Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Scenario referencing ungrounded `State_Undefined`, delimited by `;`.
- **When:** Executing delimiter recovery.
- **Then:** Emits `E0305`, resynchronizes, and halts at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0305`, `E0199`.
