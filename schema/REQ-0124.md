## 1. Normative Statement

The solver SHALL verify reachability, deadlock freedom, and livelock absence across $K \ge 2$ orthogonal regions via Bounded Model Checking (BMC).

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Cartesian Space**: States define $K$ regions ($``\mathcal{C}_{\text{global}}=\prod_{i=1}^K S_i``$) with exact-one active leaf per region ($``|C\cap\text{Leaves}(R_i)|=1``$). Unique root is $``s_0``$.
2. **SAT Unrolling**: Unrolls transitions into $``\Phi_k``$ ($``k\le k_{\text{max}}``$, default $50$), conjoining $``I(s_0)``$, transitions $``\bigwedge_{t=0}^{k-1} T(s_t,s_{t+1})``$, and violation $``\neg\text{Safe}(s_k)``$.
3. **Transition Relations**: Encodes interleavings, synchronized compound fork/join transitions, and stuttering ($``\mathbf{x}_i^{(t+1)}=\mathbf{x}_i^{(t)}``$).
4. **Deadlock & Livelock**: Deadlocks emit `E0400` with trace $\mathbf{w}$. Non-progress cycles emit `E0400`. Unreachable states emit `E0400`.
5. **Step Budget**: CDCL solver enforces budget $``\kappa_{\text{steps}}``$ ($2\times 10^5$); exceeding $``\kappa_{\text{steps}}``$ emits `E0406`.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Orthogonal state space occupancy, composite transition relations, safety predicates, and diagnostic bounds:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{C}_{\text{global}}=\prod_{i=1}^K S_i,\quad \text{Occupancy}(\mathbf{x}^{(t)})\equiv\bigwedge_{i=1}^K\left(\bigvee_{q\in S_i}x_{i,q}^{(t)}\land\bigwedge_{q\ne q^\prime\in S_i}(\neg x_{i,q}^{(t)}\lor\neg x_{i,q^\prime}^{(t)})\right) \\
I(\mathbf{x}^{(0)})\equiv\text{Occupancy}(\mathbf{x}^{(0)})\land\bigwedge_{i=1}^K\left(x_{i,q_{\text{init},i}}^{(0)}\land\bigwedge_{q\in S_i\setminus\{q_{\text{init},i}\}}\neg x_{i,q}^{(0)}\right) \\
T(\mathbf{x}^{(t)},\mathbf{x}^{(t+1)})\equiv\text{Occupancy}(\mathbf{x}^{(t)})\land\text{Occupancy}(\mathbf{x}^{(t+1)})\land(T_{\text{interleave}}\lor T_{\text{sync}}\lor T_{\text{stutter}}) \\
\text{Safe}(\mathbf{x}^{(t)})\equiv(\text{IsTerminal}(\mathbf{x}^{(t)})\lor\exists\mathbf{x}^{(t+1)},\,T(\mathbf{x}^{(t)},\mathbf{x}^{(t+1)}))\land\neg\text{LivelockCycle}(\mathbf{x}^{(0)},\dots,\mathbf{x}^{(t)}) \\
\Phi_k=I(\mathbf{x}^{(0)})\land\left(\bigwedge_{t=0}^{k-1}T(\mathbf{x}^{(t)},\mathbf{x}^{(t+1)})\right)\land\neg\text{Safe}(\mathbf{x}^{(k)}) \\
\Phi_k\models\text{SAT}\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{Trace}(\mathbf{w}),\text{E0400})\land\mathbf{w}\gets\bot_{\text{trace}} \\
N_{\text{step}}\gt\kappa_{\text{steps}}\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\mathcal{S}_{\text{orth}},\text{E0406})\land\mathcal{S}_{\text{orth}}\gets\bot_{\text{state}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{Compiler},\text{E0199}),\quad \Delta_n=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(n))
\end{aligned}
```

| Symbol / Term | Domain / Formal Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| $``\mathcal{C}_{\text{global}}, S_i``$ | Global configuration space and regional states |
| $``\mathbf{x}^{(t)}, x_{i, q}^{(t)}``$ | Global state vector and occupancy bit |
| $I(\mathbf{x}^{(0)}), T(\mathbf{x}^{(t)}, \mathbf{x}^{(t+1)})$ | Initial predicate and transition relation |
| $``\Phi_k, k_{\text{max}}``$ | Unrolled BMC formula and depth bound |
| $\text{Safe}, \text{LivelockCycle}$ | Safety invariant and cyclic livelock |
| $``N_{\text{step}}, \kappa_{\text{steps}}``$ | Step counter and step budget limit |
| $``\bot_{\text{state}}, \bot_{\text{trace}}, \Delta_n``$ | Error sentinels and UUIDv5 identifier |

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** $\mathbf{PSPACE}\text{-complete}$ (general) / $\mathbf{NP}\text{-complete}$ (BMC depth $k$).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O((N/K)^K)$ state space ($``N = \sum |S_i|``$); $O(k(N^2 + M))$ encoding ($M = |\mathcal{T}|$); $``O(\kappa_{\text{steps}} \cdot k(N^2 + M))``$ bounded SAT solving.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** BMC depth $``k \le k_{\text{max}}``$ anchors NP-5 under budget $``\kappa_{\text{steps}}``$. Work-stealing scales as $``O(T / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: BMC Unrolling & Reachability Witness
- **Given:** `CompositeState_Alpha` with 2 regions (3 states each), target reachable at $k = 3$ ($``k_{\text{max}} = 10``$).
- **When:** Unrolling transitions into $``\Phi_k``$ and solving SAT.
- **Then:** Returns SAT at $k = 3$, extracting witness $``\langle s_0, s_1, s_2, s_3 \rangle``$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Bounded Horizon Cutoff & Stuttering
- **Given:** `CompositeState_Alpha` requiring 60 steps ($``k_{\text{max}} = 50``$), with `Region_1` active and `Region_2` stuttering.
- **When:** Unrolling reaches horizon $``k_{\text{max}}``$.
- **Then:** Halts at depth 50, asserting $``\mathbf{x}_2^{(t+1)} = \mathbf{x}_2^{(t)}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Deadlock and Livelock Rejection
- **Given:** Model where `Region_1` and `Region_2` enter deadlocks or non-progress cycles.
- **When:** Evaluating $``\Phi_k``$ with safety violation $``\neg \text{Safe}(s_k)``$.
- **Then:** Detects SAT at $``k \le k_{\text{max}}``$, emits `E0400`, binding trace to $``\bot_{\text{trace}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` (OrthogonalStateDeadlockDetected).

### AC-04: Step Budget Cutoff & Recovery
- **Given:** Model exceeding budget $``\kappa_{\text{steps}} = 100``$ before delimiter (`;`), followed by `CompositeState_Beta`.
- **When:** Step count exceeds budget ($``N_{\text{step}} \gt \kappa_{\text{steps}}``$).
- **Then:** Emits `E0406`, binds $``\bot_{\text{state}}``$, recovers on `;`, halting at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0406`, `E0199`.
