## 1. Normative Statement

The compiler SHALL verify that outgoing transitions from any state on identical events have mutually disjoint guards ($``G_1 \wedge G_2 \equiv \text{false}``$), ensuring determinism.

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Trigger Partitioning**: Outgoing transitions from `State_Alpha` partition by trigger `Event_0` (or $\epsilon$). If $``|\mathcal{T}_{s, e}| \ge 2``$, checks execute across all $``\binom{|\mathcal{T}_{s, e}|}{2}``$ pairs.
2. **Mutual Exclusion**: Transitions `Transition_Alpha`, `Transition_Beta` on `Event_0` require $``\text{UNSAT}(\overline{G}_1 \land \overline{G}_2)``$; overlap or missing guards ($\text{true}$) emit `E0400`.
3. **Theory Admissibility**: Guards MUST evaluate to `Boolean` in decidable fragments (`PROP`, `QF_LRA`, `QF_LIA`). Non-linear terms or invalid types emit `E0402`.
4. **Bounded SMT Solving**: Solvers operate under budget $``\kappa_{\text{smt}} = 100{,}000``$; exhaustion emits `E0400`. Interval checks discharge 1D bounds in $O(1)$.
5. **Hierarchy Precedence**: Differing depths follow inner-first priority; sibling transitions at identical depth sharing `Event_0` MUST be disjoint, emitting `E0400`.

Partitioning, guard normalization, mutual exclusion, and solver bounds:

\forall s\in\mathcal{S},\;\forall e\in\mathcal{E}(s),\;\forall t_1,t_2\in\mathcal{T}_{s,e},\;t_1\ne t_2\implies\text{UNSAT}(\overline{G}(t_1)\land\overline{G}(t_2)) \\

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{NP}\text{-complete}$ (SMT) / Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Syntactic Fragment)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $``O(|\mathcal{T}| + K^2 \cdot T_{\text{smt}}(\kappa_{\text{smt}}))``$ for $K \le 8$ and step limit $``\kappa_{\text{smt}} = 100{,}000``$; interval checks take $O(1)$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Aligned with NP-2 (Bin Packing / SMT Partitioning). Linear fragments evaluate under budget $``\kappa_{\text{smt}}``$; non-linear forms emit `E0402`. Work-stealing scales as $``O((|\mathcal{S}| + |\mathcal{T}|) / N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Disjoint Guard Verification & Happy Path
- **Given:** `State_Alpha` with `Transition_Alpha` (`param_x < 0.0`) and `Transition_Beta` (`param_x >= 0.0`) on `Event_0`.
- **When:** Lowering verifies guard disjointness.
- **Then:** Disjointness is certified via interval checks.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Non-Disjoint Transition Guard Rejection
- **Given:** Sibling `Transition_Alpha` (`param_x >= 10.0`) and `Transition_Beta` (`param_x <= 20.0`) on `Event_0`.
- **When:** SMT solver evaluates $``\overline{G}_1 \land \overline{G}_2``$.
- **Then:** Conjunction is satisfiable (`param_x = 15.0`), emitting `E0400` with counterexample.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` (NonDisjointTransitionGuards).

### AC-03: Solver Step Limit Bound Enforcement
- **Given:** Guard conjunction requiring steps exceeding budget $``\kappa_{\text{smt}} = 100{,}000``$.
- **When:** SMT solver executes.
- **Then:** Halts deterministically without panicking, emitting `E0400`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` (GuardSolverStepLimitExceeded).

### AC-04: Non-Admissible Theory & Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Guard `param_x * param_y > 1.0` before `;`, then valid transition.
- **When:** Admissibility checking and recovery execute.
- **Then:** Emits `E0402`, binds $``\bot_{\text{guard}}``$, recovers on `;`, halting at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0402`, `E0199`.
