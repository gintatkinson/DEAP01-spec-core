## 1. Normative Statement

The network solver SHALL synthesize generalized Kirchhoff Flow Law (KFL) continuity equations at conservative junctions.

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Flow Continuity**: Ideal junctions $``J_i \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{ideal}}``$ require $``\sum \sigma_{i, e} c_e f_e(t) = 0``$ ($``\sigma_{i, e} \in \{+1, -1\}``$, $``c_e \in \mathbb{Q}^+``$), giving $``\mathbf{A}_{\text{ideal}} \mathbf{C}_{\text{unit}} \mathbf{f}(t) = \mathbf{0}``$.
2. **Dynamic Storage**: Accumulating junctions $``J_i \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{accum}}``$ satisfy $``\sum \sigma_{i, e} c_e f_e(t) = \frac{d S_i}{dt}(t)``$ with $``\mathbf{d}(S_i) \equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}} + [T]^1``$; mismatches emit `E0300`.
3. **Homogeneity**: Incident branches require $``\mathbf{d}(e_a) \equiv \mathbf{d}(e_b)``$ in $\mathbb{Q}^7$ (mismatches emit `E0300`); units scale via $``c_e \in \mathbb{Q}^+``$.
4. **Rank Defect**: Components $``\mathcal{K}_c``$ have rank defect 1 ($``\text{rank}(\mathbf{A}_c) = |\mathcal{V}_c| - 1``$); datum elimination yields full-rank $``\mathbf{A}_{\text{reduced}}``$.
5. **Static Balance**: Imbalance emits `E0301`. Dangling degree-1 junctions with flow emit `E0220` or `E0301`.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Topological incidence, flow continuity, dynamic accumulation, dimensional consistency, and diagnostic predicates:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{G}_{\text{flow}}=(\mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}},\mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}}),\quad A_{i,e}\in\{+1,-1,0\},\quad \mathbf{A}_{\text{ideal}}\mathbf{C}_{\text{unit}}\mathbf{f}(t)=\mathbf{0} \\
\forall J_i\in\mathcal{V}_{\text{accum}},\;\sum_{e\in\text{Incident}(J_i)}A_{i,e}c_e f_e(t)=\frac{d S_i}{dt}(t),\quad \mathbf{d}\left(\frac{d S_i}{dt}\right)\equiv\mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}(J_i) \\
\mathbf{d}(e_a)\not\equiv\mathbf{d}(e_b)\implies\text{Diagnostic}(J_i,\text{E0300})\wedge\mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}(J_i)=\bot_{\text{dim}} \\
\forall\mathcal{K}_c\subseteq\mathcal{G}_{\text{flow}},\;\text{rank}(\mathbf{A}_c)=|\mathcal{V}_c|-1\implies\text{rank}(\mathbf{A}_{\text{reduced}})=|\mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}|-|\mathcal{K}| \\
(J_i\in\mathcal{V}_{\text{ideal}}\wedge|\sum A_{i,e}c_e f_e|\gt 0)\implies\text{Diagnostic}(J_i,\text{E0301}) \\
(|\text{Incident}(J_i)|=1\wedge f_e\ne 0)\implies\text{Diagnostic}(J_i,\text{E0220}),\quad \Delta_J=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(J))
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(V + E)$ for incidence assembly, dimensional checks, component traversal, and constraint AST synthesis; $O(E)$ for static flow balance evaluation.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Linear formulation avoids exponential cut-set enumeration (NP-4). Parallel work-stealing scales as $``O((V + E) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Multi-Branch Ideal Junction Flow Balance
- **Given:** Junction `Junction_Alpha` with incoming `Flow_Edge_1`, `Flow_Edge_2`, `Flow_Edge_3` and outgoing `Flow_Edge_4`, `Flow_Edge_5`.
- **When:** Lowering Kirchhoff flow continuity.
- **Then:** Emits $``f_1 + f_2 + f_3 - f_4 - f_5 = 0``$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Commensurable Unit Conversion and Dynamic Accumulation
- **Given:** Dynamic junction `Junction_Beta` ($``S_i:[M]^1``$), incoming `Flow_Edge_1` (g/s, $``c_1=1/1000``$), outgoing `Flow_Edge_2` (kg/s, $``c_2=1``$).
- **When:** Lowering dynamic flow continuity.
- **Then:** Synthesizes $``(1/1000) f_1 - f_2 = \frac{d(\text{param\_s})}{dt}``$ in $[M]^1 [T]^{-1}$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Incommensurable Dimensional Mismatch Rejection
- **Given:** Junction `Junction_Alpha` connecting `Flow_Edge_1` ($[I]^1$) and `Flow_Edge_2` ($[L]^3 [T]^{-1}$).
- **When:** Dimensional checking executes.
- **Then:** Rejects junction, binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{junc}}``$, emitting `E0300`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300` (DimensionalMismatch).

### AC-04: Static Flow Balance Violation and Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Junction `Junction_Alpha` with flow imbalance (in: $10$, out: $8$) before delimiter `;` and valid `Junction_Beta`.
- **When:** Balance checking and delimiter recovery execute.
- **Then:** Emits `E0301`, resynchronizes on `;`, parsing `Junction_Beta`, emitting `E0199` at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0301`, `E0199`.
