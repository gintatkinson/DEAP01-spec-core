## 1. Normative Statement

The verification engine SHALL enforce a Verification Witness Completeness Gate (`WitnessGate_Alpha`) across the traceability graph, asserting that all requirements are witnessed.

The compiler MUST enforce:
1. **Witness Coverage**: Every $``r \in \mathcal{R}_{\text{all}}``$ MUST link to at least one witness $``w \in \mathcal{W}_{\text{verification}}``$ via $``(r, w) \in E_{\text{witness}}``$. Unwitnessed requirements ($``\text{deg}_{E_{\text{witness}}}(r) = 0``$) emit `E0407`.
2. **Method Rigor**: Witnesses declare $``\kappa_{\text{method}}(w) \in \{ \text{Test}, \text{Analysis}, \text{Inspection}, \text{Demonstration} \}``$. Safety invariant requirements in Tier 5 require `Test` or `Analysis`; other methods emit `E0400`.
3. **Reference Integrity**: Edges $(r, w)$ MUST resolve to defined entities. Dangling targets emit `E0400`.
4. **Verdict Certification**: Certification requires $\ge 1$ `Pass` and zero `Fail` verdicts; `Fail` verdicts emit `E0400`.
5. **Hierarchical Rollup**: Composite requirements satisfy completeness directly or via conjunction across all leaf descendants.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Verification witness completeness, degree constraints, certification semantics, and diagnostic mappings satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{R}_{\text{all}}\subseteq V_{\text{trace}},\quad \mathcal{W}_{\text{verification}}\subseteq V_{\text{trace}},\quad E_{\text{witness}}\subseteq\mathcal{R}_{\text{all}}\times\mathcal{W}_{\text{verification}} \\
\forall r\in\mathcal{R}_{\text{all}},\quad \text{deg}_{E_{\text{witness}}}(r)=|\{w\in\mathcal{W}_{\text{verification}}\mid(r,w)\in E_{\text{witness}}\}|\ge 1 \\
\forall r\in\mathcal{R}_{\text{safety}},\quad (r,w)\in E_{\text{witness}}\implies\kappa_{\text{method}}(w)\in\{\text{Test},\text{Analysis}\} \\
\text{Certified}(r)\iff(\exists w:(r,w)\in E_{\text{witness}}\land\nu(w)=\text{Pass})\land(\forall w':(r,w')\in E_{\text{witness}}\implies\nu(w')\ne\text{Fail}) \\
\text{deg}_{E_{\text{witness}}}(r)=0\implies\text{Diagnostic}(r,\text{E0407})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land r\gets\bot_{\text{req}} \\
(r\notin\mathcal{R}_{\text{all}}\lor\nu(w)=\text{Fail})\implies\text{Diagnostic}(w,\text{E0400})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land w\gets\bot_{\text{witness}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199}),\quad \Delta_x=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(x))
\end{aligned}
```

| :--- | :--- |
| $``\mathcal{R}_{\text{all}}, \mathcal{W}_{\text{verification}}``$ | Requirement and witness sets in $``V_{\text{trace}}``$ |
| $``E_{\text{witness}}, \text{deg}_{E_{\text{witness}}}``$ | Bipartite verification edge set and out-degree |

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $``O(|V_{\text{req}}| + |E_{\text{witness}}|)``$ witness coverage traversal over requirements $``|V_{\text{req}}|``$ and edges $``|E_{\text{witness}}|``$. Degree evaluation takes $``O(|E_{\text{witness}}|)``$; audit takes $``O(|V_{\text{req}}|)``$. Work-stealing: $``O((|V_{\text{req}}| + |E_{\text{witness}}|) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Bypasses NP-4 (REQ-0135) and NP-8 (REQ-0197) by auditing concrete bipartite incidence rather than proof obligations.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical Requirement Witness Binding & Certification
- **Given:** `Package_0` declaring `Requirement_0` and witness `Witness_1` (`Test`, verdict `Pass`).
- **When:** Executing `WitnessGate_Alpha` on `(Requirement_0, Witness_1)`.
- **Then:** Validates verification edge and marks `Requirement_0` certified.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Hierarchical Decomposition Witness Rollup
- **Given:** Composite `Requirement_0` decomposed into leaves `Requirement_1` and `Requirement_2`, each bound to a `Pass` witness.
- **When:** Evaluating leaf witness coverage and hierarchical conjunction rollup.
- **Then:** Full leaf coverage is verified and certified status propagates to `Requirement_0`.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Missing Verification Witness Rejection
- **Given:** Model declaring `Requirement_0` with zero edges ($``\text{deg}_{E_{\text{witness}}}(\text{Requirement\_0}) = 0``$).
- **When:** Auditing requirements against `WitnessGate_Alpha`.
- **Then:** Rejects compilation, emits `E0407` for `Requirement_0`, and binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{req}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0407` (UnwitnessedRequirement).

### AC-04: Dangling Witness Target & Synchronization Token Recovery
- **Given:** `Witness_1` referencing undefined `Requirement_Dangling` before `;`, followed by a valid pair.
- **When:** Validating targets and executing delimiter recovery.
- **Then:** Emits `E0400`, binds $``\bot_{\text{witness}}``$, recovers on `;`, halting at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400`, `E0199`.
