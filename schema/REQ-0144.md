## 1. Normative Statement

The compiler SHALL implement a declarative pluggable regulatory safety profiles engine defining severity scales, required attributes, checklists, and rules without mutating core AST schemas.

The pipeline MUST enforce five rules:
1. **Pluggable Architecture**: The compiler SHALL ingest profile $\mathcal{P}$ (`SafetyProfile_Alpha`) with scale $``\mathcal{S}_{\text{levels}}``$ (`SeverityScale_0`), attributes $``\mathcal{A}_{\text{required}}``$, checklists $``\mathcal{C}_{\text{checklists}}``$ (`Checklist_1`), and rules $``\mathcal{R}_{\text{rules}}``$. Unregistered profiles emit `E0400`.
2. **Metamodel Invariance**: Profile binding SHALL preserve core AST structures ($\text{Metamodel}(\mathcal{A} \mid \mathcal{P}) = \text{Metamodel}(\mathcal{A})$). Schema mutations emit `E0400`.
3. **Severity Ordering**: Profiles SHALL enforce strict total order $``s_i \prec_{\mathcal{S}} s_{i+1}``$ with rank $``\text{rank}(s_i) = i``$. Cyclic orders emit `E0400`.
4. **Monotonic Attributes**: Attributes SHALL be level-monotonic ($``s_a \prec_{\mathcal{S}} s_b \implies \mathcal{A}_{\text{required}}(\kappa, s_a) \subseteq \mathcal{A}_{\text{required}}(\kappa, s_b)``$). Missing attributes emit `E0400`.
5. **Checklists & Composition**: Verification gates $``\mathcal{C}_{\text{checklists}}``$ at threshold $``s_{\text{thresh}}``$ require evidence (`Witness_0`). Inconsistent compositions emit `E0400`.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Let $``\mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}``$ denote AST entities and $``\mathcal{E}_{\mathcal{P}} \subseteq \mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}``$ denote entities bound to $\mathcal{P}$. The engine satisfies:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{P} &= \langle \text{id}_{\mathcal{P}}, \mathcal{S}_{\text{levels}}, \prec_{\mathcal{S}}, \mathcal{A}_{\text{required}}, \mathcal{C}_{\text{checklists}}, \mathcal{R}_{\text{rules}}, \Delta_{\mathcal{P}} \rangle \\
\text{Metamodel}(\mathcal{A} \mid \mathcal{P}) &= \text{Metamodel}(\mathcal{A}) \\
s_a \prec_{\mathcal{S}} s_b &\iff \text{rank}(s_a) \lt \text{rank}(s_b) \\
s_a \prec_{\mathcal{S}} s_b &\implies \mathcal{A}_{\text{required}}(\kappa, s_a) \subseteq \mathcal{A}_{\text{required}}(\kappa, s_b) \\
\alpha \in \mathcal{A}_{\text{required}}(\kappa, s) &\implies \alpha \in \text{Attributes}(e) \land \text{Value}(e, \alpha) \ne \bot \\
\text{rank}(s_e) \ge \text{rank}(s_{\text{thresh}}) &\implies \exists w \in \text{Witnesses}(e) : \text{Satisfies}(w, c) \\
\forall r \in \mathcal{R}_{\text{rules}}, \, \phi_r(e) &\equiv \text{true}, \quad \forall x, \, \Delta_x = \text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(x)) \\
\text{Fault}(x) \implies \text{Diagnostic}(x, \text{E0400}) &\in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}, \quad |\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}| \ge C_{\text{max\_errors}} \implies \text{Diagnostic}_{\text{term}}(\text{E0199}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}
\end{aligned}
```

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial Time).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(|\text{Rules}| \cdot |\text{Artifacts}|)$ for $``|\text{Rules}| = |\mathcal{R}_{\text{rules}}| + |\mathcal{C}_{\text{checklists}}| + |\mathcal{A}_{\text{required}}|``$ and $``|\text{Artifacts}| = |\mathcal{E}_{\mathcal{P}}|``$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** N/A (Deterministic P). Bypasses NP frontiers via hash-indexed attribute tables and linear predicate checks. Parallel pool uses $``N_{\text{max\_workers}}``$ threads.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical Profile Ingestion
- **Given:** Profile `SafetyProfile_Alpha` with levels `Level_0` through `Level_3`.
- **When:** The compiler ingests the profile and computes severity ranks.
- **Then:** Order $``s_0 \prec_{\mathcal{S}} \dots \prec_{\mathcal{S}} s_3``$ is verified and ranks assigned.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Monotonic Attribute Schema
- **Given:** Model `Package_0` with `Classifier_Alpha` at `Level_2` omitting `Attribute_0`.
- **When:** Attribute completeness validation executes.
- **Then:** Lowering of `Classifier_Alpha` halts with diagnostic `E0400`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` on missing required attribute.

### AC-03: Verification Checklist Gate
- **Given:** Model `Package_0` with `Hazard_0` at `Level_3` lacking witness `Witness_0`.
- **When:** Checklist gates and profile rules $``\mathcal{R}_{\text{rules}}``$ are evaluated.
- **Then:** Lowering halts with diagnostic `E0400`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` on unsatisfied checklist gate.

### AC-04: Delimiter Error Recovery
- **Given:** Malformed profile declarations separated by delimiters (`;`, `}`).
- **When:** The validator encounters syntax or semantic faults.
- **Then:** Errors accumulate in $``\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}``$, halting at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0199` on buffer capacity.
