## 1. Normative Statement

The safety compiler SHALL construct invariants as typed `ConstraintExpr` AST nodes and lower them to canonical CNF via De Morgan normalization and bounded Tseitin transformation.

Pipeline rules:
1. **AST Nodes**: Allocates `ConstraintExpr` nodes (`ConstraintExpr_Alpha`) on terms (`param_x : Real`). Type errors emit `E0400`.
2. **De Morgan**: Rewrites $\implies, \iff, \oplus$ to $\neg, \land, \lor$. Pushes negations to leaves ($\neg(A \land B) \rightsquigarrow \neg A \lor \neg B$), drops double negations ($\neg\neg A \rightsquigarrow A$), and inverts duals ($``\neg(t_1 \lt t_2) \rightsquigarrow t_1 \ge t_2``$).
3. **CNF Lowering**: Distributes into clauses (`Clause_0`). Overflowing $``K_{\text{dist\_max}} = 256``$ triggers Tseitin transformations, enforcing linear $``O(|N_{\text{expr}}|)``$ clauses.
4. **Simplification**: Prunes tautologies ($l \lor \neg l$). Contradictions ($l \land \neg l$) emit `E0400`. Subsumes clauses ($``C_1 \subseteq C_2 \implies C_1``$).
5. **Sorting**: Sorts literals (`Literal_0`, `Literal_1`) and clauses lexicographically for thread parity.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Invariant algebra, De Morgan normalization, CNF lowering, and diagnostics satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
e ::= \text{LitBool}(b) \mid \text{Atom}(p) \mid \text{RelOp}(\circ,t_1,t_2) \mid \text{Not}(e) \mid \text{And}(e_i) \mid \text{Or}(e_i) \\
\mathcal{T}_{\text{elim}}(\text{Implies}(A,B))=\text{Or}(\text{Not}(A),B),\quad \mathcal{T}_{\text{NNF}}(\text{Not}(\text{Not}(A)))=A \\
\mathcal{T}_{\text{NNF}}(\text{Not}(\text{And}(e_i)))=\bigvee_{i=1}^k\mathcal{T}_{\text{NNF}}(\text{Not}(e_i)),\quad \mathcal{T}_{\text{NNF}}(\text{Not}(\text{Or}(e_i)))=\bigwedge_{i=1}^k\mathcal{T}_{\text{NNF}}(\text{Not}(e_i)) \\
\mathcal{T}_{\text{NNF}}(\text{Not}(\text{RelOp}(\lt,t_1,t_2)))=\text{RelOp}(\ge,t_1,t_2),\quad \mathcal{T}_{\text{NNF}}(\text{Not}(\text{RelOp}(=,t_1,t_2)))=\text{RelOp}(\ne,t_1,t_2) \\
\phi_{\text{CNF}}=\bigwedge_{i=1}^m C_i=\bigwedge_{i=1}^m\left(\bigvee_{j=1}^{k_i} l_{ij}\right),\quad K_{\text{proj}}\gt K_{\text{dist\_max}}\implies |\text{Tseitin}(\phi)|\le 3|N_{\text{expr}}| \\
(l\lor\neg l)\equiv\text{true},\quad (\emptyset\in\phi\lor(l\land\neg l\in\phi))\implies\phi_{\text{CNF}}\equiv\text{false} \\
(\phi_{\text{CNF}}\equiv\text{false}\lor\phi_{\text{CNF}}\equiv\text{true}\lor\tau(e)\ne\mathbb{B})\implies\text{Diagnostic}(e,\text{E0400})\land e\gets\bot_{\text{expr}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199}),\quad\Delta_e=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(e)\mathbin{\Vert}\text{StructuralHash}(e))
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial Time); SAT is $\mathbf{NP}\text{-complete}$.
- **Asymptotic Bound:** De Morgan pushing takes $``O(|N_{\text{expr}}|)``$. Tseitin transformation emits $``\le 3|N_{\text{expr}}|``$ clauses in $``O(|N_{\text{expr}}|)``$ time. Clause sorting: $O(m \log m \cdot L)$. Work-stealing: $``O((|N_{\text{expr}}| \log |N_{\text{expr}}|) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Frontier **NP-2** (Structural Allocation & Tractable Partitioning). Linear Tseitin CNF prevents $O(2^N)$ explosion, separating AST lowering from NP-8 (REQ-0197) and NP-4 (REQ-0135).

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical AST De Morgan Normalization
- **Given:** `Package_0` with `ConstraintExpr_Alpha` over `param_x : Real`.
- **When:** Lowering elimination and De Morgan normalization.
- **Then:** Emits canonical NNF AST with zero diagnostics.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Bounded Distribution & Tseitin Fallback
- **Given:** Clauses in `Clause_0` with $``K_{\text{proj}} > K_{\text{dist\_max}} = 256``$.
- **When:** Evaluating expansion threshold.
- **Then:** Emits linear $``O(|N_{\text{expr}}|)``$ clauses via Tseitin transformation.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Contradictory Invariant Rejection
- **Given:** `Invariant_1` with contradictory clauses ($l \land \neg l$) or vacuous $\text{true}$.
- **When:** Validating and simplifying invariant AST.
- **Then:** Rejects formula, emits `E0400`, and binds $``\bot_{\text{expr}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` (UnsatisfiableSafetyInvariant).

### AC-04: Delimiter Error Recovery
- **Given:** Malformed expressions separated by delimiters (`;`, `}`).
- **When:** Parser encounters syntax errors or invalid connectives.
- **Then:** Emits `E0400`, recovers on delimiter, and halts at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400`, `E0199`.
