## 1. Normative Statement

The formal safety compiler SHALL enforce an upward allocation completeness gate (`AllocationGate_Alpha`) ensuring every system requirement allocates to architecture or safety invariants.

Verification MUST enforce:
1. **Target Admissibility**: Allocation edge $``e_{\text{alloc}} = (r, e)``$ MUST connect $``r \in V_{\text{req}}``$ (`RequirementNode_0`) to $``e \in \mathcal{E}_{\text{target}} = T_3 \cup T_4 \cup T_5``$ (`Component_1`, `Port_1`, `Invariant_Safe_0`); external targets emit `E0400`.
2. **Completeness & Coverage**: Each $``r \in V_{\text{req}}``$ requires $|\text{Alloc}(r)| \ge 1$ (faults emit `E0400`). Global coverage $``\mu_{\text{alloc}}(V_{\text{req}})``$ MUST equal $1.0$; deficits emit `E0404`.
3. **Hierarchical Completeness**: Decomposed requirements require parent allocation ($|\text{Alloc}(r)| \ge 1$) or leaf allocation ($\forall c \in \text{Leaves}(r), |\text{Alloc}(c)| \ge 1$); unallocated leaves emit `E0400`.
4. **Irreflexivity & Acyclicity**: Allocations MUST be irreflexive ($``(r, r) \notin E_{\text{alloc}}``$) and acyclic over $``E_{\text{alloc}} \cup E_{\text{decomp}}``$; cycles emit `E0400`.
5. **Multi-Cardinality**: Supports 1-to-N and N-to-1 allocation mappings deterministically.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Upward allocation completeness, target validity, coverage metrics, and diagnostic triggers satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
G_{\text{trace}}=\langle V_{\text{trace}},E_{\text{trace}}\rangle,\quad V_{\text{req}}=T_2,\quad \mathcal{E}_{\text{target}}=T_3\cup T_4\cup T_5 \\
E_{\text{alloc}}=\{(r,e)\in E_{\text{trace}}\mid r\in V_{\text{req}}\land e\in\mathcal{E}_{\text{target}}\},\quad \text{Alloc}(r)=\{e\in\mathcal{E}_{\text{target}}\mid (r,e)\in E_{\text{alloc}}\} \\
\forall r\in V_{\text{req}},\;|\text{Alloc}(r)|\ge 1\land \mu_{\text{alloc}}(V_{\text{req}})=\frac{|\{r\in V_{\text{req}}\mid |\text{Alloc}(r)|\ge 1\}|}{|V_{\text{req}}|}=1.0 \\
\forall r\in V_{\text{req}},\;(|\text{Alloc}(r)|\ge 1)\lor(\forall c\in\text{Leaves}(r),\,|\text{Alloc}(c)|\ge 1) \\
\forall (r,e)\in E_{\text{alloc}},\;r\ne e\land\neg\text{Reachable}(e,r,E_{\text{alloc}}\cup E_{\text{decomp}}) \\
(|\text{Alloc}(r)|=0\lor e\notin\mathcal{E}_{\text{target}}\lor (r,r)\in E_{\text{alloc}})\implies\text{Diagnostic}(u,\text{E0400})\land u\gets\bot_{\text{alloc}} \\
(\mu_{\text{alloc}}(V_{\text{req}})\lt 1.0)\implies\text{Diagnostic}(V_{\text{req}},\text{E0404}),\quad |\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199}) \\
\forall (r,e)\in E_{\text{alloc}},\;\Delta_{(r,e)}=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(r)\mathbin{\Vert}\text{"::"}\mathbin{\Vert}\text{FQN}(e))
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial Time)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $``O(|V_{\text{req}}| + |E_{\text{alloc}}|)``$ check; leaf search takes $``O(|V_{\text{req}}| + |E_{\text{decomp}}|)``$; work-stealing: $``O((|V_{\text{req}}| + |E_{\text{alloc}}|) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Polynomial reachability; avoids NP-4 (REQ-0135) and NP-2 (REQ-0147) by checking declared edges in linear time.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical Requirement-to-Element Allocation
- **Given:** `Package_0` declaring `RequirementNode_0`, `Component_1`, and `Invariant_Safe_0`.
- **When:** `AllocationGate_Alpha` verifies allocation edges.
- **Then:** Records edges in $``E_{\text{alloc}}``$, confirms $``\mu_{\text{alloc}} = 1.0``$, and succeeds.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink empty.

### AC-02: Hierarchical Decomposition Leaf Completeness
- **Given:** Composite `Requirement_Parent` decomposed into leaves `Requirement_Leaf_0` and `Requirement_Leaf_1` allocated to `Component_1` and `Component_2`.
- **When:** `AllocationGate_Alpha` verifies leaf completeness.
- **Then:** Confirms complete leaf allocation, satisfying upward coverage.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink empty.

### AC-03: Unallocated Requirement & Invalid Target Rejection
- **Given:** Unallocated `RequirementNode_0` ($|\text{Alloc}| = 0$) or allocation targeting `Package_1`.
- **When:** `AllocationGate_Alpha` checks out-degrees and target tiers.
- **Then:** Emits `E0400` rejecting the orphan requirement or invalid target.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` (UnallocatedSystemRequirement).

### AC-04: Allocation Coverage Deficit & Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Coverage deficit ($``\mu_{\text{alloc}} < 1.0``$) and malformed statement before `;`.
- **When:** Validating upward coverage and parsing declarations.
- **Then:** Emits `E0404`, binds $``\bot_{\text{alloc}}``$, recovers on `;`, halting at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0404`, `E0199`.
