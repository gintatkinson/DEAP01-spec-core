## 1. Normative Statement

The compiler SHALL synthesize Port Definition Rosters and bind port types for Level 1C Interface Control Documents (ICD), indexing declarations across hierarchies.

---

## 2. Formal Invariant

Let $``\mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}``$ denote AST entities, $``\mathcal{C}_{\text{comp}}``$ component classifiers, and $``\mathcal{P} = \mathcal{P}_{\text{def}} \cup \mathcal{P}_{\text{usage}}``$ declared ports. Roster synthesis satisfies:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{R}_{\text{ports}} &= \{ \langle p, \text{owner}(p), \text{dir}(p), \tau(p), \pi(p), \text{is\_conjugated}(p), \Delta_p \rangle \mid p \in \mathcal{P} \} \\
p_{\text{child}} \in \text{features}(p_{\text{parent}}) &\implies \text{owner}(p_{\text{child}}) = p_{\text{parent}} \land \text{owner}^k(p) \in \mathcal{C}_{\text{comp}} \\
\text{is\_conjugated}(p) &\implies \text{dir}(p) = {\sim}\text{dir}(\text{base\_def}(p)) \land \tau(p) \cong \tau(\text{base\_def}(p)) \\
{\sim}(\text{In}) = \text{Out}, &\quad {\sim}(\text{Out}) = \text{In}, \quad {\sim}(\text{InOut}) = \text{InOut} \\
r_a \prec_{\text{lex}} r_b &\iff \text{FQN}(r_a) \lt \text{FQN}(r_b), \quad \Delta_p = \text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(p)) \\
(\tau(p) = \bot \lor \langle p, p \rangle \in \text{owner}^+) &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(p, \text{E0200}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
\text{Conflict}(p) \implies \text{Diagnostic}(p, \text{E0205}) &\in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}, \quad |\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}| \ge C_{\text{max\_errors}} \implies \text{Diagnostic}_{\text{term}}(\text{E0199}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}
\end{aligned}
```

| Symbol / Term | Domain / Formal Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| $``\mathcal{P}_{\text{def}}, \mathcal{P}_{\text{usage}}``$ | Port definitions and component usages |
| $``\mathcal{R}_{\text{ports}}, \Delta_p``$ | Port roster relation and UUIDv5 anchor |
| $\text{owner}(p), \text{dir}(p)$ | Containment mapping and directionality |
| $\tau(p), \pi(p)$ | Structural type and protocol contract |
| ${\sim}, D$ | Conjugation involution and depth bound |

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial Time).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(|P| \cdot D)$ for roster generation ($``|P| = |\mathcal{P}_{\text{def}}| + |\mathcal{P}_{\text{usage}}|``$, depth $D \ll |P|$); sorting requires $O(|P| \log |P|)$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** N/A (Deterministic P). Bypasses NP frontiers via pre-indexed ownership chains. Parallel pool uses $``N_{\text{max\_workers}}``$ threads.

---

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical Roster Synthesis & Type Binding
- **Given:** Model `Package_0` with `PortDef_0` and `Port_Alpha`.
- **When:** Roster generation executes.
- **Then:** Ports are cataloged in `Roster_0` with resolved types and lexicographical ordering.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Hierarchical Nested Port Resolution
- **Given:** Component `Classifier_1` declaring `Port_Parent` with child `Port_Child`.
- **When:** Containment traversal executes.
- **Then:** Mappings $``\text{owner}(\text{Port\_Child}) = \text{Port\_Parent}``$ and $``\text{owner}(\text{Port\_Parent}) = \text{Classifier\_1}``$ hold within depth $D$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Conjugation Direction Inversion
- **Given:** Port `PortDef_Alpha` (`In`) instantiated as `Port_Beta : ~PortDef_Alpha`.
- **When:** Conjugation lowering evaluates ${\sim}$.
- **Then:** Direction evaluates to `Out` with invariant type $``\tau(\text{Port\_Beta}) \cong \tau(\text{PortDef\_Alpha})``$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-04: Negative Diagnostic Rejection & Error Recovery
- **Given:** Model with an unresolvable type, direction conflict, and cyclic containment.
- **When:** Port roster validation executes.
- **Then:** Rejection emits `E0200` and `E0205`, halting at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0200`, `E0205`, `E0199`.
