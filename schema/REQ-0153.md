## 1. Normative Statement

The compiler SHALL enforce failsafe domain validity and boundary assertion gate `FailsafeGate_Alpha` across signals in the Master Signal Flow Dictionary.

---

## 2. Formal Invariant

The failsafe declaration, domain containment, boundary assertion, and safety invariant predicates satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\forall\sigma\in\mathcal{F}_{\text{dict}},\quad & \tau_{\text{tgt}}=\tau(P_{\text{tgt}}(\sigma)),\quad v_{\text{failsafe}}(\sigma)\in\text{Domain}(\tau_{\text{tgt}}) \\
\mathcal{I}_{\text{safe}}(v_{\text{failsafe}}(\sigma)) & \iff(v_{\min}\le v_{\text{failsafe}}(\sigma)\le v_{\max})\land(\text{IsDivisor}(\sigma)\implies v_{\text{failsafe}}(\sigma)\ne 0) \\
& \quad\land(\forall t\in\Phi_{\text{forbidden}}(P_{\text{tgt}}),\,\text{Eval}(\text{Guard}(t)[v_{\text{failsafe}}(\sigma)/\sigma])\equiv\text{false}) \\
(v_{\text{failsafe}}(\sigma)=\bot\lor v_{\text{failsafe}}(\sigma)\notin\text{Domain}(\tau_{\text{tgt}})) & \implies\text{Diagnostic}(\sigma,\text{E0208})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land\sigma\gets\bot_{\text{failsafe}} \\
\neg\mathcal{I}_{\text{safe}}(v_{\text{failsafe}}(\sigma)) & \implies\text{Diagnostic}(\sigma,\text{E0208})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land\sigma\gets\bot_{\text{failsafe}} \\
(P_{\text{tgt}}(\sigma)\notin\mathcal{P}_{\text{boundary}}\lor\tau_{\text{tgt}}=\bot_{\text{type}}) & \implies\text{Diagnostic}(\sigma,\text{E0200})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}} & \implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
\Delta_\sigma=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(\sigma)), & \quad\Delta_{\text{failsafe}}=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(\sigma)\mathbin{\Vert}\text{"::FailsafeDefault"})
\end{aligned}
```

| $``\mathcal{F}_{\text{dict}}, \sigma``$ | Cataloged signal flow dictionary and individual signal flow record |
| $``P_{\text{tgt}}, \tau_{\text{tgt}}``$ | Destination boundary port handle and target classifier type |
| $``v_{\text{failsafe}}(\sigma)``$ | Declared failsafe default value literal or constant expression |
| $\text{Domain}(\tau)$ | Admissible value domain of classifier type $\tau$ |

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial Time).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(|F|)$ domain membership evaluation ($``|F| = |\mathcal{F}_{\text{dict}}|``$). Work-stealing pool: $``O(|F| / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$. Space: $O(|F|)$ arena footprint, $O(1)$ error sink.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** N/A (Deterministic P). Ground interval checks execute in linear time, bypassing NP-5 (REQ-0124) and NP-8 (REQ-0197).

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Valid In-Domain Failsafe Value
- **Given:** Signal `Signal_Alpha` in `Package_0` routed to `Port_Tgt` (`Int32`), with failsafe `Failsafe_1` set to `0`.
- **When:** Lowering through gate `FailsafeGate_Alpha`.
- **Then:** Confirms $0 \in [-2^{31}, 2^{31}-1]$ and satisfies $``\mathcal{I}_{\text{safe}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Boundary Envelope Conformance
- **Given:** Signal `Signal_Alpha` (`param_x : Real`) targeting `Port_Tgt` with envelope $[0.0, 100.0]$ and failsafe `25.0`.
- **When:** Evaluating boundary assertions.
- **Then:** Confirms $0.0 \le 25.0 \le 100.0$ and admits declaration.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Out-of-Domain Failsafe Rejection
- **Given:** Signal `Signal_Alpha` targeting `Port_Tgt` (`Int8`) with failsafe value `256`.
- **When:** Verifying destination port domain.
- **Then:** Fails closed, emits `E0208`, and binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{failsafe}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0208` (FailsafeValueDomainViolation).

### AC-04: Divisor Hazard & Error Recovery
- **Given:** Signals `Signal_Alpha` (divisor failsafe `0`) and `Signal_Beta` (missing `Port_Src`), separated by `;`.
- **When:** Validating with delimiter error recovery.
- **Then:** Emits `E0208` and `E0200`, resynchronizes, and halts at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0208`, `E0200`, `E0199`.
