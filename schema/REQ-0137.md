## 1. Normative Statement

The safety compiler SHALL synthesize Boolean safety invariants from hazard scenarios by generating the logical negation of hazardous state predicates over the state vector.

---

## 2. Formal Invariant

Synthesis invariants, safe envelopes, and diagnostic triggers satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{X}=\prod_{j=1}^k D_j,\quad \mathbf{x}=\langle x_1,\dots,x_k\rangle\in\mathbf{X},\quad \mathcal{H}=\{H_1,\dots,H_m\} \\
\mathcal{S}_{\text{hazard}, i}=\{\mathbf{x}\in\mathbf{X}\mid H_i(\mathbf{x})=\text{true}\},\quad \mathcal{I}_{\text{safe}, i}(\mathbf{x})\equiv\neg H_i(\mathbf{x}) \\
\mathcal{I}_{\text{safe}, i}^{\text{context}}(\mathbf{x})\equiv C_i(\mathbf{x}) \implies \neg H_i(\mathbf{x})\equiv\neg C_i(\mathbf{x})\lor\neg H_i(\mathbf{x}) \\
\mathcal{I}_{\text{system}}(\mathbf{x})=\bigwedge_{i=1}^m\mathcal{I}_{\text{safe}, i}(\mathbf{x})\equiv\neg\left(\bigvee_{i=1}^m H_i(\mathbf{x})\right) \\
\mathcal{S}_{\text{safe}}=\{\mathbf{x}\in\mathbf{X}\mid\mathcal{I}_{\text{system}}(\mathbf{x})=\text{true}\}=\mathbf{X}\setminus\bigcup_{i=1}^m\mathcal{S}_{\text{hazard}, i} \\
(\exists H_i\in\mathcal{H}:\forall\mathbf{x},\,H_i(\mathbf{x})\equiv\text{true})\implies\text{Diagnostic}(H_i,\text{E0400})\land H_i\gets\bot_{\text{inv}} \\
(\exists i\ne j:\forall\mathbf{x},\,\mathcal{I}_i(\mathbf{x})\land\mathcal{I}_j(\mathbf{x})\equiv\text{false})\implies\text{Diagnostic}((\mathcal{I}_i,\mathcal{I}_j),\text{E0400}) \\
(\exists x\in\text{Vars}(H_i):x\notin\mathbf{x})\implies\text{Diagnostic}(x,\text{E0400})\land H_i\gets\bot_{\text{inv}} \\
N_{\text{step}}\gt\kappa_{\text{safety}}\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\mathcal{H},\text{E0406}) \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199}) \\
\forall H_i\in\mathcal{H},\quad \Delta_{\text{inv}}(H_i)=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(H_i)\mathbin{\Vert}\text{"::SafeInvariant"})
\end{aligned}
```

| Symbol / Term | Domain / Formal Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| $\mathbf{X}, \mathbf{x}$ | State space $``\prod D_j``$ and state vector |
| $``\mathcal{H}, H_i``$ | Hazards and Boolean hazard predicate |
| $``\mathcal{S}_{\text{hazard}}, \mathcal{S}_{\text{safe}}``$ | Hazardous states and safe envelope |
| $``\mathcal{I}_{\text{safe}}, \mathcal{I}_{\text{system}}``$ | Synthesized individual and composite invariants |
| $``C_i(\mathbf{x})``$ | Context guard predicate |
| $``\kappa_{\text{safety}}, N_{\text{step}}``$ | Solver step budget and step counter |
| $``\bot_{\text{inv}}, \Delta_{\text{inv}}``$ | Sentinel node and UUIDv5 anchor |

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial Time).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** Syntactic negation and AST allocation run in $O(|H| \cdot |\text{preds}|)$ for $|H| = m$ hazards and $|\text{preds}|$ predicates. Composite conjunction runs in $O(m)$. Consistency checking takes $``O(m \cdot \kappa_{\text{safety}})``$. Work-stealing: $``O((m \cdot |\text{preds}| + m \cdot \kappa_{\text{safety}}) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$. Space: $O(m \cdot |\text{preds}|)$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** NP Frontier Anchor N/A (Deterministic P). Dual invariant synthesis is linear AST construction; consistency verification is step-bounded by $``\kappa_{\text{safety}}``$ with `E0406` on timeout, avoiding NP-4 (REQ-0135) and NP-8 (REQ-0197) exponential search.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical Boolean Invariant Synthesis
- **Given:** Model `Package_0` with `StateVector_1` (`param_x : Real`) and hazard `Hazard_Scenario_0` ($``H_0(\mathbf{x}) \equiv (\text{param\_x} \gt 80.0)``$).
- **When:** Lowering hazard scenarios into synthesized invariants (`SafetyInvariant_Alpha`).
- **Then:** Emits `AssertConstraintUsage` AST node with predicate $``\neg(\text{param\_x} \gt 80.0) \equiv (\text{param\_x} \le 80.0)``$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Context-Guarded Conditional Invariant Synthesis
- **Given:** Model `Package_0` with hazard `Hazard_Scenario_1` guarded by $``C_1(\mathbf{x}) \equiv (\text{StateVector\_1}.\text{mode} == 1)``$.
- **When:** Generating context-conditioned invariant AST nodes.
- **Then:** Emits implication $``C_1(\mathbf{x}) \implies \neg H_1(\mathbf{x}) \equiv \neg C_1(\mathbf{x}) \lor \neg H_1(\mathbf{x})``$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Unsatisfiable Invariant & Undefined Variable Rejection
- **Given:** Model `Package_0` declaring universal hazard $``H_0 \equiv \text{true}``$ or unresolved variable $x \notin \mathbf{x}$.
- **When:** Invariant satisfiability and scope validation execute.
- **Then:** Rejects input, emits diagnostic `E0400`, and binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{inv}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` (UnsatisfiableSafetyInvariant).

### AC-04: Solver Step Limit Enforcement & Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Hazard model exceeding step budget $``\kappa_{\text{safety}}``$ or syntax faults preceding delimiter (`;`, `}`).
- **When:** Solver evaluates consistency and parser resynchronizes on delimiters.
- **Then:** Emits `E0406` on step budget breach, accumulates errors in $``\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}``$, and halts at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0406`, `E0199`.
