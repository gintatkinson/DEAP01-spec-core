## 2. Formal Invariant

CFG $``\mathcal{G}_{\text{cfg}}``$, SSA bindings, and diagnostic triggers satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{G}_{\text{cfg}}=(\mathcal{B},\mathcal{E}_{\text{cfg}}),&\quad\text{pred}(B_{\text{entry}})=\emptyset\land\forall B\in\mathcal{B},\text{Paths}(B_{\text{entry}},B)\ne\emptyset \\
\forall v\in\mathcal{V}_{\text{ssa}},&\quad|\text{Def}(v)|=1\land\forall u\in\text{Uses}(v),\text{Def}(v)\ \mathbf{dom}\ u \\
\forall B\in\mathcal{B},&\quad|\text{pred}(B)|\le 1\implies\mathbf{\Phi}_B=\emptyset \\
\phi=\left(v_{\phi}\leftarrow\phi(\langle v_1,B_1\rangle,\dots,\langle v_k,B_k\rangle)\right)&\implies\{B_1,\dots,B_k\}=\text{pred}(B)\land\tau(v_i)\equiv\tau(v_{\phi}) \\
\text{DF}(B)&=\left\{Y\in\mathcal{B}\mid\exists P\in\text{pred}(Y):B\ \mathbf{dom}\ P\land\neg(B\ \mathbf{sdom}\ Y)\right\} \\
|\text{Def}(v)|\ne 1\lor\exists u\in\text{Uses}(v):\neg(\text{Def}(v)\ \mathbf{dom}\ u)&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(v,\text{E0501})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land v\gets\bot_{\text{ssa}} \\
T_B=\bot\lor\exists B'\in\text{targets}(T_B):B'\notin\mathcal{B}&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(B,\text{E0300})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land B\gets\bot_{\text{cfg}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}},\quad\Delta_e=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(e))
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial Time).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $``O(|B| + |E_{\text{cfg}}|)``$ CFG and dominance traversal ($``|B| = |\mathcal{B}|, |E_{\text{cfg}}| = |\mathcal{E}_{\text{cfg}}|``$). Lowering runs in $``O(|B| + |E_{\text{cfg}}| + |I|)``$ for $|I|$ instructions. Dominator trees take $``O(|B| + |E_{\text{cfg}}|)``$; SSA checks take $O(1)$ per use. Work-stealing scales as $``O((|B| + |E_{\text{cfg}}|)/N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$. Arena memory is $``O(|B| + |E_{\text{cfg}}| + |I|)``$.
- **Tractable Subclass and Frontier:** N/A (Deterministic P). SSA lowering avoids NP-7 coloring (REQ-0180), NP-5 reachability (REQ-0124), and NP-8 solving (REQ-0197).

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical CFG Construction
- **Given:** Model in `Package_0` declaring `Classifier_Alpha::Function_0` with branching.
- **When:** Lowering AST into `LumiIR_Alpha`.
- **Then:** Emits valid CFG with entry `BasicBlock_0`, linear instructions, and terminators.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Join Block $\phi$-Node Resolution
- **Given:** Join block `BasicBlock_Join` with predecessors `BasicBlock_0` and `BasicBlock_1`.
- **When:** Lowering variables into `SSA_1`.
- **Then:** Inserts $\phi$-node bijectively matching predecessors with uniform types.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: SSA Multiple Definition Rejection
- **Given:** Malformed IR defining `SSA_1` multiple times or violating dominance.
- **When:** Validating SSA invariants.
- **Then:** Fails closed, emits `E0501`, and binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{ssa}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0501`.

### AC-04: Terminator Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Block `BasicBlock_0` missing a terminator, delimiter `;`, and `BasicBlock_1`.
- **When:** Recovering across `;`.
- **Then:** Emits `E0300`, binds $``\bot_{\text{cfg}}``$, parses `BasicBlock_1`, halting at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300`, `E0199`.
