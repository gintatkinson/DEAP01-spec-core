## 1. Normative Statement

The compiler SHALL verify that engineering units of signal flows match connected port dimensions in $\mathbb{Q}^7$.

---

## 2. Formal Invariant

The dimensional vector, triad equality, scale adapter validity, and diagnostic rules satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{u}=[d_L,d_M,d_T,d_I,d_\Theta,d_N,d_J]^T\in\mathbb{Q}^7, & \quad d_k=\frac{p_k}{q_k} \quad (p_k\in\mathbb{Z}, q_k\in\mathbb{Z}^+, \gcd(|p_k|,q_k)=1) \\
\forall\sigma=\langle\text{id},p_s,p_t,\mathbf{u}_\sigma,s_\sigma\rangle\in\mathcal{F}_{\text{dict}}, & \quad \mathbf{u}_\sigma=\mathbf{u}(p_s)=\mathbf{u}(p_t)\in\mathbb{Q}^7 \\
(p_s\notin\mathcal{P} \lor p_t\notin\mathcal{P}) & \implies \text{Diagnostic}(\sigma,\text{E0200})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \land \sigma\gets\bot_{\text{dim}} \\
(\mathbf{u}(p_s)\ne\mathbf{u}(p_t) \lor \mathbf{u}_\sigma\ne\mathbf{u}(p_s) \lor \mathbf{u}_\sigma\ne\mathbf{u}(p_t)) & \implies \text{Diagnostic}(\sigma,\text{E0207})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \land \sigma\gets\bot_{\text{dim}} \\
(s(p_s)\ne s(p_t) \land \text{Adapter}(\sigma)=\bot) & \implies \text{Diagnostic}(\sigma,\text{E0207})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}} \implies \text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}, & \quad \Delta_\sigma=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(\sigma))
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial Time).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(|F| \cdot 7)$ rational vector equivalence for $``|F| = |\mathcal{F}_{\text{dict}}|``$ flows. Comparing vectors in $\mathbb{Q}^7$ executes 7 coordinate checks ($O(1)$ each). Parallel execution achieves $``O((|F| \cdot 7) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$. Space: $O(|F|)$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** N/A (Deterministic P). Evaluates closed-form rational equivalence, bypassing NP-2 (REQ-0147) and NP-8 (REQ-0197).

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Homogeneous Dimensional Triad Conformance
- **Given:** Flow `Flow_Alpha` connecting `Port_1` to `Port_2` with dimension `UnitVector_0` and scale $1$.
- **When:** Evaluating SI unit verification.
- **Then:** Evaluates $``\mathbf{u}_{\text{flow}} = \mathbf{u}(p_1) = \mathbf{u}(p_2)``$ and passes.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Unit Scale Factor Adaptation
- **Given:** Ports sharing `UnitVector_0` with scale disparity ($10^{-3}$ vs $1$) and adapter `Adapter_Alpha`.
- **When:** Evaluating scale compatibility.
- **Then:** Validates $``s_{\text{ratio}} = 10^{-3}``$ against `Adapter_Alpha` and passes.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Dimensional Mismatch Rejection
- **Given:** Flow `Flow_Beta` connecting `Port_1` (`UnitVector_0`) to `Port_2` (`UnitVector_1`).
- **When:** Comparing endpoint vectors in $\mathbb{Q}^7$.
- **Then:** Detects mismatch, fails closed, binds $``\bot_{\text{dim}}``$, emitting `E0207`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0207` (DimensionalMismatchViolation).

### AC-04: Unresolved Endpoint & Error Recovery
- **Given:** Flow referencing missing `Port_Unknown` and flow with dimensional mismatch, separated by `;`.
- **When:** Delimiter recovery on syntax and semantic faults.
- **Then:** Emits `E0200` and `E0207`, resynchronizes on `;`, and halts at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0200`, `E0207`, `E0199`.
