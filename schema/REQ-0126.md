## 1. Normative Statement

The state machine solver SHALL validate and execute Shallow History (`[H]`) and Deep History (`[H*]`) restoration semantics.

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Taxonomy**: Pseudostates partition into Shallow (`History_Shallow`) and Deep (`History_Deep`) within composite (`CompositeState_Alpha`), orthogonal composite, or region (`Region_1`). Root ($``s_{\text{root}}``$), leaf, or duplicate placement emits `E0400`.
2. **Default Transition**: Pseudostates MUST declare one unconditional transition $``T_{\text{default}}``$ ($``e_{\text{trig}} = \epsilon, g \equiv \text{true}``$) to child state ($``s_{\text{tgt}} \in \mathcal{C}_{\text{parent}}``$); violations emit `E0400`, external targets emit `E0411`.
3. **Non-Occupational**: History pseudostates cannot be in active configuration $C$ ($``C \cap \mathcal{P}_{\text{history}} = \emptyset``$); actions emit `E0400`.
4. **Snapshot & Reset**: Exiting $``S_{\text{comp}}``$ records active descendants: $``\mathcal{H}(S_{\text{comp}}) \leftarrow C \cap \text{descendants}(S_{\text{comp}})``$. Final state or reset clears history ($\mathcal{H} \leftarrow \emptyset$).
5. **Restoration**: Shallow (`[H]`) restores child $s^* \in \mathcal{H} \cap \mathcal{C}$ (or $``T_{\text{default}}``$ if $\mathcal{H} = \emptyset$), resolving initial transitions deeper. Deep (`[H*]`) restores descendants $``\mathcal{H}(S_{\text{comp}})``$ (or $``T_{\text{default}}``$ if $\mathcal{H} = \emptyset$) without initial transitions. Corrupted states emit `E0411`.
6. **Order**: Transition effect $\to$ container entry $\to$ default action or restored entry actions.

## 2. Formal Invariant

State history containment, default transitions, and configuration restoration:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{P}_{\text{history}}=\mathcal{P}_{\text{shallow}}\cup\mathcal{P}_{\text{deep}},\quad \forall h\in\mathcal{P}_{\text{history}},\;\text{parent}(h)\in(\mathcal{S}_{\text{xor}}\cup\mathcal{S}_{\text{orth}}\cup\mathcal{R})\setminus\{s_{\text{root}}\} \\
\forall p,\;|\{h\in\mathcal{P}_{\text{shallow}}\mid\text{parent}(h)=p\}|\le 1\land |\{h\in\mathcal{P}_{\text{deep}}\mid\text{parent}(h)=p\}|\le 1 \\
\forall h\in\mathcal{P}_{\text{history}},\;|\{\tau\mid s_{\text{src}}(\tau)=h\}|=1\land s_{\text{tgt}}(T_{\text{default}}(h))\in\mathcal{C}_{\text{parent}(h)}\land C\cap\mathcal{P}_{\text{history}}=\emptyset \\
\mathcal{H}(S_{\text{comp}})\leftarrow C\cap\text{descendants}(S_{\text{comp}}),\quad (C\cap\mathcal{S}_{\text{final}}\ne\emptyset\implies\mathcal{H}(S_{\text{comp}})\leftarrow\emptyset) \\
C_{\text{restore}}(h_{\text{shallow}})=(\mathcal{H}=\emptyset\mathrel{?}\{s_{\text{def}}\}\cup\text{ResolveInitial}(s_{\text{def}}):\{s^*\}\cup\text{ResolveInitial}(s^*)) \\
C_{\text{restore}}(h_{\text{deep}})=(\mathcal{H}=\emptyset\mathrel{?}\{s_{\text{def}}\}\cup\text{ResolveInitial}(s_{\text{def}}):\mathcal{H}(S_{\text{comp}})),\quad \Delta_n=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(n))
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial Time)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(D)$ tree snapshot and restoration per transition ($D \le 32$); $``O(N + |\mathcal{E}_{\text{trans}}|)``$ AST validation for $N$ states.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Traverses trees without unrolling (NP-5). Work-stealing scales as $``O((N + |\mathcal{E}_{\text{trans}}|) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Shallow History Restoration
- **Given:** `CompositeState_Alpha` with `State_Sub_0`, `State_Sub_1`, and `History_Shallow` defaulting to `State_Sub_0`.
- **When:** `State_Sub_1` active, `CompositeState_Alpha` exits and re-enters on `History_Shallow`.
- **Then:** Restores `State_Sub_1` without evaluating `State_Sub_0`.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink empty.

### AC-02: Deep History Subtree Restoration
- **Given:** `CompositeState_Alpha` with child `CompositeState_Beta`, active leaf `State_Leaf_A2`, and `History_Deep`.
- **When:** `CompositeState_Alpha` exits and re-enters on `History_Deep`.
- **Then:** Restores `CompositeState_Beta` and `State_Leaf_A2` in $O(D)$ time without initial transitions.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink empty.

### AC-03: Default Transition Execution
- **Given:** `CompositeState_Alpha` with `History_Shallow` and uninitialized history ($\mathcal{H} = \emptyset$).
- **When:** Transition targets `History_Shallow`.
- **Then:** Fires $``T_{\text{default}}``$, activating `State_Sub_0` in zero time ($\Delta t = 0$).
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink empty.

### AC-04: Structural Malformation Rejection
- **Given:** History at root $``s_{\text{root}}``$, duplicate history, and external target, delimited by `;`.
- **When:** Validation passes execute.
- **Then:** Rejects root/duplicate with `E0400`, rejects external target with `E0411`, recovering on `;` with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400`, `E0411`, `E0199`.
