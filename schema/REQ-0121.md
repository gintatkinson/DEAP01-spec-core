## 1. Normative Statement

The state machine solver SHALL validate and lower discrete event dispatching under Run-to-Completion (RTC) semantics with a bounded FIFO event queue.

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Queue Semantics**: Events partition into Signal, Call, Time, Change, and Completion. External events enqueue at FIFO tail ($``K_{\text{max\_queue}}=65536``$); completion events precede external stimuli. Overflow ($``|Q|\ge K_{\text{max\_queue}}``$) or non-positive intervals ($``\delta_{\text{timer}}\le 0``$) emit `E0400`.
2. **RTC Non-Interruptibility**: Steps execute atomically from head event $e$. Arriving events buffer at tail without preemption or state mutation. Dequeuing $``e_{i+1}``$ requires quiescence and action completion.
3. **Trigger Matching**: Transition $\tau$ matches $e$ iff trigger, kind, and payload conform ($``\text{type}(\pi_e)\sqsubseteq\text{type}(\text{param}(\tau))``$); faults emit `E0400`. Satisfied guards enable transitions; ambiguous overlapping sibling guards emit `E0400`.
4. **Priority & Deferral**: Deeper states take priority ($``\tau_1\succ\tau_2\iff\text{depth}(\text{source}(\tau_1))\gt\text{depth}(\text{source}(\tau_2))``$). Invalid containment emits `E0402`. States may defer events to $``\mathcal{Q}_{\text{defer}}``$; conflicting trigger/deferral emits `E0400`. Unhandled events discard safely.
5. **Orthogonal Concurrency**: Events dispatch across regions, firing $\le 1$ transition per region without races.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Event queue semantics, trigger matching, and diagnostic triggers:

```math
\begin{aligned}
e=\langle\text{id}_e,\text{name}_e,\kappa_e,\pi_e,t_{\text{enq}},\Delta_e\rangle\in\mathcal{E},\quad Q=\langle e_1,\dots,e_p\rangle\in\mathcal{E}^*,\;|Q|\le K_{\text{max\_queue}} \\
\text{MatchTrigger}(\tau,e)\iff(\text{trigger}(\tau)=\text{name}_e)\land(\text{kind}(\tau)=\kappa_e)\land(\text{type}(\pi_e)\sqsubseteq\text{type}(\text{param}(\tau))) \\
\text{Enabled}(\tau,C,e,\sigma)\iff\text{source}(\tau)\in C\land\text{MatchTrigger}(\tau,e)\land(\text{guard}(\tau)=\emptyset\lor\text{Eval}(\text{guard}(\tau),\sigma)) \\
\forall\tau_1,\tau_2\in\text{Enabled}(C,e,\sigma),\;\tau_1\succ\tau_2\iff\text{depth}(\text{source}(\tau_1))\gt\text{depth}(\text{source}(\tau_2)) \\
\langle C,Q,\sigma,t\rangle\xrightarrow{\text{RTCStep}(e)}\langle C',Q',\sigma',t'\rangle,\quad\forall a\in\text{Actions}(\mathcal{T}_{\text{fire}}),\;\text{status}(a,t')=\text{Terminated} \\
(|Q|\ge K_{\text{max\_queue}}\lor\text{type}(\pi_e)\not\sqsubseteq\text{type}(\text{param}(\tau))\lor\text{AmbiguousGuards}\lor\delta_{\text{timer}}\le 0)\implies\text{Diagnostic}(n,\text{E0400})\land n\gets\bot_{\text{event}} \\
(\text{source}(\tau)\notin C\lor\text{target}(\tau)\notin\mathcal{S}_{\text{all}})\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\tau,\text{E0402})\land\tau\gets\bot_{\text{trans}} \\
\forall n\in\mathcal{V}_{\text{event}}\cup\mathcal{V}_{\text{queue}}\cup\mathcal{V}_{\text{trigger}},\;\Delta_n=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(n))
\end{aligned}
```

| $C, C'$ | Pre-step and post-step active state configurations |

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(1)$ event dequeue; $O(T)$ RTC step lowering for $``T=|\mathcal{E}_{\text{trans}}|``$ (with $O(1)$ matching, $O(h)$ LCA depth for $h\le 32$); simulation is $O(K\cdot T)$ for $K=|Q|$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Avoids exponential NP-5 state reachability (REQ-0124) via operational RTC semantics. Work-stealing scales as $``O((K\cdot T)/N_{\text{max\_workers}}+\log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: FIFO Dequeue & RTC Transition Firing
- **Given:** `EventQueue_1` with $``\langle\text{Event\_Signal1},\text{Event\_Call1}\rangle``$ in `State_Alpha`.
- **When:** Dequeuing `Event_Signal1` fires `Transition_1` to `State_Beta`.
- **Then:** Actions terminate; $``C'=\{\text{State\_Beta}\}``$ settles before next dequeue.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: RTC Non-Interruptibility & Queue Overflow
- **Given:** Active RTC step in `StateMachine_Alpha` with full queue ($|Q|=65536$).
- **When:** Event arrives during step, followed by overflow enqueue.
- **Then:** Buffers event at tail without preemption; overflow emits `E0400`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` (EventQueueOverflow).

### AC-03: Trigger Payload Mismatch & Ambiguous Guards
- **Given:** Transition expecting `param_x : Real` with payload mismatch, or overlapping sibling guards.
- **When:** Type checking and conflict analysis execute.
- **Then:** Rejects payload mismatch or ambiguous guard overlap with `E0400`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` (MalformedTriggerOrConflict).

### AC-04: Hierarchy Containment Fault & Recovery
- **Given:** Transition violating containment before `;`, followed by a valid transition.
- **When:** Containment checking and delimiter recovery execute.
- **Then:** Emits `E0402`, binds $``\bot_{\text{trans}}``$, recovers on `;`, halting at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0402`, `E0199`.
