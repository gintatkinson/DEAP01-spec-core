## 1. Normative Statement

The dynamics solver SHALL assign interval bounds $``[t_{\text{start}}, t_{\text{end}}] \subset \mathbb{R} \cup \{-\infty, +\infty\}``$ to lifecycles, actions, and states referenced to epoch $``t_{\text{epoch}} = 0``$.

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Interval Bounds**: Intervals require $``t_{\text{start}} \le t_{\text{end}}``$ and $``t_{\text{start}} \ge 0``$. Inversions ($``t_{\text{start}} \gt t_{\text{end}}``$) or negative coordinates emit `E0400`.
2. **Lifecycle Dichotomy**: Events require $``t_{\text{start}} = t_{\text{end}}``$ ($\text{duration} = 0$). Durative occurrences require $``t_{\text{start}} \lt t_{\text{end}}``$ ($\text{duration} \gt 0$). Violations emit `E0400`.
3. **Duration Consistency**: Nominal $``\delta_{\text{nom}} \ge 0``$ requires $``|(t_{\text{end}} - t_{\text{start}}) - \delta_{\text{nom}}| \le \epsilon_{\text{tol}}``$ ($``\epsilon_{\text{tol}} = 10^{-9}``$). Envelopes require $``\delta_{\min} \le t_{\text{end}} - t_{\text{start}} \le \delta_{\max}``$. Breaches emit `E0400`.
4. **Hierarchical Containment**: Sub-occurrences ($``o_{\text{sub}} \subseteq_{\text{occ}} o_{\text{super}}``$) require $``[t_{\text{start}}(o_{\text{sub}}), t_{\text{end}}(o_{\text{sub}})] \subseteq [t_{\text{start}}(o_{\text{super}}), t_{\text{end}}(o_{\text{super}})]``$; breaches emit `E0401`.
5. **State Continuity**: Sequential states satisfy $``t_{\text{end}}(S_i) = t(\tau) = t_{\text{start}}(S_{i+1})``$. Exclusive state overlaps emit `E0401`.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Occurrence lifecycle intervals, duration consistency, hierarchical containment, and error mappings satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
I(o)=[t_{\text{start}}(o),t_{\text{end}}(o)],\quad 0\le t_{\text{start}}(o)\le t_{\text{end}}(o),\quad \text{duration}(I(o))=t_{\text{end}}(o)-t_{\text{start}}(o)\ge 0 \\
(o\in\mathcal{O}_{\text{event}}\implies t_{\text{start}}=t_{\text{end}})\land (o\in\mathcal{O}_{\text{durative}}\implies t_{\text{start}}\lt t_{\text{end}}) \\
\delta_{\min}\le\text{duration}(I(o))\le\delta_{\max},\quad |\text{duration}(I(o))-\delta_{\text{nom}}|\le\epsilon_{\text{tol}} \\
o_{\text{sub}}\subseteq_{\text{occ}} o_{\text{super}}\implies (t_{\text{start}}(o_{\text{super}})\le t_{\text{start}}(o_{\text{sub}})\le t_{\text{end}}(o_{\text{sub}})\le t_{\text{end}}(o_{\text{super}})) \\
S_i\xrightarrow{\tau} S_{i+1}\implies t_{\text{end}}(S_i)=t(\tau)=t_{\text{start}}(S_{i+1}),\quad I(S_i)\cap I(S_j)\subseteq\{t_{\text{boundary}}\} \\
(t_{\text{start}}\gt t_{\text{end}}\lor t_{\text{start}}\lt 0\lor \Delta t\lt 0)\implies\text{Diagnostic}(o,\text{E0400})\land I(o)\gets\bot_{\text{interval}} \\
(o_{\text{sub}}\not\subseteq_{\text{time}} o_{\text{super}}\lor I(S_i)\cap I(S_j)\not\subseteq\{t_{\text{boundary}}\})\implies\text{Diagnostic}(o,\text{E0401})\land o_{\text{sub}}\gets\bot_{\text{occ}} \\
\forall o\in\mathcal{O}_{\text{life}},\quad \Delta_o=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(o))
\end{aligned}
```

| $``\mathcal{O}_{\text{event}}, \mathcal{O}_{\text{durative}}``$ | Events ($\Delta t = 0$) and durative occurrences ($\Delta t \gt 0$) |

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial Time)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(1)$ interval check; $O(N)$ lowering for $``N = |\mathcal{O}_{\text{life}}|``$ nodes; $O(N)$ containment traversal for $M \le N - 1$ edges. Work-stealing: $``O(N / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Deterministic polynomial; avoids NP-complete temporal reasoning (NP-1).

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Valid Occurrence Lifecycle and Interval Lowering
- **Given:** `Occurrence_Alpha` ($[0.0, 10.0]$) and event `Event_0` ($[5.0, 5.0]$) in `Package_0`.
- **When:** Lowering assigns interval bounds.
- **Then:** Assigns durations $10.0 \gt 0$ and $0.0$; diagnostic sink remains empty.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Inverted Bounds and Negative Epoch Rejection
- **Given:** `Occurrence_1` ($[15.0, 10.0]$) and `Occurrence_2` ($[-5.0, 10.0]$).
- **When:** Lowering checks interval bounds.
- **Then:** Rejects coordinates, binding $``\bot_{\text{interval}}``$ and emitting `E0400`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` (TemporalIntervalInverted / NegativeEpochTime).

### AC-03: Duration Parameter Consistency and Envelope Breach
- **Given:** `Action_Alpha` ($[0.0, 10.0]$, $``\delta_{\text{nom}} = 12.0``$) and `Action_Beta` ($[0.0, 8.0]$, envelope $[1.0, 5.0]$).
- **When:** Verifying duration consistency.
- **Then:** Nominal error and envelope breach emit `E0400`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` (DurationBoundsInconsistency / DurationEnvelopeBreach).

### AC-04: Hierarchical Containment Breach and State Overlap
- **Given:** Parent $[10.0, 50.0]$ with child $[5.0, 60.0]$, and exclusive states overlapping on $[10.0, 12.0]$.
- **When:** Containment and state continuity checks execute.
- **Then:** Breaches emit `E0401`, binding sentinel $``\bot_{\text{occ}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0401` (TemporalContainmentBreach / StateOccupancyOverlap).
