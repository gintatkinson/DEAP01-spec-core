## 1. Normative Statement

Subsystem 12 SHALL discharge formal proof obligations (`ProofObligation_Alpha`) from contracts via `SmtSolver_0`.

---

## 2. Formal Invariant

Syntactic admissibility, refutational equivalence, and solver cutoff bounds satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\text{Admissible}(\phi)&\iff\text{Theory}(\phi)\in\{\text{QF\_LRA},\text{QF\_LIA},\text{QF\_UF}\}\land\text{Quantifiers}(\phi)=\emptyset\land\text{Degree}(\phi)\le 1 \\
\text{UNSAT}(\Phi_{\text{hyp}} \land \neg \psi_{\text{concl}})&\iff(\Phi_{\text{hyp}}\models\psi_{\text{concl}}) \\
\text{Verify}(\phi,\kappa_{\text{smt}})&\in\{\text{Satisfied}(\mathcal{M}_{\text{counter}}),\text{Unsatisfiable}(\mathcal{W}_{\text{proof}}),\text{Timeout}(\kappa_{\text{smt}})\} \\
\text{Degree}(\phi)\gt 1\lor\text{Quantifiers}(\phi)\ne\emptyset&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\phi,\text{E0406})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land\phi\gets\bot_{\text{proof}} \\
N_{\text{conflicts}}(\phi)\gt\kappa_{\text{smt}}\lor\text{SAT}(\Phi_{\text{refute}})&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\phi,\text{E0406})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land\phi\gets\bot_{\text{proof}} \\
\neg\text{ValidPath}(p)&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(p,\text{E0300})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land p\gets\bot_{\text{proof}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}| \ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** NP-complete (Decidable QF Fragments) / Undecidable (General First-Order Logic).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** DPLL(T) search is bounded by cutoff $``O(\kappa_{\text{smt}} \cdot (C + A \log A))``$ for $C$ clauses and $A$ atoms. Theory solving runs in polynomial time. Work-stealing scales as $``O(\sum T_i / N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$. Arena footprint is $``O(V + E + \kappa_{\text{smt}})``$.
- **Tractable Subclass and Frontier:** NP-8 (SMT Invariant Contract Satisfiability). Enforces quantifier-free fragments (`QF_LRA`, `QF_LIA`, `QF_UF`) and cutoff $``\kappa_{\text{smt}} \le 100{,}000``$, distinguishing NP-8 from NP-1 (REQ-0117).

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Decidable Fragment Satisfaction
- **Given:** `Package_0` with invariant $``\text{param\_x} \le 100.0``$ given $``\text{param\_x} \le 50.0``$.
- **When:** `SmtSolver_0` verifies `ProofObligation_Alpha`.
- **Then:** Discharges as $\text{UNSAT}$, certifying satisfaction with witness $``\mathcal{W}_{\text{proof}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Non-Linear and Quantifier Rejection
- **Given:** Contract declaring term $``\text{param\_x} \cdot \text{param\_y}``$ or quantifier $\forall x \exists y$.
- **When:** Admissibility is evaluated.
- **Then:** Emits `E0406`, binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{proof}}``$, and halts dispatch.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0406`.

### AC-03: Counterexample and Step Timeout
- **Given:** Invariant violation or cutoff $``\kappa_{\text{smt}} = 20``$.
- **When:** `SmtSolver_0` evaluates refutation formula.
- **Then:** On $\text{SAT}$, extracts counterexample $``\mathcal{M}_{\text{counter}}``$ and emits `E0406`; on timeout, emits `E0406`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0406`.

### AC-04: Delimiter Resynchronization and Recovery
- **Given:** Malformed contract with delimiter `;` and valid `ProofObligation_Beta`.
- **When:** Parser processes stream into $``\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}``$.
- **Then:** Recovers across `;`, parses `ProofObligation_Beta`, halting at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0406`, `E0199`.
