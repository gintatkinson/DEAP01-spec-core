## 1. Normative Statement

The compiler SHALL bootstrap KerML `ControlFunctions` (`Package_ControlFunctions`) into root scope at initialization, providing `ControlNode` (specializing `Feature` and `Step`) and primitive functions `Control_Fork`, `Control_Join`, `Control_Decision`, and `Control_Merge` (specializing `ControlNode` and `Function`).

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Topology Degrees**: Exact degree constraints: `Control_Fork` ($``\text{deg}_{\text{in}}=1, \text{deg}_{\text{out}}\ge 2``$), `Control_Join` ($``\text{deg}_{\text{in}}\ge 2, \text{deg}_{\text{out}}=1``$), `Control_Decision` ($``\text{deg}_{\text{in}}=1, \text{deg}_{\text{out}}\ge 2``$), and `Control_Merge` ($``\text{deg}_{\text{in}}\ge 2, \text{deg}_{\text{out}}=1``$); violations emit `E0200`.
2. **Token Passing**: Petri net marking $M$: Fork replicates 1 token to all outputs; Join awaits all input tokens before emitting 1 token; Decision routes 1 token to the true guard branch; Merge forwards arriving tokens immediately.
3. **Decision Guards**: Outgoing guards must type-check to `Boolean` (non-Boolean emits `E0202`), be pairwise disjoint, and be exhaustive; overlapping guards emit `E0200`.
4. **Join Deadlock Freedom**: Join inputs must originate from distinct outputs of a common ancestor fork; conflicting guards or un-forked decision paths emit `E0200`.
5. **Instant Cycles**: Closed cycles formed purely of control nodes emit `E0203`.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Control classifier specialization, degree constraints, token transition semantics, guard properties, and cycle freedom invariants:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{C}_{\text{ctrl}} &= \{\text{Control\_Fork}, \text{Control\_Join}, \text{Control\_Decision}, \text{Control\_Merge}\} \subset \text{ControlNode} \cap \text{Function} \\
\forall u \in V_{\text{ctrl}}, &\quad |\text{InEdges}(u)|=d_{\text{in}}(u), \quad |\text{OutEdges}(u)|=d_{\text{out}}(u) \\
\text{Fork}(u) &: M(e_{\text{in}})\ge 1 \implies M'(e_{\text{in}})=M(e_{\text{in}})-1 \wedge (\forall e\in\text{OutEdges},\; M'(e)=M(e)+1) \\
\text{Join}(u) &: (\forall e\in\text{InEdges},\; M(e)\ge 1) \implies (\forall e,\; M'(e)=M(e)-1) \wedge M'(e_{\text{out}})=M(e_{\text{out}})+1 \\
\text{Decision}(u) &: (M(e_{\text{in}})\ge 1 \wedge g_i(\sigma)) \implies M'(e_{\text{in}})=M(e_{\text{in}})-1 \wedge M'(e_i)=M(e_i)+1 \\
\text{Merge}(u) &: (\exists e_j\in\text{InEdges} : M(e_j)\ge 1) \implies M'(e_j)=M(e_j)-1 \wedge M'(e_{\text{out}})=M(e_{\text{out}})+1 \\
\forall u\in V_{\text{dec}}, &\quad \forall i\ne j,\; \neg(g_i(\sigma)\wedge g_j(\sigma)) \wedge \tau(g_i)\equiv\text{Boolean} \\
\forall u\in V_{\text{join}}, &\quad \exists w\in V_{\text{fork}} : \text{Reachable}(w, u) \wedge \text{SAT}\left(\bigwedge_e \text{Guards}(w \rightsquigarrow e)\right) \\
\forall u\in V_{\text{ctrl}}, &\quad (u, u)\notin E_{\text{ctrl,node}}^+ \wedge \Delta_u=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(u))
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial Time)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(V + E)$ topology and cycle checks; $O(1)$ amortized token dispatch; $O(E)$ guard typing.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Acyclic action control graphs avoid EXPSPACE-complete Petri net reachability (NP-5). Degree checks, dominator join-deadlock verification, and Tarjan cycle detection evaluate in $O(V + E)$ time. Parallel work-stealing across $``N_{\text{max\_workers}}``$ threads scales as $``O((V + E) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical Library Bootstrap
- **Given:** Initialized compiler root environment.
- **When:** Standard library `Package_ControlFunctions` is bootstrapped.
- **Then:** `Control_Fork`, `Control_Join`, `Control_Decision`, and `Control_Merge` resolve as specializations of `ControlNode` and `Function` with stable 32-bit arena handles.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Fork-Join Token Synchronization
- **Given:** Model declaring `Control_Fork` distributing to `Step_Alpha` and `Step_Beta`, converging into `Control_Join`.
- **When:** Discrete execution simulation evaluates token flow.
- **Then:** `Control_Fork` replicates 1 token into 2 concurrent tokens; `Control_Join` awaits both tokens and fires 1 synchronized token upon complete arrival.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Malformed Control Node Degree Rejection
- **Given:** Model declaring `Control_Fork` with in-degree 2 or out-degree 1, or `Control_Join` with in-degree 1.
- **When:** Static topology degree verification executes.
- **Then:** Lowering rejects malformed nodes and emits diagnostic `E0200`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0200` (Malformed control topology).

### AC-04: Guard Typing and Deadlock Detection
- **Given:** Model declaring `Control_Decision` with non-Boolean guard `param_x + 1.0` or overlapping guards, or `Control_Join` with un-forked decision inputs.
- **When:** Semantic type analysis and deadlock freedom validation execute.
- **Then:** Rejection emits `E0202` for non-Boolean guards and `E0200` for ambiguous guards or join deadlock, binding sentinel $``\bot_{\text{ctrl}}``$, and emitting `E0199` at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0200`, `E0202`, or `E0199`.
