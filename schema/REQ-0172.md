## 1. Normative Statement

Subsystem 9 SHALL maintain bidirectional traceability across AST entities and projected artifacts, executing cascade `Cascade_1` over `TraceGraph_Alpha` ($``\mathcal{G}_{\text{trace}} = (V, E_{\text{trace}})``$) upon AST mutation.

---

## 2. Formal Invariant

Traceability topology, reachability cascades, and diagnostic bindings satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{G}_{\text{trace}}&=(V,E_{\text{trace}}),\quad V=V_{\text{AST}}\cup V_{\text{proj}},\quad E_{\text{trace}}=E_{\text{fwd}}\cup E_{\text{bwd}} \\
\forall(u,v)&\in E_{\text{fwd}},\quad(v,u)\in E_{\text{bwd}} \\
\forall v&\in V,\quad(\exists u\in V_{\text{dirty}}\land(u,v)\in E_{\text{trace}}^*)\implies\text{Dirty}(v)=1 \\
(u\notin V\lor v\notin V)&\implies\text{Diagnostic}((u,v),\text{E0300})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land(u,v)\gets\bot_{\text{trace}} \\
((u,v)\in E_{\text{fwd}}\land(v,u)\notin E_{\text{bwd}})&\implies\text{Diagnostic}((u,v),\text{E0314})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land v\gets\bot_{\text{link}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}},\quad\Delta_v=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(v))
\end{aligned}
```

| Symbol / Term | Domain / Formal Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| $``\mathcal{G}_{\text{trace}}, V, E_{\text{trace}}``$ | Traceability graph, vertex set, edge relations |
| $``V_{\text{AST}}, V_{\text{proj}}``$ | AST entity nodes and projected artifact nodes |
| $``E_{\text{fwd}}, E_{\text{bwd}}``$ | Forward projection edges and backward provenance edges |
| $``V_{\text{dirty}}, \text{Dirty}(v)``$ | Mutated seed nodes and dirty predicate |

| $``\Delta_v, \text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}``$ | Immutable UUIDv5 anchor and namespace OID |

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Polynomial Time).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(|V| + |E|)$ BFS dirty cascade over $``\mathcal{G}_{\text{trace}}``$. Bitset lookups run in $O(1)$ time. Scaling: $``O((|V| + |E|) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$. Arena: $O(|V| + |E|)$.
- **Tractable Subclass and Frontier:** N/A (Deterministic P). Linear BFS avoids NP-hard feedback sets, NP-1 intervals (REQ-0117), NP-2 packing (REQ-0147), and NP-8 SMT (REQ-0197).

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical Forward and Backward Dirty Cascade
- **Given:** Graph `TraceGraph_Alpha` with `Classifier_Alpha` (`DirtyNode_0`) linked to `Artifact_Alpha`.
- **When:** Mutating `DirtyNode_0` and triggering `Cascade_1`.
- **Then:** Traverses edges via BFS, marking `Classifier_Alpha` and `Artifact_Alpha` dirty.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Multi-Parent Convergence and Idempotent Traversal
- **Given:** Artifact `Artifact_Gamma` with edges from mutated `DirtyNode_0` and clean `Classifier_Beta`.
- **When:** Executing cascade invalidation across multiple passes.
- **Then:** Marks `Artifact_Gamma` dirty on initial visit, skipping redundant passes with zero diff churn.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Asymmetric Traceability Link Rejection
- **Given:** Forward edge $``(u, v) \in E_{\text{fwd}}``$ lacking reciprocal edge $``(v, u) \in E_{\text{bwd}}``$.
- **When:** Verifying link symmetry during cascade initialization.
- **Then:** Rejects asymmetric link, binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{link}}``$, and emits `E0314`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0314` (TraceabilityLinkInvalidationViolation).

### AC-04: Dangling Endpoint and Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Malformed link referencing `Node_Missing`, delimiter `;`, and valid `Link_0`.
- **When:** Delimiter error recovery processes tokens.
- **Then:** Emits `E0300`, recovers across `;`, parses `Link_0`, halting at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300`, `E0199`.
