## 1. Normative Statement

The simulation engine of Subsystem 10 SHALL synthesize discrete fixed-step Forward Euler blocks (`EulerSolver_Alpha`) for continuous dynamical models (`Package_0::Classifier_Alpha`).

---

## 2. Formal Invariant

Let $``S = \langle \mathbf{x}, \mathbf{u}, f, \mathbf{A}, \mathbf{B}, \Delta t, \mathbf{x}_0 \rangle``$ be a continuous solver model. Solver invariants satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{x}_{k+1}&=\mathbf{x}_k+\Delta t\cdot f(\mathbf{x}_k,\mathbf{u}_k,t_k)=(\mathbf{I}_n+\Delta t\mathbf{A})\mathbf{x}_k+\Delta t\mathbf{B}\mathbf{u}_k \\
\|\mathbf{d}_{k+1}\|&\le\frac{M_2}{2}\Delta t^2,\quad\|\mathbf{e}_K\|\le\frac{M_2}{2L}(e^{L(t_K-t_0)}-1)\Delta t \\
\forall\lambda&\in\text{eig}(\mathbf{A}),\quad|1+\lambda\Delta t|\le 1\iff\rho(\mathbf{I}_n+\Delta t\mathbf{A})\le 1 \\
\Delta_S&=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(S)\mathbin{\Vert}\text{"::EulerSolver::"}\mathbin{\Vert}\text{name}_S) \\
(\Delta t\le 0\lor\dim(\mathbf{x})\ne\dim(f))&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(S,\text{E0300})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land S\gets\bot_{\text{solver}} \\
(|1+\lambda\Delta t|>1\lor\text{isnan}(x_i))&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(S,\text{E0505})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land S\gets\bot_{\text{div}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|&\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}
\end{aligned}
```

| Symbol / Term | Domain / Formal Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| $``\mathbf{x}_k, \mathbf{u}_k, \Delta t``$ | State vector, input vector, and timestep |
| $``f, \mathbf{A}, \mathbf{B}, \mathbf{I}_n``$ | Vector field, system matrix, input matrix, identity |
| $``\rho(\mathbf{\Phi}), \mathbf{d}_{k+1}, \mathbf{e}_K``$ | Spectral radius, local error, global error |
| $``\Delta_S, \bot_{\text{solver}}, \bot_{\text{div}}, \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}``$ | UUIDv5 anchor, sentinels, diagnostic sink |

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial Time).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(n)$ update per timestep ($n=\dim(\mathbf{x})$); dense updates take $O(n^2+nm)$ ($m=\dim(\mathbf{u})$); horizon over $K$ steps takes $O(K\cdot n)$. Work-stealing takes $``O((M\cdot K\cdot n)/N_{\text{max\_workers}}+\log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$. Arena space is $O(n)$.
- **Tractable Subclass and Frontier:** N/A (Deterministic P). Evaluates arithmetic without NP-1 intervals (REQ-0117) or NP-8 solving (REQ-0197).

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Discrete Forward Euler Recurrence Execution
- **Given:** Model in `Package_0` with $\dot{x}=-2.0x$, $``x_0=1.0``$, `TimeStep_1` ($\Delta t=0.01$).
- **When:** Executing Forward Euler step.
- **Then:** Evaluates to $``x_1=0.98``$ within tolerance $\epsilon=10^{-12}$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Boundary Stability Condition Verification
- **Given:** Model `Matrix_A` with pole $\lambda=-10.0$ and critical timestep $``\Delta t_{\text{crit}}=0.2``$.
- **When:** Evaluating stable $\Delta t=0.18$ versus unstable $\Delta t=0.22$.
- **Then:** Decays if stable; emits `E0505` on $|1+\lambda\Delta t|>1$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0505`.

### AC-03: Timestep Positivity and Dimension Mismatch Rejection
- **Given:** Model `EulerSolver_Alpha` with timestep $\Delta t\le 0$ or mismatch $\dim(\mathbf{x})\ne\dim(f)$.
- **When:** Validating parameters during ingestion.
- **Then:** Fails closed, binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{solver}}``$, and emits `E0300`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300`.

### AC-04: Numerical Divergence Trapping and Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Equations with states ($``|x_i|>10^{300}``$, NaN) and syntax faults separated by `;`.
- **When:** Trapping divergence and recovering across delimiters.
- **Then:** Traps `E0505`; recovers on `;`, halting at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0199`, `E0505`.
