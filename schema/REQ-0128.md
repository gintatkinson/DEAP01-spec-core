## 1. Normative Statement

The safety compiler SHALL build, validate, and query a deterministic 7-tier bipartite DAG (`TraceabilityGraph_Alpha`) $``G_{\text{trace}} = (V_{\text{trace}}, E_{\text{trace}})``$ linking requirements, design elements, interfaces, constraints, code, and witnesses.

Validation MUST enforce:
1. **Disjoint Partitioning**: Vertices $``V_{\text{trace}}``$ partition into seven disjoint tiers: `Tier_1` ($``T_1``$, $``\mathcal{R}_{\text{mission}}``$), `Tier_2` ($``T_2``$, $``\mathcal{R}_{\text{system}}``$), `Tier_3` ($``T_3``$, $``\mathcal{C}_{\text{arch}}``$), `Tier_4` ($``T_4``$, $``\mathcal{P}_{\text{port}}``$), `Tier_5` ($``T_5``$, $``\mathcal{I}_{\text{safety}}``$), `Tier_6` ($``T_6``$, $``\mathcal{M}_{\text{code}}``$), and `Tier_7` ($``T_7``$, $``\mathcal{W}_{\text{verify}}``$). Unpartitioned or multi-tier entities emit `E0402`.
2. **Strict Adjacent Edges**: Edges $``(u, v) \in E_{\text{trace}}``$ MUST satisfy $``\tau_V(v) = \tau_V(u) + 1``$ within $``E_{\text{trace}} \subseteq \bigcup_{i=1}^6 (T_i \times T_{i+1})``$. Skip-tier, backward, and intra-tier edges emit `E0400`.
3. **Typing & Acyclicity**: Edges bind relations: `Refines`, `Allocates`, `Exposes`, `Guards`, `Enforces`, `Witnesses`. Monotonic tier steps guarantee acyclicity; cycles or conflicts emit `E0400`.
4. **Bidirectional Traversal**: Adjacency $\text{Adj}^+$ and transposed $\text{Adj}^-$ support forward $\text{Reach}^+$ and backward $\text{Reach}^-$ sweeps, indexing verification threads $``\langle v_1, \dots, v_7 \rangle``$.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Traceability graph partitioning, edge relations, and reachability satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
G_{\text{trace}}=\langle V_{\text{trace}},E_{\text{trace}},\mathcal{T}_{\text{tiers}},\tau_V,\tau_E,\Delta_V,\Delta_E,\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\rangle,\quad V_{\text{trace}}=\biguplus_{i=1}^7 T_i \\
\mathcal{T}_{\text{tiers}}=\langle\mathcal{R}_{\text{mission}},\mathcal{R}_{\text{system}},\mathcal{C}_{\text{arch}},\mathcal{P}_{\text{port}},\mathcal{I}_{\text{safety}},\mathcal{M}_{\text{code}},\mathcal{W}_{\text{verify}}\rangle \\
E_{\text{trace}}\subseteq\bigcup_{i=1}^6(T_i\times T_{i+1})\iff\forall(u,v)\in E_{\text{trace}}:\tau_V(v)=\tau_V(u)+1 \\
\forall(u,v)\in E_{\text{trace}}:\tau_E(u,v)\in\{\text{Refines},\text{Allocates},\text{Exposes},\text{Guards},\text{Enforces},\text{Witnesses}\} \\
\forall u\in V_{\text{trace}}:\neg\exists\text{Path}(u,u),\quad\text{Reach}^+(u)=\{v\in V_{\text{trace}}\mid\exists\text{Path}(u,v)\} \\
\Delta_V(v)=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(v)),\quad\Delta_E(e)=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(u)\mathbin{\Vert}\text{"::"}\mathbin{\Vert}\text{FQN}(v)) \\
(\tau_V(u)\notin\{1,\dots,7\}\lor u\in T_i\cap T_j)\implies\text{Diagnostic}(u,\text{E0402})\land u\gets\bot_{\text{node}} \\
(\tau_V(v)\ne\tau_V(u)+1\lor\text{Conflict}(e_1,e_2))\implies\text{Diagnostic}(e,\text{E0400})\land e\gets\bot_{\text{edge}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199})
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial Time)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(|V| + |E|)$ for construction, validation, and traversal; work-stealing bounds to $``O((|V| + |E|) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Avoids NP-4 (REQ-0135) explosion by validating concrete bipartite tiers without combinatorial search.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical 7-Tier Verification Thread Construction
- **Given:** `Package_0` with 7-tier chain from `Req_Mission_0` (`Tier_1`) to `Witness_0` (`Tier_7`).
- **When:** Lowering `TraceabilityGraph_Alpha` and indexing adjacency.
- **Then:** Validates 6 adjacent relations and indexes verification thread without diagnostics.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Non-Adjacent Edge & Structural Acyclicity Rejection
- **Given:** Model with skip-tier edge (`Req_Mission_0` to `Classifier_Alpha`) or backward edge (`Tier_6` to `Tier_3`).
- **When:** Validating edge tier step $``\tau_V(v) - \tau_V(u) \ne 1``$.
- **Then:** Rejects edge, binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{edge}}``$, emitting `E0400`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` (InvalidTraceabilityEdgeTier).

### AC-03: Disjoint Tier Partitioning & Multi-Tier Rejection
- **Given:** Node declared in conflicting tiers (`Tier_3` and `Tier_7`), or lacking tier mapping.
- **When:** Evaluating vertex tier assignment during ingestion.
- **Then:** Rejects node, binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{node}}``$, emitting `E0402`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0402` (ConflictingTierAssignment).

### AC-04: Delimiter Recovery & Diagnostic Capacity Halt
- **Given:** Model declaring 5 invalid edges separated by delimiters (`;`, `}`).
- **When:** Parsing malformed statements with delimiter recovery.
- **Then:** Resynchronizes on delimiters, records `E0400` in $``\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}``$, halting with `E0199` at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400`, `E0199`.
