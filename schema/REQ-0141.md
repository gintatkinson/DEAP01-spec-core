## 1. Normative Statement

The safety compiler SHALL synthesize Run-Time Assurance (RTA) Simplex and Duplex patterns into verified control structures.

---

## 2. Formal Invariant

The synthesized RTA architecture, arbitration laws, and diagnostic trigger rules satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\text{RTA}=\langle\Pi,C_{\text{adv}},C_{\text{rec}},\mathcal{M}_{\text{safe}},\mathcal{S}_{\text{switch}},\mathcal{S}_{\text{safe}},\mathcal{S}_{\text{recover}},\Delta_{\text{rta}}\rangle \\
\Pi=\text{Simplex}\implies\mathbf{u}(t)=\begin{cases}\mathbf{u}_{\text{adv}}(t),&\text{if }\mathcal{M}(\mathbf{x}(t))\equiv\text{Safe}\\\mathbf{u}_{\text{rec}}(t),&\text{if }\mathcal{M}(\mathbf{x}(t))\equiv\text{Unsafe}\end{cases} \\
\Pi=\text{Duplex}\implies\mathbf{u}(t)=\begin{cases}\mathbf{u}_A(t),&\text{if }\mathcal{M}(\mathbf{x}(t))\equiv\text{Safe}\land\|\mathbf{u}_A(t)-\mathbf{u}_B(t)\|\le\epsilon_{\text{discrepancy}}\\\mathbf{u}_{\text{rec}}(t),&\text{otherwise}\end{cases} \\
\mathcal{S}_{\text{safe}}=\{\mathbf{x}\in\mathbb{R}^n\mid\bigwedge_{j=1}^k h_j(\mathbf{x})\ge 0\},\quad\mathcal{S}_{\text{recover}}=\{\mathbf{x}_0\in\mathcal{S}_{\text{safe}}\mid\forall t\ge 0,\,\phi(t;\mathbf{x}_0,\mathbf{u}_{\text{rec}})\in\mathcal{S}_{\text{safe}}\} \\
\mathcal{M}(\mathbf{x}(t))=\begin{cases}\text{Safe},&\text{if }\mathbf{x}(t)\in\mathcal{S}_{\text{recover}}\land\forall j,\,h_j(\mathbf{x}(t))\ge\delta_{\text{margin}}\\\text{Unsafe},&\text{otherwise}\end{cases} \\
(C_{\text{rec}}=\bot\lor\mathcal{M}_{\text{safe}}=\bot\lor\text{ObsPorts}(\mathcal{M}_{\text{safe}})=\emptyset)\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0400})\land\text{RTA}\gets\bot_{\text{rta}} \\
(\exists\mathbf{u}\in\text{Range}(C_{\text{rec}}):\mathbf{u}\notin[u_{\min},u_{\max}])\implies\text{Diagnostic}(C_{\text{rec}},\text{E0440}) \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199}),\quad\Delta_{\text{rta}}=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(\text{RTA}))
\end{aligned}
```

| $``[u_{\min}, u_{\max}], \bot_{\text{rta}}, \Delta_{\text{rta}}``$ | Saturation domain, error sentinel, and UUIDv5 anchor |

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial Time).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $``O(|N_{\text{sub}}|)``$ architectural synthesis and lowering for $``|N_{\text{sub}}| = |C_{\text{adv}}| + |C_{\text{rec}}| + |\mathcal{M}_{\text{safe}}| + |\mathcal{S}_{\text{switch}}| + k + m``$ elements and flows. Work-stealing pool uses $``O(|N_{\text{sub}}| / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$. Space bound is $``O(|N_{\text{sub}}|)``$ AST node allocations.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** N/A (Deterministic P). Synthesis executes in linear time, avoiding NP-4 (REQ-0135) and NP-8 (REQ-0197) search.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical Simplex & Duplex Pattern Synthesis
- **Given:** Package `Package_0` declaring `AdvancedController_0`, `RecoveryController_1`, `SafetyMonitor_Alpha`, and `SwitchingLogic_0`.
- **When:** Synthesizing the RTA Simplex or Duplex pattern.
- **Then:** Emits composite RTA block with arbitrated ports, fan-out flows, and zero diagnostics.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Boundary & Envelope Protection Margin Trip
- **Given:** State $\mathbf{x}(t)$ with $``h_j(\mathbf{x}(t)) < \delta_{\text{margin}}``$ or Duplex discrepancy $``\|\mathbf{u}_A - \mathbf{u}_B\| > \epsilon_{\text{discrepancy}}``$.
- **When:** `SafetyMonitor_Alpha` evaluates feedback state.
- **Then:** Monitor asserts $\text{Unsafe}$ and `SwitchingLogic_0` transfers authority to `RecoveryController_1`.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Missing Subcomponent or Output Saturation Rejection
- **Given:** RTA block missing recovery controller ($``C_{\text{rec}} = \bot``$) or output exceeding $``[u_{\min}, u_{\max}]``$.
- **When:** Semantic envelope checks execute.
- **Then:** Rejects block, binds $``\bot_{\text{rta}}``$, and emits diagnostic `E0400` or `E0440`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` (MissingRecoveryController) or `E0440` (RecoveryOutputEnvelopeViolation).

### AC-04: Delimiter Recovery & Error Sink Saturation
- **Given:** Malformed declarations separated by statement delimiters (`;`, `}`).
- **When:** Ingestion encounters syntax or type faults.
- **Then:** Records diagnostics in $``\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}``$, recovers on delimiters, and halts with `E0199` at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0199` (ErrorSinkOverflow).
