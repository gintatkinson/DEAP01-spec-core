## 1. Normative Statement

The metrology engine SHALL validate conservative interfaces using dual conjugate variable pairs $(e, f)$ whose dimensional product evaluates to universal Power $``\mathbf{d}_{\text{Power}} = (1, 2, -3, 0, 0, 0, 0) \in \mathbb{Q}^7``$.

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Conjugate Declaration**: Conservative elements declare pairs $(e, f) \in \mathbb{Q}^7 \times \mathbb{Q}^7$ of Across effort $e$ and Through flow $f$.
2. **Power Invariant**: Addition requires $``\mathbf{d}(e) + \mathbf{d}(f) \equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{Power}}``$. Mismatches emit `E0301`; zero denominators emit `E0300`.
3. **Domain Closure**: Abstract pairs (`Port_Alpha`, `Effort_Var`, `Flow_Var`) verify closure in $\mathbb{Q}^7$: Potential, Dynamic, Rotational, and Volumetric pairs each sum to $``\mathbf{d}_{\text{Power}}``$.
4. **Interconnect Compatibility**: Connecting ports $``P_1, P_2``$ requires $``\mathbf{d}(e_{P_1}) \equiv \mathbf{d}(e_{P_2})``$ and $``\mathbf{d}(f_{P_1}) \equiv \mathbf{d}(f_{P_2})``$, synthesizing $``f_{P_1} + f_{P_2} = 0``$. Mismatches emit `E0300`.
5. **Constitutive Homogeneity**: Parameters satisfy $\mathbf{d}(R) = \mathbf{d}(e) - \mathbf{d}(f)$, $``\mathbf{d}(C) = \mathbf{d}(f) + \mathbf{e}_T - \mathbf{d}(e)``$, and $``\mathbf{d}(I) = \mathbf{d}(e) + \mathbf{e}_T - \mathbf{d}(f)``$. Mismatches emit `E0300`. Non-conservative interfaces bypass checks.

\forall C=\langle e,f\rangle\in\mathcal{C}_{\text{pair}}, &\quad \mathbf{d}(e)+\mathbf{d}(f)\equiv\mathbf{d}_{\text{Power}} \\
(P_1,P_2)\in\mathcal{E}_{\text{conn}}\implies(\mathbf{d}(e_{P_1})\equiv\mathbf{d}(e_{P_2}) &\land \mathbf{d}(f_{P_1})\equiv\mathbf{d}(f_{P_2}) \land f_{P_1}+f_{P_2}=0) \\
\mathbf{d}(R)=\mathbf{d}(e)-\mathbf{d}(f), \quad \mathbf{d}(C)=\mathbf{d}(f)+\mathbf{e}_T-\mathbf{d}(e), &\quad \mathbf{d}(I)=\mathbf{d}(e)+\mathbf{e}_T-\mathbf{d}(f) \\

- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Rational addition terminates in $O(1)$ Euclidean $\gcd$ steps, bypassing NP search (NP-3 metrological fragment). Work-stealing scales as $``O(N / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical Power Invariant & Conjugate Lowering
- **Given:** Port `Port_Alpha` with `Effort_Var` ($(1,2,-3,-1,0,0,0)$) and `Flow_Var` ($(0,0,0,1,0,0,0)$).
- **When:** Lowering checks dimensional product.
- **Then:** Sum evaluates to $``\mathbf{d}_{\text{Power}}``$ with empty diagnostic sink.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Conservative Interconnect Compatibility
- **Given:** Ports `Port_Alpha` and `Port_Beta` sharing identical effort and flow dimensions.
- **When:** Interconnect validation and continuity synthesis execute.
- **Then:** Dimensions match, $``f_{\text{Port\_Alpha}} + f_{\text{Port\_Beta}} = 0``$ holds, and sink remains empty.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Conjugate Power Product Violation Rejection
- **Given:** Conservative port `Port_Alpha` declaring dimensions summing to $``(1,1,-1,0,0,0,0) \ne \mathbf{d}_{\text{Power}}``$.
- **When:** Conjugate invariant validation executes.
- **Then:** Rejection emits `E0301`, assigning sentinel $``\bot_{\text{pair}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0301`.

### AC-04: Port Dimensional Mismatch & Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Connection with mismatched effort dimensions before delimiter (`;`) and valid `Port_Gamma`.
- **When:** Semantic validation and delimiter recovery execute.
- **Then:** Rejection emits `E0300`, recovers on delimiter, parses `Port_Gamma`, and halts at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300`, `E0199`.
