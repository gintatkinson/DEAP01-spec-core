## 1. Normative Statement

Subsystem 10 SHALL synthesize formal verification scripts (`.m`) targeting verification engines (MATLAB / SLDV) from AST classifiers (`Classifier_Alpha`) via `ProofSynthesizer_Alpha`.

---

## 2. Formal Invariant

Classifiers $\mathcal{C}$ and invariants $``\Psi_{\text{invariants}}``$ satisfy script synthesis invariants:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\forall C\in\mathcal{C},\quad\mathcal{S}_{\text{script}}(C)&=\langle\text{id}_S,\text{name}_S,\mathcal{O}_{\text{proof}},\mathcal{P}_{\text{param}},\Delta_S\rangle \\
\forall\psi\in\Psi_{\text{invariants}},\quad\omega(\psi)&=\langle\text{id}_{\text{inv}},C,\text{Parenthesize}(\psi),\kappa_{\text{obj}},\Delta_{\omega}\rangle \\
\text{Parenthesize}(\psi)&=\begin{cases}
c,&\psi=\text{Literal}(c) \\
v,&\psi=\text{Identifier}(v) \\
(\odot\,\text{Parenthesize}(\psi_1)),&\psi=\text{UnaryOp}(\odot,\psi_1) \\
(\text{Parenthesize}(\psi_1)\odot\text{Parenthesize}(\psi_2)),&\psi=\text{BinaryOp}(\odot,\psi_1,\psi_2)
\end{cases} \\
\text{Emit}(\omega)&=\begin{cases}
\text{sldv.assert}(\text{Parenthesize}(\psi),\text{id}_{\text{inv}}),&\kappa_{\text{obj}}=\text{SLDV\_ASSERT} \\
\text{sldv.assume}(\text{Parenthesize}(\psi),\text{id}_{\text{inv}}),&\kappa_{\text{obj}}=\text{SLDV\_ASSUME} \\
\text{sldv.testobjective}(\text{Parenthesize}(\psi),\text{id}_{\text{inv}}),&\kappa_{\text{obj}}=\text{SLDV\_TESTOBJECTIVE}
\end{cases} \\
\forall\psi\in\Psi_{\text{invariants}},&\quad\text{AST}(\psi)\cong\text{AST}(\text{Parse}_m(\text{Emit}(\omega(\psi)))) \\
(\text{HasTypeMismatch}(\psi)\lor\text{Unresolved}(\psi))&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\psi,\text{E0300})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land\omega\gets\bot_{\text{proof}} \\
(\neg(\text{AST}(\psi)\cong\text{AST}(\text{Parse}_m(\text{Emit}(\omega))))\lor v_{\min}\gt v_{\max})&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\psi,\text{E0513})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land\omega\gets\bot_{\text{proof}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
\Delta_S=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(C)),&\quad\Delta_{\omega}=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(C)\mathbin{\Vert}\text{"::"}\mathbin{\Vert}\text{id}_{\text{inv}})
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial Time).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** Obligation synthesis runs in asymptotic bound $``O(|\Psi_{\text{invariants}}|)``$ linear script generation time. Node checks take $O(1)$. Streaming chunks requires $``O(N_{\text{bytes}})``$. Work-stealing scales as $``O((|\Psi_{\text{invariants}}| + N_{\text{bytes}}) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$. Arena footprint is $``O(|V_{\text{AST}}| + N_{\text{bytes}})``$.
- **Tractable Subclass and Frontier:** N/A (Deterministic P). Bypasses NP-1 intervals (REQ-0117), NP-5 model checking (REQ-0124), and NP-8 solving (REQ-0197).

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Obligation Synthesis and Precedence Preservation
- **Given:** `Classifier_Alpha` with invariant `Invariant_0` expression `(a + b * c) <= d`.
- **When:** Synthesizing script `verify_Classifier_Alpha.m`.
- **Then:** Emits `sldv.assert` with parenthesized `((a) + ((b) * (c))) <= (d)`, preserving precedence.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Operational Envelope Boundary Verification
- **Given:** `Classifier_Alpha` with envelope $[0.0, 100.0]$ on `param_x : Real`.
- **When:** Synthesizing assumption `Obligation_0`.
- **Then:** Emits `sldv.assume` bounds `((param_x >= 0.0) and (param_x <= 100.0))`.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Inverted Envelope Bound Rejection
- **Given:** `Classifier_Alpha` with inverted envelope $[100.0, 0.0]$ ($``v_{\min} > v_{\max}``$).
- **When:** Validating envelope interval bounds.
- **Then:** Halts emission, binds $``\bot_{\text{proof}}``$, and records `E0513`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0513`.

### AC-04: Type Mismatch and Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** `Classifier_Alpha` with type mismatch in `Predicate_1`, delimiter `;`, and `Invariant_2`.
- **When:** Detecting type mismatch and resynchronizing across `;`.
- **Then:** Emits `E0300`, binds $``\bot_{\text{proof}}``$, parses `Invariant_2`, halting at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300`, `E0199`.
