## 1. Normative Statement

The compiler SHALL bootstrap `Package_SI` and `Package_SIPrefixes` into root scope, lowering `UnitDefinition` and `PrefixDefinition` into immutable AST nodes.

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Specialization**: `UnitDefinition` specializes `MeasurementUnit` ($``\text{UnitDef} \le \text{MeasurementUnit}``$); `PrefixDefinition` specializes `Prefix`. Incompatible specializations emit `E0200`.
2. **Base Units**: Basis $``\mathcal{B} = \{\text{kg},\text{m},\text{s},\text{A},\text{K},\text{mol},\text{cd}\}``$ defines lattice $``\mathbb{Z}^7``$ ($c = 1, o = 0$, $``\text{is\_ratio} = \text{true}``$).
3. **Derived Units**: Coherent units are power products ($c = 1, o = 0$); non-coherent units bind $c \in \mathbb{Q}^+$; affine units define offset $o \in \mathbb{Q}$.
4. **Metric Prefixes**: Prefixes scale $c$ to $10^k \cdot c$ for $k \in \{-24, \dots, 24\}$ in exact arithmetic $\mathbb{Q}$.
5. **Commensurability**: Conversions require identical dimensions ($``\mathbf{d}(U_1) = \mathbf{d}(U_2)``$); mismatches emit `E0201`; cycles emit `E0203`.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Dimensional lattice and conversion invariants:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\forall U \in \mathcal{U},\; &\mathbf{d}(U) \in \mathbb{Z}^7,\; c_U \in \mathbb{Q}^+,\; o_U \in \mathbb{Q} \\
\mathcal{B}=\{\text{kg},\text{m},\text{s},\text{A},\text{K},\text{mol},\text{cd}\},\; &\mathbf{d}(B_i)=\mathbf{e}_i,\; c_{B_i}=1,\; o_{B_i}=0 \\
\text{Prefix}(k, U) \implies &\mathbf{d}(P \cdot U)=\mathbf{d}(U),\; c_{P \cdot U}=10^k \cdot c_U \\
U_1 \sim U_2 \iff \mathbf{d}(U_1)=\mathbf{d}(U_2),\; &\text{convert}(v, U_1, U_2)=\frac{c_{U_1} \cdot v + o_{U_1} - o_{U_2}}{c_{U_2}} \\
\forall U \in \mathcal{U},\; (U, U) \notin \mathcal{R}_{\text{unit\_dep}}^+,\; &\Delta_n = \text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(n))
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(1)$ conversion; bootstrapping is $``O(N_U + N_P)``$; cycle detection is $``O(|\mathcal{U}| + |\mathcal{R}_{\text{unit\_dep}}|)``$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Meet-semilattice subsumption evaluates in $O(1)$ time, bypassing NP search (NP-3). Work-stealing scales as $``O((N_U + N_P + K) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical SI Base & Derived Unit Bootstrapping
- **Given:** Compiler loading `Package_SI`.
- **When:** Resolving base and derived units.
- **Then:** Base units lower with $c = 1, o = 0$ and basis $``\mathbf{e}_i \in \mathbb{Z}^7``$; derived units preserve power products.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Metric Prefix Scaling & Exact Rational Conversion
- **Given:** Unit `Unit_Alpha` ($c \in \mathbb{Q}^+$) and prefix `Prefix_Alpha` ($10^k$).
- **When:** Lowering `Prefix_Alpha Unit_Alpha` and evaluating conversion.
- **Then:** Factor evaluates to $10^k \cdot c$ in exact arithmetic $\mathbb{Q}$ without drift.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Incommensurable Unit Conversion Rejection
- **Given:** Incommensurable `Unit_Alpha` and `Unit_Beta` ($``\mathbf{d}(\text{Unit\_Alpha}) \ne \mathbf{d}(\text{Unit\_Beta})``$).
- **When:** Dimensional validation executes.
- **Then:** Lowering halts conversion, assigns sentinel $``\bot_{\text{unit}}``$, and records `E0201`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0201`.

### AC-04: Malformed Unit Specialization & Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Malformed `unit def Unit_Alpha :> Classifier_Alpha;` preceding valid `unit def Unit_Beta;`.
- **When:** Delimiter recovery executes.
- **Then:** Lowering rejects `Unit_Alpha` with `E0200`, recovers on delimiter, and halts at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0200`, `E0199`.
