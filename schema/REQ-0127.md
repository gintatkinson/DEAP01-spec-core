## 1. Normative Statement

The dynamics engine SHALL provide a deterministic stepper (`SimulationStepper_Alpha`) and trace logger (`TraceLog_1`) stepping statecharts against event traces.

Stepping MUST enforce:
1. **Configuration**: Tracks $``C = \langle \mathcal{S}_{\text{active}}, \mathcal{V}_{\text{env}}, \mathcal{Q}_{\text{events}}, t_{\text{sim}}, \kappa_{\text{step}} \rangle``$ from root $``s_0``$. Ancestor closure or XOR breaches emit `E0402`.
2. **Transition Selection**: Dequeued $e$ evaluates guards $``g_T``$. Conflicts resolve by depth, priority $\pi(T)$, and FQN order. Guard faults emit `E0400`.
3. **LCA Traversal**: Firing $T^*$ executes bottom-up exits to $``\text{LCA}(s_{\text{src}}, s_{\text{tgt}})``$, effect $``A_{\text{trans}}``$, and top-down entries. Spontaneous $\epsilon$-steps loop to quiescence; exceeding $``\kappa_{\text{micro\_max}}``$ emits `E0400`.
4. **Trace Logging**: Emits JSON trace (`TraceLog_1`) recording steps, timestamps, configurations, events, guards, firings, actions, and status, guaranteeing bitwise determinism.
5. **Halting**: Unmatched events record `Status::Quiescent`, preserving $C$. Final states with empty queues halt simulation (`Status::Terminal`).

## 2. Formal Invariant

Statechart simulation stepping, configuration invariants, and diagnostic mappings satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{M}=\langle\mathcal{S},\mathcal{T},\mathcal{E},\mathcal{A},s_0,\mathcal{S}_{\text{final}},\sqsubset,\pi\rangle,\quad C=\langle\mathcal{S}_{\text{active}},\mathcal{V}_{\text{env}},\mathcal{Q}_{\text{events}},t_{\text{sim}},\kappa_{\text{step}}\rangle \\
C_0=\langle\text{InitActive}(s_0),\mathcal{V}_0,\mathcal{Q}_0,0.0,0\rangle,\quad s_0\in\mathcal{S}_{\text{active}}\land\text{ancestors}(s_0)\subseteq\mathcal{S}_{\text{active}} \\
\text{Enabled}(C,e)=\{T\in\mathcal{T}\mid s_{\text{src}}(T)\in\mathcal{S}_{\text{active}}\land e_{\text{trig}}(T)=e\land\text{Eval}(g_T,\mathcal{V}_{\text{env}})\equiv\text{true}\} \\
T^*=\arg\max_{T\in\text{Enabled}(C,e)}\langle\text{Depth}(s_{\text{src}}(T)),\pi(T),-\text{FQN}(T)\rangle \\
\mathcal{S}'_{\text{active}}=(\mathcal{S}_{\text{active}}\setminus\text{ExitPath}(s_{\text{src}}(T^*),s_{\text{tgt}}(T^*)))\cup\text{EntryPath}(s_{\text{src}}(T^*),s_{\text{tgt}}(T^*)) \\
\text{Step}(C,e)=\langle C',\sigma\rangle,\quad \sigma=\langle\kappa_{\text{step}},t_{\text{sim}},\mathcal{S}_{\text{active}},e,T^*,A_{\text{exec}},\mathcal{S}'_{\text{active}},\text{status}\rangle \\
\text{Trace}(\vec{e})=\langle\sigma_1,\dots,\sigma_m\rangle,\quad \vec{e}_1=\vec{e}_2\implies\mathcal{J}(\text{Trace}(\vec{e}_1))=\mathcal{J}(\text{Trace}(\vec{e}_2)) \\
(\text{Eval}(g_T,\mathcal{V}_{\text{env}})=\bot_{\text{eval}}\lor\kappa_{\text{micro}}\gt\kappa_{\text{micro\_max}})\implies\text{Diagnostic}(T,\text{E0400})\land\sigma\gets\bot_{\text{step}} \\
(\text{ancestors}(s)\not\subseteq\mathcal{S}_{\text{active}}\lor\text{LCA}(s_{\text{src}},s_{\text{tgt}})=\bot)\implies\text{Diagnostic}(s,\text{E0402})\land s\gets\bot_{\text{state}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199}),\quad \Delta_\sigma=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(\mathcal{M})\mathbin{\Vert}\text{String}(\kappa))
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(m \cdot D)$ trace step generation for trace length $m$ and depth $D \le 32$; transitions take $``O(\Delta_{\max})``$, actions take $O(|A|)$, streaming takes $O(m)$ bytes. Work-stealing: $``O((m \cdot D) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Avoids NP-5 (REQ-0124) explosion by stepping concrete paths without state unrolling.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical Stepping & Trace Generation
- **Given:** `Package_0` declaring `SimulationStepper_Alpha` stepping `State_Alpha` $\to$ `State_Beta` $\to$ `State_Gamma`.
- **When:** Stepper processes event sequence $``\vec{e} = \langle \text{Event\_0}, \text{Event\_1} \rangle``$.
- **Then:** Completes 2 macro-steps, emitting `TraceLog_1` with exact configurations and `Status::Success`.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Hierarchical LCA Exit/Entry & Action Ordering
- **Given:** Composite `State_Comp_0` with `State_Child_0`, stepping from `State_Alpha` to `State_Gamma`.
- **When:** Stepping transitions across composite boundaries.
- **Then:** `TraceLog_1` logs top-down entry and bottom-up exit, maintaining ancestor closure.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Guard Evaluation Fault & Micro-Step Limit Rejection
- **Given:** Invalid guard (`param_x / 0.0 > 1.0`) or cyclic $\epsilon$-transitions.
- **When:** Evaluating candidate guards or completion micro-steps.
- **Then:** Traps fault or limit $``\kappa_{\text{micro}} > \kappa_{\text{micro\_max}}``$, emitting `E0400` and binding $``\bot_{\text{step}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` (SimulationGuardEvaluationFault).

### AC-04: Hierarchy Containment Violation & Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Active configuration violating XOR singularity before `;`, followed by a valid step.
- **When:** Validating configurations and performing delimiter recovery.
- **Then:** Emits `E0402`, binds $``\bot_{\text{state}}``$, recovers on `;`, halting at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0402`, `E0199`.
