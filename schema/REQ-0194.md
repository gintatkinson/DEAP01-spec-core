## 1. Normative Statement

Subsystem 11 SHALL detect drift and enforce zero-divergence bijective synchronization between schemas and artifacts via `DriftDetector_Alpha` and `ModelSync_0`.

---

## 2. Formal Invariant

Bijective synchronization, zero-divergence equivalence, and diagnostic triggers satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{A}_{\text{schema}}=\langle\mathcal{V}_{\text{schema}},\mathcal{E}_{\text{schema}},\mathcal{P}_{\text{schema}},\Delta_{\text{schema}}\rangle,&\quad\mathcal{A}_{\text{artifacts}}=\langle\mathcal{V}_{\text{artifacts}},\mathcal{E}_{\text{artifacts}},\mathcal{P}_{\text{artifacts}},\Delta_{\text{artifacts}}\rangle \\
\text{Drift}(\mathcal{A}_{\text{schema}}, \mathcal{A}_{\text{artifacts}})=0&\iff\mathcal{A}_{\text{schema}}\cong\mathcal{A}_{\text{artifacts}} \\
\exists\psi:\mathcal{V}_{\text{schema}}\leftrightarrow\mathcal{V}_{\text{artifacts}}\quad\text{s.t.}&\quad|\mathcal{V}_{\text{schema}}|=|\mathcal{V}_{\text{artifacts}}| \\
\forall u,v\in\mathcal{V}_{\text{schema}},\forall\tau\in\mathcal{T}_{\text{rel}},\quad(u,v,\tau)\in\mathcal{E}_{\text{schema}}&\iff(\psi(u),\psi(v),\tau)\in\mathcal{E}_{\text{artifacts}} \\
\forall u\in\mathcal{V}_{\text{schema}},\quad\mathcal{P}_{\text{schema}}(u)=\mathcal{P}_{\text{artifacts}}(\psi(u))&\land\Delta_{\text{schema}}(u)=\Delta_{\text{artifacts}}(\psi(u)) \\
\Delta(u)&=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(u)\mathbin{\Vert}\text{"::Node"}) \\
\text{Drift}(\mathcal{A}_{\text{schema}},\mathcal{A}_{\text{artifacts}})&=\delta_{\text{nodes}}+\delta_{\text{edges}}+\delta_{\text{props}} \\
\text{Drift}(\mathcal{A}_{\text{schema}},\mathcal{A}_{\text{artifacts}})\gt 0&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(v,\text{E0500})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land v\gets\bot_{\text{drift}} \\
\neg\text{ValidPath}(p)&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(p,\text{E0300})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land p\gets\bot_{\text{drift}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}| \ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial Time).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(|V| + |E|)$ bijective tree isomorphism comparison ($``|V| = |\mathcal{V}_{\text{schema}}| + |\mathcal{V}_{\text{artifacts}}|``$, $``|E| = |\mathcal{E}_{\text{schema}}| + |\mathcal{E}_{\text{artifacts}}|``$). UUIDv5 hashing resolves node mapping in $O(1)$. Work-stealing scales as $``O((|V| + |E|) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$. Arena memory is $O(|V| + |E|)$.
- **Tractable Subclass and Frontier:** N/A (Deterministic P). Pre-assigned UUIDv5 anchors collapse graph isomorphism to linear-time traversal, bypassing NP-1 intervals (REQ-0117) and NP-8 solving (REQ-0197).

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Zero-Divergence Bijective Isomorphism Baseline
- **Given:** Model `Package_0` with `Classifier_Alpha`, `Port_1`, and artifact `Artifact_0`.
- **When:** `DriftDetector_Alpha` executes bijective synchronization via `ModelSync_0`.
- **Then:** Verifies `Bijection_1` ($\text{Drift} == 0$) with identical topology and attributes.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Structural Relational and Property Drift Detection
- **Given:** `Artifact_0` with divergent `param_x : Real = 43.0` (schema: `42.0`) or missing edge.
- **When:** `DriftDetector_Alpha` checks isomorphism against `Package_0`.
- **Then:** Emits `E0500`, binds $``\bot_{\text{drift}}``$, and isolates coordinates without persisting.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0500`.

### AC-03: Workspace Path Validation and Anchor Rejection
- **Given:** Artifact referencing escaping path `../Artifact_Escaping` or ungrounded anchor.
- **When:** `ModelSync_0` validates path confinement and anchor validity.
- **Then:** Fails closed, binds $``\bot_{\text{drift}}``$, and registers `E0300` in $``\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300`.

### AC-04: Delimiter Resynchronization and Error Saturation Limit
- **Given:** Malformed artifact with delimiter `;` and valid `Classifier_Beta`.
- **When:** `DriftDetector_Alpha` parses stream, accumulating diagnostics in $``\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}``$.
- **Then:** Recovers across `;`, parses `Classifier_Beta`, halting at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0500`, `E0199`.
