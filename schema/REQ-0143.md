## 1. Normative Statement

The safety compiler SHALL formulate Control Barrier Function (CBF) forward invariance contracts for critical state constraints, ensuring synthesized and filtered control laws maintain safe set forward invariance.

---

## 2. Formal Invariant

Forward invariance, filter QP, and diagnostics satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{C}=\{\mathbf{x}\in\mathbb{R}^n\mid h(\mathbf{x})\ge 0\},\;\partial\mathcal{C}=\{\mathbf{x}\in\mathbb{R}^n\mid h(\mathbf{x})=0\},\;\forall\mathbf{x}\in\partial\mathcal{C},\nabla h(\mathbf{x})\ne\mathbf{0} \\
L_f h(\mathbf{x})=\nabla h(\mathbf{x})\cdot f(\mathbf{x}),\;L_g h(\mathbf{x})=\nabla h(\mathbf{x})\cdot g(\mathbf{x}) \\
K_{\text{cbf}}(\mathbf{x})=\{\mathbf{u}\in\mathcal{U}_{\text{ctrl}}\mid L_f h(\mathbf{x})+L_g h(\mathbf{x})\mathbf{u}+\alpha(h(\mathbf{x}))\ge 0\},\;\mathbf{x}(0)\in\mathcal{C}\implies\forall t\ge 0,\mathbf{x}(t)\in\mathcal{C} \\
\psi_0(\mathbf{x})=h(\mathbf{x}),\;\psi_i(\mathbf{x})=\dot{\psi}_{i-1}(\mathbf{x})+\alpha_i(\psi_{i-1}(\mathbf{x})),\;\forall i\in\{1,\dots,\gamma\} \\
\mathbf{u}^*(\mathbf{x})=\arg\min_{\mathbf{u}\in\mathcal{U}_{\text{ctrl}}}\frac{1}{2}\|\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{u}_{\text{nom}}(\mathbf{x})\|^2,\;-L_g h(\mathbf{x})\mathbf{u}\le L_f h(\mathbf{x})+\alpha(h(\mathbf{x})) \\
\exists\mathbf{x}\in\mathcal{C}:K_{\text{cbf}}(\mathbf{x})\cap\mathcal{U}_{\text{ctrl}}=\emptyset\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{CBF},\text{E0440})\land\text{CBF}\gets\bot_{\text{cbf}} \\
((\gamma\gt 1\land|\vec{\alpha}|\lt\gamma)\lor\text{NonDiff}(h)\lor f=\bot)\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{CBF},\text{E0400})\land\text{CBF}\gets\bot_{\text{cbf}} \\
\text{UndecidableSMT}(h,f,g)\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{CBF},\text{E0406})\land\text{CBF}\gets\bot_{\text{cbf}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199}),\;\Delta_{\text{cbf}}=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(\text{CBF}))
\end{aligned}
```

| $``L_f h, L_g h, K_{\text{cbf}}``$ | Lie derivatives and safe control half-space |
| $``\psi_i, \gamma, \vec{\alpha}``$ | Auxiliary chain, relative degree, and $``\mathcal{K}_{\infty}``$ sequence |

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (formulation); per-step filtering bounded by QP solver integration $``O(n_{\text{ctrl}}^3)``$.
- **Asymptotic Bound:** Formulation: $``O(N_{\text{cbf}}\cdot(\gamma\cdot n_{\text{state}}+k\cdot n_{\text{ctrl}}))``$. Work-stealing: $``O((N_{\text{cbf}}\cdot(\gamma\cdot n_{\text{state}}+k\cdot n_{\text{ctrl}}))/N_{\text{max\_workers}}+\log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$. Filter QP step: $``O(n_{\text{ctrl}}^3)``$. Space: $``O(N_{\text{cbf}}\cdot(n_{\text{state}}+n_{\text{ctrl}}+k))``$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** N/A (Deterministic P). Operates in polynomial time, avoiding NP-4 (REQ-0135) and NP-8 (REQ-0197) via polynomial Lie derivatives and convex bounds.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical CBF Lowering & QP Filter Formulation
- **Given:** `Package_0` declaring $\dot{\mathbf{x}}=f(\mathbf{x})+g(\mathbf{x})\mathbf{u}$, barrier `BarrierFunction_Alpha` ($\gamma=1$), and `ControlLaw_1`.
- **When:** Lowering CBF contract into filter `ControlFilter_0`.
- **Then:** Synthesizes $``L_f h, L_g h``$ and QP system $``A_{\text{cbf}}\mathbf{u}\le\mathbf{b}_{\text{cbf}}``$ in arena.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Actuator Saturation Infeasibility Rejection
- **Given:** `Package_0` with safe set `SafeSet_0` where $``K_{\text{cbf}}(\mathbf{x})\cap\mathcal{U}_{\text{ctrl}}=\emptyset``$.
- **When:** Checking constraint polytope feasibility on boundary $\partial\mathcal{C}$.
- **Then:** Aborts filter lowering, binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{cbf}}``$, and emits `E0440`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0440` (ActuatorSaturationConflict).

### AC-03: Relative Degree Mismatch & Undecidable Barrier Rejection
- **Given:** `Package_0` with barrier $\gamma=2$ lacking auxiliary chain, or undecidable SMT dynamics.
- **When:** Validating barrier relative degree and decidability.
- **Then:** Rejects barrier, binds $``\bot_{\text{cbf}}``$, and emits `E0400` or `E0406`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` / `E0406`.

### AC-04: Delimiter Resynchronization & Error Recovery
- **Given:** Malformed barrier missing dynamics before `;`, followed by valid `BarrierFunction_Alpha`.
- **When:** Parser encounters syntax fault and resynchronizes.
- **Then:** Emits `E0400`, recovers on `;`, and halts at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400`, `E0199`.
