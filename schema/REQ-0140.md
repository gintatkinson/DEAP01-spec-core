## 1. Normative Statement

The safety compiler SHALL compute Risk Priority Numbers ($RPN$), evaluating integer scores and mitigation gates.

`RPN_Engine_0` MUST enforce:
1. **Ratings**: Ingests modes $``\mathcal{F} = \{ f_1, \dots, f_N \}``$ (`FailureMode_Alpha`) in `Package_0`. Modes MUST declare $\langle S(f), O(f), D(f) \rangle \in [1, 10]^3 \cap \mathbb{Z}^+$. Invalid ratings emit `E0400`.
2. **Scoring**: Evaluates $RPN(f) = S(f) \times O(f) \times D(f) \in [1, 1000]$ via exact integer arithmetic.
3. **Criticality**: Evaluates $\text{IsCritical}(f) \iff (S(f) \ge 9 \lor RPN(f) \ge 200)$, enforcing severity primacy ($S \ge 9$) and product thresholding ($RPN \ge 200$). Sets $\text{AP}(f) \in \{ \text{High}, \text{Medium}, \text{Low} \}$.
4. **Mitigation**: Critical modes ($\text{IsCritical}(f) \equiv \text{true}$) MUST bind constraint $``\mathbf{C}_{\text{mitig}}(f) \ne \emptyset``$ (`Constraint_0`). Unmitigated modes emit `E0400`.
5. **Ordering**: Sorts $\mathcal{F}$ under total order $``\succ_{\text{RPN}}``$ by $RPN \downarrow, S \downarrow, O \downarrow, D \downarrow$, and $\text{FQN}(f) \uparrow$, guaranteeing bitwise parity.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Rating bounds, RPN integer scoring, criticality gating, and diagnostic triggers satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\forall f\in\mathcal{F},\quad S(f),O(f),D(f)\in[1,10]\cap\mathbb{Z}^+,\quad RPN(f)=S(f)\cdot O(f)\cdot D(f)\in[1,1000] \\
\text{IsCritical}(f)\iff(S(f)\ge 9\lor RPN(f)\ge 200),\quad \text{IsCritical}(f)\implies\mathbf{C}_{\text{mitig}}(f)\ne\emptyset \\
\text{AP}(f)=\begin{cases}\text{High},&S(f)\ge 9\lor(S(f)\ge 7\land O(f)\ge 6\land D(f)\ge 5)\lor RPN(f)\ge 200\\\text{Medium},&\neg(\text{AP}(f)=\text{High})\land((S(f)\ge 6\land O(f)\ge 4)\lor RPN(f)\ge 80)\\\text{Low},&\text{otherwise}\end{cases} \\
f_a\succ_{\text{RPN}} f_b\iff\langle RPN(f_a),S(f_a),O(f_a),D(f_a)\rangle\gt_{\text{lex}}\langle RPN(f_b),S(f_b),O(f_b),D(f_b)\rangle \\
\lor(\langle RPN(f_a),S(f_a),O(f_a),D(f_a)\rangle=\langle RPN(f_b),S(f_b),O(f_b),D(f_b)\rangle\land\text{FQN}(f_a)\lt_{\text{lex}}\text{FQN}(f_b)) \\
(S(f)\notin[1,10]\lor O(f)\notin[1,10]\lor D(f)\notin[1,10])\implies\text{Diagnostic}(f,\text{E0400})\land f\gets\bot_{\text{rpn}} \\
(\text{IsCritical}(f)\land\mathbf{C}_{\text{mitig}}(f)=\emptyset)\implies\text{Diagnostic}(f,\text{E0400})\land f\gets\bot_{\text{rpn}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199}),\quad\Delta_f=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(f)\mathbin{\Vert}\text{"::RPN"})
\end{aligned}
```

| Symbol / Term | Domain / Formal Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| $\mathcal{F}, f$ | Failure modes and mode element |
| $S, O, D, RPN$ | Ratings in $[1, 10]$ and product in $[1, 1000]$ |
| $``\text{IsCritical}, \mathbf{C}_{\text{mitig}}``$ | Criticality predicate and mitigating constraints |
| $``\text{AP}, \succ_{\text{RPN}}``$ | Action Priority and Pareto total order |
| $``\bot_{\text{rpn}}, \Delta_f``$ | Sentinel and UUIDv5 anchor |

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial Time).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(|\mathcal{F}|)$ scoring and criticality checks for modes $\mathcal{F}$. Pareto sorting runs in $O(|\mathcal{F}| \log |\mathcal{F}|)$ ($O(|\mathcal{F}|)$ bucket sort). Work-stealing achieves $``O(|\mathcal{F}| / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$. Space: $O(|\mathcal{F}|)$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** N/A (Deterministic P). Scalar arithmetic avoids combinatorial explosion, bypassing NP frontiers and supplying prioritized inputs to NP-4 (REQ-0135).

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical Scoring & Criticality
- **Given:** `Package_0` with `FailureMode_Alpha` ($S=5, O=4, D=3$) and `FailureMode_Beta` ($S=9, O=1, D=1$).
- **When:** `RPN_Engine_0` evaluates scores and criticality.
- **Then:** Yields $RPN=60$ ($\text{AP}=\text{Low}$) and $RPN=9$ ($\text{IsCritical}=\text{true}$, $\text{AP}=\text{High}$).
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Boundary Limits & Mitigation
- **Given:** `Package_0` with `FailureMode_Min` ($S=1, O=1, D=1$) and `FailureMode_Max` ($S=10, O=10, D=10$).
- **When:** Evaluating boundary products and mitigations.
- **Then:** Yields $RPN=1$ and $RPN=1000$ ($\text{IsCritical}=\text{true}$) without error.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Invalid Rating & Unmitigated Critical Rejection
- **Given:** `Package_0` with invalid `FailureMode_Invalid` ($S=11$) and unmitigated `FailureMode_Unmitigated` ($S=10, O=5, D=5$).
- **When:** Validating rating domains and mitigation sets.
- **Then:** Rejects input, binds $``\bot_{\text{rpn}}``$, and emits `E0400`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400` (RatingDomainViolation).

### AC-04: Delimiter Recovery & Error Saturation
- **Given:** Malformed declarations ($S=4.5$, missing $D$) separated by delimiters (`;`).
- **When:** Ingestion encounters syntax or domain errors.
- **Then:** Emits `E0400`, recovers on `;`, and halts at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400`, `E0199`.
