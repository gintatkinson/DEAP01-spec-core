## 1. Normative Statement

The temporal solver SHALL enforce consistency over lifecycles and interval constraints.

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Formulation**: Lifecycles $o \in \mathcal{O}$ define $``I(o) = [t_{\text{start}}, t_{\text{end}}]``$. Constraints $``C_{ij}``$ bind masks $``\mathbf{m}_{R(i, j)} \in [0, 8191]``$ over $``\mathcal{B}_{13}``$ with symmetry $``R(o_j, o_i) \equiv (R(o_i, o_j))^\smile``$.
2. **ORD-Horn Filter**: Membership $``R(o_i, o_j) \in \mathcal{H}_{\text{ORD}}``$ evaluates in $O(1)$ time. Tractable components route to path consistency; others route to difference logic SMT.
3. **Path Consistency**: Solves ORD-Horn networks via $``R(o_i, o_k) \leftarrow R(o_i, o_k) \cap (R(o_i, o_j) \circ R(o_j, o_k))``$. Contradictions ($R = \emptyset$) emit `E0400`.
4. **Difference Logic**: General networks lower to QF_RDL formula $``\Phi_{\text{temporal}}``$ over $``\{t_{i, s}, t_{i, e}\}``$, enforcing duration guards and selector clauses.
5. **Bounded SMT**: General networks (NP-1) solve under budget $``\kappa_{\text{temporal}}``$. Unsatisfiability emits `E0400`; step exhaustion emits `E0406`.

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** $\mathbf{NP}\text{-complete}$ / $\mathbf{P}$ (ORD-Horn Fragment $``\mathcal{H}_{\text{ORD}}``$)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(N^3)$ for ORD-Horn path consistency ($N = |\mathcal{O}|$); $O(M)$ for classification; $``O(\kappa_{\text{temporal}} \cdot N^2)``$ for QF_RDL SMT.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Algorithmic Bound NP-1. The ORD-Horn subclass $``\mathcal{H}_{\text{ORD}}``$ guarantees $O(N^3)$ decision without backtracking; general networks solve via SMT under cutoff $``\kappa_{\text{temporal}}``$. Work-stealing scales as $``O((N^3 + \kappa_{\text{temporal}} \cdot N^2) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Pure ORD-Horn Consistent Network Propagation
- **Given:** Model `Package_0` with 15 occurrences constrained by $``\mathcal{H}_{\text{ORD}}``$ relations.
- **When:** Path consistency propagation executes.
- **Then:** Computes fixed point in $O(N^3)$ operations.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Pure ORD-Horn Contradiction Rejection
- **Given:** Occurrences `Occurrence_Alpha`, `Occurrence_Beta`, and `Occurrence_Gamma` with cyclic `before` relations.
- **When:** Path consistency evaluates triangular compositions.
- **Then:** Derives empty relation $R = \emptyset$, binds $``\bot_{\text{net}}``$, emitting `E0400`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0400`.

### AC-03: General Temporal Network SMT Satisfiability
- **Given:** Network `Network_Beta` with non-ORD-Horn constraints ($``R \notin \mathcal{H}_{\text{ORD}}``$).
- **When:** Compiling to QF_RDL and solving via SMT.
- **Then:** Proves satisfiability within $``\kappa_{\text{temporal}}``$ steps.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-04: SMT Step Budget Exceeded & Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Network exceeding budget $``\kappa_{\text{temporal}} = 50``$ before `;`, followed by `Occurrence_Delta`.
- **When:** SMT solver hits step limit and recovers.
- **Then:** Emits `E0406`, recovers on `;`, parses `Occurrence_Delta`, emitting `E0199` at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0406`, `E0199`.
