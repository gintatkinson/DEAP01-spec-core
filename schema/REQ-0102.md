## 1. Normative Statement

The metrology engine SHALL compute and validate dimension vectors for multiplication ($A \cdot B$), division ($A / B$), and reciprocal ($1 / B$) via vector addition, subtraction, and negation in $``\mathbb{Q}^7``$.

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Multiplication**: $E = A \cdot B$ evaluates to dimension sum $``\mathbf{d}_{A \cdot B} = \mathbf{d}_A + \mathbf{d}_B``$ and scale product $``c_{A \cdot B} = c_A \cdot c_B``$. Identity $``\mathbf{0}``$ satisfies $``\mathbf{d} + \mathbf{0} = \mathbf{d}``$.
2. **Division & Reciprocal**: $E = A / B$ evaluates to dimension difference $``\mathbf{d}_{A / B} = \mathbf{d}_A - \mathbf{d}_B``$ and scale quotient $``c_{A / B} = c_A / c_B``$. Reciprocal $E = 1 / B$ evaluates to $``\mathbf{d}_{1 / B} = -\mathbf{d}_B``$ and $``c_{1 / B} = 1 / c_B``$. If $``\mathbf{d}_A \equiv \mathbf{d}_B``$, division yields identity $\mathbf{0}$.
3. **Affine Prohibition**: Operands MUST belong to ratio scales ($o = 0$). Offsets $o \ne 0$ emit diagnostic `E0300`.
4. **Zero Divisor & Bounds**: Compile-time constant zero divisor ($``v_B = 0``$) emits `E0300` with sentinel $``\bot_{\text{dim}}``$. Exponents $``d_i = p_i / q_i``$ require $``|p_i|, q_i \le 32767``$; overflow emits `E0300`.

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(1)$ per node; aggregate lowering is $O(N)$ for $``N = |\mathcal{E}_{\text{expr}}|``$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** 7D rational arithmetic terminates in $O(1)$ Euclidean $\gcd$ steps ($\le 30$ iterations), bypassing exponential frontiers (NP-3, NP-6). Work-stealing scales as $``O(N / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Multiplicative Exponent Addition & Scale Composition
- **Given:** Quantities `Quantity_Alpha` (dimension $``\mathbf{e}_M + \mathbf{e}_L - 2\mathbf{e}_T``$, scale $``c_1``$) and `Quantity_Beta` (dimension $``\mathbf{e}_L``$, scale $``c_2``$).
- **When:** Lowering multiplication `Expr_Alpha = Quantity_Alpha * Quantity_Beta`.
- **Then:** Dimension evaluates to $``\mathbf{e}_M + 2\mathbf{e}_L - 2\mathbf{e}_T``$ with scale $``c_1 \cdot c_2``$, offset $o = 0$, and empty diagnostic sink.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Division Exponent Subtraction & Dimensional Cancellation
- **Given:** Quantities `Factor_1` (dimension $``\mathbf{e}_L``$, scale $1000$) and `Factor_2` (dimension $``\mathbf{e}_L``$, scale $1$).
- **When:** Lowering division `Expr_Alpha = Factor_1 / Factor_2`.
- **Then:** Dimension evaluates to identity $\mathbf{0}$ with scale $1000$ and empty diagnostic sink.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Affine Unit Rejection
- **Given:** Expression `Expr_Alpha = param_x * param_y` where `param_x` has affine unit `Unit_Alpha` ($o = 273.15$).
- **When:** Lowering and metrology type checking execute.
- **Then:** Expression is rejected with diagnostic `E0300` and assigns sentinel $``\bot_{\text{dim}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300` (Affine unit in multiplicative expression).

### AC-04: Compile-Time Division by Zero & Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Invalid division `Expr_Fault = Factor_1 / 0.0` followed by `;` and valid `Expr_Beta = Factor_1 * Factor_2`.
- **When:** Delimiter error recovery executes.
- **Then:** Lowering emits `E0300`, binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{dim}}``$, recovers on `;`, parses `Expr_Beta`, and halts at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300`, `E0199`.
