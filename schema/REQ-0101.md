## 1. Normative Statement

The compiler SHALL validate dimensional homogeneity on additive ($A\pm B$), relational ($A\sim B$), and extremum ($\min,\max$) expressions in $\mathbb{Q}^7$.

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Additive Invariant**: $A\pm B$ requires $``\mathbf{d}_A\equiv\mathbf{d}_B``$; $E$ inherits $``\mathbf{d}_E=\mathbf{d}_A``$. Unary operators preserve dimensions ($``\mathbf{d}_{\pm A}=\mathbf{d}_A``$).
2. **Relational Typing**: Comparisons $A\sim B$ require $``\mathbf{d}_A\equiv\mathbf{d}_B``$, yielding Boolean type with dimensionless $\mathbf{0}\in\mathbb{Q}^7$.
3. **Commensurability**: When $``\mathbf{d}_A\equiv\mathbf{d}_B``$ with $``u_A\ne u_B``$, compiler scales $B$ by $``k=c_B/c_A\in\mathbb{Q}^+``$ in unit $``u_A``$. Mismatches emit `E0300`.
4. **Affine Offsets**: Summing interval quantities ($``o_A,o_B\ne 0``$) emits `E0300`. Difference ($``o_A=o_B``$) yields ratio scale ($``o_E=0``$). Offsets combine as $``o_E=o_A``$.
5. **Cascade Suppression**: Violations emit `E0300`, assign sentinel $``\bot_{\text{dim}}``$, and suppress parent diagnostics.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Dimensional equivalence, additive preservation, conversion synthesis, and diagnostic rules:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{d}_A\equiv\mathbf{d}_B &\iff \forall i \in \{1,\dots,7\},\; \mathbf{d}_A[i]=\mathbf{d}_B[i] \\
E=A\pm B &\implies (\mathbf{d}_A\equiv\mathbf{d}_B \implies \mathbf{d}_E=\mathbf{d}_A) \wedge (\mathbf{d}_A\not\equiv\mathbf{d}_B \implies \text{Diagnostic}(E,\text{E0300})) \\
E=A\sim B &\implies (\mathbf{d}_A\equiv\mathbf{d}_B \implies \mathbf{d}_E=\mathbf{0}) \wedge (\mathbf{d}_A\not\equiv\mathbf{d}_B \implies \text{Diagnostic}(E,\text{E0300})) \\
u_A\ne u_B &\implies \text{convert}(B,u_A)=(c_B/c_A)\cdot B \\
\Delta_E=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{NS}_{\text{DEAP}},&\text{FQN}(E)),\; \mathbf{d}_A=\bot_{\text{dim}} \implies \mathbf{d}_E=\bot_{\text{dim}}
\end{aligned}
```

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Additive Homogeneity and Dimension Inheritance
- **Given:** Operands `Term_1` and `Term_2` of dimension $(1,1,-2,0,0,0,0)\in\mathbb{Q}^7$.
- **When:** Lowering `Term_1 + Term_2` or `Term_1 - Term_2`.
- **Then:** Validation inherits operand dimension $(1,1,-2,0,0,0,0)$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Commensurable Unit Conversion Synthesis
- **Given:** Operands `Term_1` and `Term_2` of dimension $(0,1,0,0,0,0,0)$ in `Unit_Alpha` ($c=1$) and `Unit_Beta` ($c=1/1000$).
- **When:** Lowering `Term_1 + Term_2`.
- **Then:** Compiler synthesizes node scaling `Term_2` by $1/1000\in\mathbb{Q}^+$ in unit `Unit_Alpha`.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Incommensurable Dimensional Mismatch Rejection
- **Given:** Operands `Term_1` (Force) and `Term_2` (Energy) with mismatched dimensions.
- **When:** Lowering `Term_1 + Term_2` or `Term_1 < Term_2`.
- **Then:** Rejection emits `E0300`, isolating mismatched length coordinate, and assigns sentinel $``\bot_{\text{dim}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300`.

### AC-04: Affine Interval Addition Rejection and Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Sum `Term_1 + Term_2` of interval quantities ($``o_1=o_2=5463/20``$) before valid statement.
- **When:** Semantic validation and delimiter recovery execute.
- **Then:** Rejection emits `E0300`, recovers on delimiter, and halts at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300`, `E0199`.
