## 1. Normative Statement

The metrology engine SHALL validate parameters and signals against declared operational envelopes $``[v_{\min}, v_{\max}] \subset \overline{\mathbb{R}}``$.

Lowering MUST enforce:
1. **Closed Intervals**: Envelopes $``\mathcal{E}_x = [v_{\min}, v_{\max}]``$ require $``v_{\min} \le v_{\max}``$; inversions emit `E0300`. Singletons ($``v_{\min} = v_{\max}``$) are valid.
2. **Homogeneity**: Bounds and operating points require $``\mathbf{d} \equiv \mathbf{d}_{\mathcal{E}} \in \mathbb{Q}^7``$ (mismatches emit `E0300`); units scale via $c \in \mathbb{Q}^+$.
3. **Containment**: Values $``v \in \{v_{\text{const}}, v_{\text{nom}}, v_{\text{init}}, v_{\text{default}}\}``$ require $``v_{\min} \le v \le v_{\max}``$; violations emit `E0301`. Endpoints are valid.
4. **Monotonic Hierarchy**: Nested tiers require $``\mathcal{E}_{\text{normal}} \subseteq \mathcal{E}_{\text{extended}} \subseteq \mathcal{E}_{\text{safety}}``$ ($``v_{\min}^{\text{safe}} \le v_{\min}^{\text{norm}} \le v_{\max}^{\text{norm}} \le v_{\max}^{\text{safe}}``$); inversions emit `E0300`.
5. **Interconnects**: Connected ports require $``\mathcal{E}_1 \cap \mathcal{E}_2 \ne \emptyset``$; disjoint envelopes emit `E0301`. Signals synthesize assertions $``v_{\min} \le x(t) \le v_{\max}``$.

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial)
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(1)$ per interval bound and containment test; aggregate lowering is $O(N + M)$ for $``N = |\mathcal{V}_{\text{param}}|``$ envelopes and $``M = |\mathcal{E}_{\text{conn}}|``$ interconnects.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** Inequalities evaluate without search (NP-3 fragment). Work-stealing scales as $``O((N + M) / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$.

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Envelope Lowering & Nominal Containment
- **Given:** Parameter `Param_X : Real` with envelope `Envelope_Alpha` ($[10.0, 50.0]$) and $``v_{\text{nom}} = 25.0``$.
- **When:** Lowering validates bounds and containment.
- **Then:** Passes with $10.0 \le 25.0 \le 50.0$ and empty sink.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Unit Scaling & Monotonic Inclusion
- **Given:** Parameter `Param_Y` with tiers $``\mathcal{E}_{\text{normal}} = [20.0\text{ mm}, 80.0\text{ mm}]``$, $``\mathcal{E}_{\text{safety}} = [0.01\text{ m}, 0.10\text{ m}]``$.
- **When:** Lowering normalizes units ($c = 1/1000$) and checks monotonicity.
- **Then:** Validates $[0.02, 0.08] \subseteq [0.01, 0.10]$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Inverted Bounds & Dimensional Rejection
- **Given:** Envelope `Envelope_Beta` ($[100.0, 50.0]$) and parameter `Param_Z` with bounds $[L]^1$ vs $[T]^1$.
- **When:** Lowering checks bound ordering and dimensions.
- **Then:** Rejects declarations, emits `E0300`, binds $``\bot_{\text{env}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300` (InvalidOperationalEnvelopeBounds).

### AC-04: Containment Violation & Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Constant `Param_Const = 75.0` with envelope $[10.0, 50.0]$ before `;`, and disjoint ports $[10.0, 30.0]$, $[50.0, 80.0]$.
- **When:** Containment and interconnect intersection validate.
- **Then:** Emits `E0301`, recovers on `;`, emits `E0199` at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0301`, `E0199`.
