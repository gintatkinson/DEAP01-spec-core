## 8. Operational Environments & MIL-STD-810H Environmental Envelopes

### 8.1 Climatic & Thermal Operating Envelopes (-40°C to +55°C)
In accordance with MIL-STD-810H, the system is engineered to maintain full operational integrity across extreme worldwide climatic categories:
- **Operational Ambient Temperature Range:** -40°C to +55°C (continuous operational exposure per MIL-STD-810H Method 501.7 High Temperature Procedure II and Method 502.7 Low Temperature Procedure II).
- **Storage / Transit Temperature Range:** -50°C to +70°C (unpowered storage in ruggedized field containers per MIL-STD-810H Method 501.7 / 502.7 Procedure I).
- **Thermal Shock Resilience:** Capable of transitioning between 20°C ambient storage and -40°C Arctic launch conditions at thermal gradient up to 10°C / minute without avionics condensation or optical fogging (Method 503.7).

### 8.2 Ingress Protection (IP67) & Precipitation Limits
- **Enclosure Ingress Rating:** Full **IP67** ingress protection rating per IEC 60529 (100% dust-tight sealing; submersible in water up to 1.0 m depth for 30 minutes).
- **Rain & Blowing Rain Resistance:** Verified in accordance with MIL-STD-810H Method 506.6 Procedure I:
  - Precipitation rate: 100.0 mm/hr continuous rainfall.
  - Accompanying wind velocity: 18.0 m/s blowing spray.
  - Internal moisture barrier: Dual Viton O-ring seals across all structural fuselage seams and payload quick-disconnect bulkheads.

### 8.3 Wind & Gust Operating Thresholds
The aerodynamic control surfaces and propulsion system are dimensioned to maintain precise path tracking under severe atmospheric turbulence:
- **Maximum Operational Steady Wind Speed:** 15.0 m/s (29.1 knots / 54.0 km/h).
- **Maximum Operational Wind Gust Speed:** 20.0 m/s (38.8 knots / 72.0 km/h) with 3.0-second gust duration.
- **Crosswind Launch & Recovery Limit:** 10.0 m/s (19.4 knots) perpendicular to runway / recovery heading.

### 8.4 Sand, Dust & Particulate Exposure (MIL-STD-810H Method 510.7)
- **Blowing Dust Concentration:** 10.6 g/m³ particulate density at 15.0 m/s air velocity (Procedure I).
- **Blowing Sand Concentration:** 2.2 g/m³ quartz sand (particle size 150 µm to 850 µm) at 18.0 m/s (Procedure II).
- **Protective Countermeasures:** Sealed brushless motor bearings (ceramic hybrid, dual contact rubber seals); hydrophobic and oleophobic PTFE membrane vents over acoustic and barometric sensor ports.

### 8.5 Salt Fog & Marine Corrosion Resistance (MIL-STD-810H Method 509.7)
- **Exposure Profile:** 5% sodium chloride (NaCl) saline atomization at 35°C for 48 hours continuous exposure, followed by 48 hours drying cycle (2 complete 96-hour cycles).
- **Material Selection:** Aerospace-grade 7075-T6 aluminum components treated with MIL-A-8625 Type III hardcoat anodization; 316L stainless steel structural fasteners; conformal coating (MIL-I-46058C Class E) across all printed circuit boards.

### 8.6 Solar Radiation & Thermal Loading (MIL-STD-810H Method 505.7)
- **Peak Solar Irradiance:** 1120.0 W/m² (A1 Worldwide Hot-Dry climatic category, Procedure I).
- **Thermal Solar Loading:** Internal avionics compartment temperature rise limited to < 12°C above ambient via active heat pipes and radiative thermal barrier topcoat.

### 8.7 Icing Conditions & Cold Weather Operation (MIL-STD-810H Method 521.4)
- **Atmospheric Icing Envelope:** Capable of operating in light freezing rain and supercooled liquid water droplet clouds with liquid water content (LWC) up to 0.5 g/m³ at temperatures down to -15°C.
- **Anti-Icing Provisions:** Thermally heated pitot-static tube (40 W heating element, RTCA DO-160G Section 11); superhydrophobic nano-coatings on leading wing edges and propeller blade surfaces to delay ice accretion.

### 8.8 Electromagnetic Interference (EMI) & RF Environments
- **Radiated Susceptibility:** Withstands high-intensity radiated fields (HIRF) up to 200.0 V/m across 10 kHz to 40 GHz frequency spectrum per MIL-STD-461G Method RS103.
- **Conducted Susceptibility:** Compliant with MIL-STD-461G CS114 / CS115 / CS116 power lead transient injection limits.
- **GNSS-Denied Operational Resilience:** Maintains tactical navigation for up to 15 minutes of continuous wideband GNSS jamming via optical flow odometry and tactical-grade MEMS IMU dead reckoning (drift rate < 1.5% distance traveled).

### 8.9 Physical Spatial Constraints & Deployment Envelopes
- **Launch & Recovery Footprint:** Minimum cleared level ground area of 5.0 m x 5.0 m free from vertical obstacles exceeding 1.0 m height.
- **Transit & Packaged Dimensions:** Entire air vehicle, GCS, tracking antenna mast, and GSE battery charging hub packed into two standard MIL-STD-810H drop-certified ruggedized Pelican cases (maximum dimensions: 1100 mm x 600 mm x 450 mm; individual case mass < 32.0 kg for two-person transport).
